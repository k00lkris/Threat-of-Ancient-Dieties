A pair of elegant gloves made from fine, supple leather dyed in deep shades of purple and violet. Intricate patterns resembling swirling winds and ancient runes are embossed along the length of the gloves, subtly glowing with an otherworldly light. Each fingertip is adorned with a small, polished amethyst gemstone that glimmers in various shades of purple, enhancing the wearer's dexterity and precision. The wristbands are reinforced with silver threading, creating a seamless blend of form and function. These gloves emanate a faint, magical aura, indicating their powerful enchantments and connection to celestial craftsmanship.

---

#### Tier 1 (Level 1+)
- **Doubled Efficiency:** While wearing the gloves, you count as two characters for the purpose of crafting, cutting your crafting time in half.

#### Tier 2 (Level 5+)
- **Superior Efficiency:** While wearing the gloves, you count as three characters for the purpose of crafting, further reducing your crafting time by one-third.
- **Enhanced Precision:** You gain a +2 bonus to all skill checks related to crafting (e.g., Smith's tools, Alchemist's supplies, Weaver's tools, etc.).
- **Magical Insight:** Once per day, you can cast the "Identify" spell without using a spell slot or material components.

#### Tier 3 (Level 10+)
- **Master Crafter:** While wearing the gloves, you count as four characters for the purpose of crafting, reducing your crafting time by half again.
- **Expert Artisan:** The bonus to all skill checks related to crafting increases to +4.
- **Elemental Infusion:** You can infuse one crafted item per day with elemental energy, granting it an additional 1d6 damage of your choice of fire, cold, lightning, or acid damage. This infusion lasts for 24 hours.

#### Tier 4 (Level 15+)
- **Legendary Craftsman:** While wearing the gloves, you count as five characters for the purpose of crafting, drastically reducing your crafting time.
- **Supreme Artisan:** The bonus to all skill checks related to crafting increases to +6.
- **Amethyst Inspiration:** Once per long rest, you can grant a crafted item a temporary magical effect, as if it were a +1 magical item (or a +2 if the item is already magical), for 24 hours.
- **Celestial Craftsmanship:** You can cast the "Fabricate" spell once per day without using a spell slot.
