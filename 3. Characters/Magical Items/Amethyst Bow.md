A stunning longbow crafted from an otherworldly, polished wood that shimmers with a faint purple hue. The limbs of the bow are intricately carved with swirling patterns that seem to glow with a soft, inner light. Embedded within the curves and contours of the bow are small amethyst gemstones, which sparkle and catch the light in mesmerizing ways. The grip is wrapped in fine, deep violet leather, providing a comfortable and secure hold. When drawn, the bowstring emits a gentle, melodic hum, resonating with the bow's magical energy. The overall design of the bow exudes both elegance and power, making it a truly magnificent weapon.

---

#### Tier 1 (Level 1+)
- **+1 Bonus:** The bow grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

#### Tier 2 (Level 5+)
- **+2 Bonus:** The bow's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +2.
- **Amethyst Arrows:** When an arrow is fired from the bow, it transforms into a bolt of amethyst energy, dealing an additional 1d6 radiant damage on a hit.
- **Guiding Shot:** Once per short rest, you can declare a guiding shot before making an attack. If the attack hits, the target is illuminated by a faint, purple glow, granting advantage to the next attack roll made against it before the end of your next turn.

#### Tier 3 (Level 10+)
- **+3 Bonus:** The bow's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +3.
- **Amethyst Arrows:** The additional radiant damage increases to 2d6.
- **Splitting Shot:** Once per long rest, you can use your action to fire a single arrow that splits into two, targeting two creatures within 30 feet of each other. Make a separate attack roll for each target.
- **Arcane Sight:** While holding the bow, you can see invisible creatures and objects within 30 feet of you, as if they were visible.

#### Tier 4 (Level 15+)
- **+4 Bonus:** The bow's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +4.
- **Amethyst Arrows:** The additional radiant damage increases to 3d6.
- **Amethyst Burst:** Once per long rest, you can fire an arrow that explodes in a 20-foot radius on impact. Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier), taking 6d6 radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
- **Celestial Connection:** While attuned to the bow, you can cast the "Divination" spell once per long rest without expending a spell slot. This spell allows you to seek guidance from celestial beings.
