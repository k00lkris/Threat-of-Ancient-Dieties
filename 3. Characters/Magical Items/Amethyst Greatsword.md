A majestic greatsword forged from a unique, lustrous metal that shimmers with a deep purple hue. The blade is broad and slightly curved, with intricate patterns resembling celestial constellations etched into its surface, glowing softly with a radiant light. Embedded along the fuller of the blade are small amethyst gemstones, each perfectly cut to reflect and amplify the light. The crossguard is made of polished silver, intricately designed with swirling motifs and set with larger amethyst gems at its ends. The grip is wrapped in rich, dark violet leather, providing a firm and comfortable hold, while the pommel features a large, brilliantly cut amethyst stone that pulses with arcane energy. The overall design of the greatsword exudes both elegance and immense power, making it a formidable and awe-inspiring weapon.

### Amethyst Greatsword

#### Tier 1 (Level 1+)
- **+1 Bonus:** The greatsword grants a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls.

#### Tier 2 (Level 5+)
- **+2 Bonus:** The greatsword's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +2.
- **Amethyst Edge:** The greatsword deals an additional 1d6 radiant damage on a hit.
- **Shining Strike:** Once per short rest, you can declare a shining strike before making an attack. If the attack hits, the target is blinded until the end of your next turn.

#### Tier 3 (Level 10+)
- **+3 Bonus:** The greatsword's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +3.
- **Amethyst Edge:** The additional radiant damage increases to 2d6.
- **Radiant Cleave:** Once per long rest, you can use your action to make a sweeping attack. Each creature within 10 feet of you must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier). On a failed save, a creature takes 4d6 radiant damage, or half as much on a successful save.
- **Celestial Glow:** The greatsword emits bright light in a 20-foot radius and dim light for an additional 20 feet. You can command the light to extinguish or reignite as a bonus action.

#### Tier 4 (Level 15+)
- **+4 Bonus:** The greatsword's bonus to attack and damage rolls increases to +4.
- **Amethyst Edge:** The additional radiant damage increases to 3d6.
- **Amethyst Burst:** Once per long rest, you can unleash the full power of the greatsword, causing it to explode with radiant energy. Each creature within 20 feet must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier). On a failed save, a creature takes 6d6 radiant damage and is knocked prone, or half as much damage and not knocked prone on a successful save.
- **Celestial Command:** While attuned to the greatsword, you can cast the "Guardian of Faith" spell once per long rest without expending a spell slot.

### Summary of Enhancements by Tier

**Tier 1 (Level 1+)**
- +1 to attack and damage rolls

**Tier 2 (Level 5+)**
- +2 to attack and damage rolls
- Amethyst Edge: +1d6 radiant damage
- Shining Strike: Blind a target until the end of your next turn

**Tier 3 (Level 10+)**
- +3 to attack and damage rolls
- Amethyst Edge: +2d6 radiant damage
- Radiant Cleave: Sweeping attack dealing 4d6 radiant damage in a 10-foot radius

- +4 to attack and damage rolls
- Amethyst Edge: +3d6 radiant damage
