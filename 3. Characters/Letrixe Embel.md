### Letrexie Embel's Story: Emon

**Geography:**
- **Location:** Emon is the capital of Tal'Dorei, a grand city known for its impressive architecture, political intrigue, and vibrant cultural scene.
- **Description:** The city is a blend of opulent estates, bustling markets, and political institutions. It is a place where power and wealth are concentrated, but also where shadows and secrets thrive.

**Community and Culture:**
- **Nobility and Politics:** Emon is home to the ruling Council of Tal'Dorei, including influential noble families. The city is a hotbed of political maneuvering and intrigue.
- **Cultural Hub:** Emon is also a center for the arts, with theaters, galleries, and public performances providing a rich cultural backdrop.

**Family and Early Life:**
- **Heritage:** Born Miribel Divoux, Letrexie was the youngest child and only daughter of a powerful noble family. Her parents were members of the ruling Council, and she grew up in luxury.
- **Cult Influence:** Bored and overshadowed by her older brothers, Miribel and her best friend Tillie fell under the influence of a dark cult that sought to resurrect the Annunaki.

**The Incident:**
- **Family Tragedy:** Urged by Tillie and the cult, Miribel murdered her family in a bid to prove her loyalty. This act shattered her mind, creating the dual personalities of Letrexie and Miribel.
- **New Path:** As Letrexie, she fled Emon and took up residence in Westruun, dedicating herself to the service of The Everlight and helping the unfortunate.
