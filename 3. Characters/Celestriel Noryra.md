### Celestriel's Home: The Summit Peaks

For Celestriel's home, let's place it in the Summit Peaks, a mountain range located in Tal'Dorei that isn't the Cliffkeep Mountains. The Summit Peaks provide a majestic and fitting home for an Air Ashari tribe, given their high altitudes and strong winds.

### Description of the Summit Peaks

**Geography:**
- The Summit Peaks are a towering range of mountains characterized by their sharp, jagged peaks and deep, narrow valleys. The highest peaks are often snow-capped, while the lower slopes are covered in dense forests.
- The air is thin and crisp, with strong winds constantly howling through the peaks. The skies are often clear, providing breathtaking views of the surrounding landscapes.

**The Air Ashari Village: Zephrah**
- The Air Ashari village of Zephrah is nestled high in the Summit Peaks, situated on a series of interconnected plateaus and cliffside terraces. The village is accessible primarily by narrow, winding paths and hidden trails known only to the Ashari.
- The buildings are constructed with natural materials, blending seamlessly with the surrounding environment. Many structures are built on stilts or into the cliffside, allowing the wind to flow freely around them.
- The village is adorned with wind chimes, banners, and other wind-activated decorations that create a constant symphony of natural music.

**Community and Culture:**
- The Air Ashari of Zephrah are deeply connected to the element of air, focusing on maintaining the balance of the skies and weather. They are skilled in controlling the winds, predicting weather patterns, and harnessing the power of storms.
- The community values harmony, freedom, and the pursuit of knowledge. They are often seen meditating on high peaks or practicing their airbending skills in the open air.
- The village is led by a headmaster or headmistress, who oversees the spiritual and practical training of the tribe members.

### Celestriel's Background in Zephrah

**Family and Early Life:**
- Celestriel was born to a respected family within the Air Ashari tribe. Her parents were both accomplished airbenders, known for their wisdom and prowess in controlling the winds.
- From a young age, Celestriel showed a natural affinity for air magic. Her parents and tribe elders recognized her potential and began her training early, teaching her the ways of the Air Ashari.

**Training and Duties:**
- Celestriel's training involved not only mastering the control of air but also understanding the balance of nature and the importance of maintaining harmony in the world.
- She participated in various rituals and ceremonies, such as the Wind Dance, a traditional performance that honors the spirits of the air and asks for their blessings.
- As part of her duties, she often accompanied her parents and other tribe members on journeys to observe and protect the natural world, deepening her connection to the land and its elements.

**The Aramente:**
- Upon reaching adulthood, Celestriel embarked on her Aramente, a rite of passage that involves traveling the world to gain wisdom and experience.
- Her journey took her far from Zephrah, leading her to join the adventuring party with Hurk, Heijla, Letrexie, and Notos. Despite her memory loss, the core principles and skills of her training remain deeply ingrained in her.

### Connections to the Campaign

**Artifact Retrieval:**
- The Celestial Tear required for the resurrection ritual might be linked to Zephrah, hidden in a sacred temple or high peak known only to the Air Ashari. Celestriel's knowledge and connections to her tribe could be crucial in locating and obtaining this artifact.
- Her tribe's wisdom and resources could provide valuable support in the party's quest, offering guidance, healing, and shelter.

**Personal Growth:**
- As Celestriel uncovers more about her past and reconnects with her tribe, she may face challenges and opportunities for personal growth. This could involve reconciling her past actions as a mercenary with the values of the Air Ashari.
- Her journey could also include learning advanced airbending techniques or discovering hidden powers within herself, further enhancing her role in the party.

**Conflict and Resolution:**
- Celestriel's return to Zephrah might not be without conflict. She may have to prove herself to skeptical tribe members or deal with threats to her home from external forces.
- Resolving these conflicts and reaffirming her place within the tribe can be a significant part of her character arc, solidifying her identity and purpose.

### Conclusion

By establishing Celestriel's home in Zephrah within the Summit Peaks, we create a rich and detailed background that ties her closely to the Air Ashari tribe and the element of air.
