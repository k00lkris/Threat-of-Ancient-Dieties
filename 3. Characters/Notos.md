### Notos' Story: Westruun

**Geography:**
- **Location:** Westruun is a major trade city located in the heart of Tal'Dorei, serving as a crossroads for merchants and travelers.
- **Description:** The city is a bustling

trade hub with a mix of urban development and surrounding farmland. It's known for its markets, artisans, and strategic importance.

**Community and Culture:**
- **Trade and Commerce:** Westruun thrives on trade, with caravans and merchants constantly coming and going. The city's markets are filled with goods from all over Exandria.
- **Thieves and Rogues:** Alongside legitimate trade, Westruun has a thriving underworld. The Clasp, a notorious thieves' guild, operates here, and crime is a significant aspect of city life.

**Family and Early Life:**
- **Heritage:** Notos grew up in poverty, abandoned by his mother at an early age. He survived by stealing food and begging for money on the streets.
- **Joining The Clasp:** His talent for thievery caught the eye of The Clasp, and he was initiated into the guild. He quickly rose through the ranks, becoming a skilled thief and assassin.

**The Incident:**
- **Turning Point:** Notos eventually grew disillusioned with the life of crime, particularly after meeting Letrexie, who showed him kindness and compassion. She helped him see a different path.
- **New Path:** Leaving The Clasp was dangerous, but Notos managed to escape and join Letrexie on her adventures. Despite his memory loss, his skills and the lessons he learned on the streets remain with him.
