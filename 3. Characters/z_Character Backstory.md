### Integrating "Dark Matter" Plot Elements into the Campaign

To draw more from the plot of "Dark Matter" and integrate it into your campaign, we can build on the idea that the party were once notorious mercenaries known for their ruthlessness and effectiveness. Their memory wipe has given them a fresh start, but their past actions and reputation are slowly catching up with them. Here's how we can weave this narrative into the characters' stories and the campaign plot:

### The Mercenary Past

**Group Identity:**
- **Mercenary Group Name:** The Reapers
- **Reputation:** Feared and respected for their ability to complete any job, no matter how dark or illegal. They were known for their ruthless efficiency and willingness to take on the most dangerous and morally ambiguous contracts.

**Backstory:**
- **Memory Wipe Incident:** The party’s memory wipe occurred after a particularly harrowing mission that went wrong, involving powerful magic or a mysterious artifact that erased their memories. They woke up in Kymal with no recollection of their past and decided to forge a new path as more righteous adventurers.

### Character-Specific Backstories

#### Hurk Dimbleback
- **Past Identity:** Hurk was a feared enforcer, known for his brutal tactics and no-nonsense approach to piracy and mercenary work.
- **Reputation:** Known as "The Crimson Reaver," Hurk's name struck fear into the hearts of his enemies.
- **Amnesia:** The memory wipe gave Hurk a chance to leave his violent past behind, but old rivals and former crewmates may seek him out for vengeance or to reclaim their enforcer.

#### Celestriel Noryra
- **Past Identity:** Celestriel was a skilled tracker and assassin for hire, using her druidic abilities to hunt down targets with precision.
- **Reputation:** Known as "The Shadow of the Wilds," she was feared for her ability to strike from the shadows and leave no trace.
- **Amnesia:** Her new path as a protector of nature contrasts sharply with her past, but former employers or hunted targets may recognize her and seek retribution.

#### Heijla Valkyr
- **Past Identity:** Heijla was a powerful sorceress involved in arcane heists and magical espionage, using her abilities to infiltrate and steal valuable magical artifacts.
- **Reputation:** Known as "The Arcane Phantom," she was notorious for her cunning and magical prowess.
- **Amnesia:** Without memories of her past heists, Heijla seeks knowledge for its own sake now, but her past crimes may draw the attention of arcane authorities or former associates.

#### Letrexie Embel (Miribel Divoux)
- **Past Identity:** Miribel was a cultist and dark cleric who carried out sinister rituals and assassinations for her cult, using her divine powers for nefarious purposes.
- **Reputation:** Known as "The Dark Acolyte," she was a key figure in her cult's operations.
- **Amnesia:** Letrexie, unaware of her past deeds, strives to heal and protect. However, cult members or victims of Miribel's actions may come seeking her out.

**Notos**
- **Past Identity:** Notos was a master thief and assassin for The Clasp, involved in high-stakes heists and targeted killings.
- **Reputation:** Known as "The Ghostblade," his skills in stealth and combat were unmatched.
- **Amnesia:** Now trying to leave his past behind, Notos still feels the pull of his old life. Former guild members or betrayed targets may track him down.

### Campaign Integration

**Clues to the Past:**
- **Old Contacts:** The party may encounter NPCs who recognize them, providing cryptic hints about their past deeds and identities.
- **Flashbacks:** Occasional flashbacks triggered by specific locations, items, or events can reveal snippets of their previous lives.
- **Artifacts and Notes:** They may find artifacts, letters, or journals from their past selves, detailing their mercenary activities and providing context to their previous lives.

**Moral Dilemmas:**
- **Past Actions:** The party may face the consequences of their past actions, such as towns devastated by their previous missions or individuals seeking revenge.
- **Redemption Opportunities:** They will have chances to atone for their past misdeeds, helping those they previously harmed and making amends.

**Major Plot Points:**
- **Hargraves' Connection:** Lord Hargraves might have been a former employer or ally, aware of their skills and seeking to manipulate them or eliminate them as they pose a threat to his plans.
- **Resurrection Ritual:** As they gather the artifacts for the ritual, they uncover more about their past connections to these items and the entities guarding them.
- **Reputation Revelation:** The ultimate reveal of their past mercenary identities can be a climactic moment, forcing them to choose between embracing their new path or reverting to their old ways.

### Artifacts for the Resurrection Ritual

1. **Heart of a Titan:**
   - **Connection:** Linked to Hurk's past as a pirate who encountered titans or their remnants.

2. **Celestial Tear:**
   - **Connection:** Tied to Letrexie's divine magic and her quest for redemption.

3. **Eldritch Crystal:**
   - **Connection:** Related to Heijla's past involvement in arcane heists and magical theft.

4. **Ancient Relic of the Annunaki:**
   - **Connection:** Connected to Notos' past heists and thefts from ancient ruins.

5. **Blood of a Dragon:**
   - **Connection:** Linked to Celestriel's past as a tracker and assassin, possibly involving dragon hunts.

6. **Soul Gem:**
   - **Connection:** A collective effort requiring all their skills, tied to their mercenary group's history of dark rituals.

### Conclusion

By integrating these elements, you can create a compelling narrative that combines the mystery and self-discovery of "Dark Matter" with the rich lore of your campaign. The players will be deeply invested in uncovering their pasts, facing the consequences of their former lives, and ultimately deciding their future paths. This approach adds depth and complexity to the characters and the overarching story, making for an engaging and memorable campaign.
