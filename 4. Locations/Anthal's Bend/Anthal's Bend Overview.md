Anthal's Bend is a town between Kymal and Westruun. It is popular among tourists due to its close proximity to the river.

## Events
### Love Triangle
As the party approaches Anthal's Bend, they encounter 2 men fighting over a woman.

##### Men's Names
- Donall Pylus
- Morgan Titantamer

##### Woman's Name
- Zenthi Aragnaed

She wanted Morgan to fall in love with her, so she bought a "love" potion. She spiked his drink with it and he instantly was attracted to her. Leaving the cup unattended, her cousin, Donall, came in and drank the remainder of the potion, also falling in love with her.

Morgan tried to flee Anthal's Bend with her and was caught by Donall. She is trying to stop them from fighting over her. A Lesser Restoration spell willl cure both men. If cured, they willl be furious with her and refuse to take her back to the village with them.

### A Cruel Joke
Towards the end of the day, on the third day of travel past Anthal's Bend, the party comes across a woman wearing a black cloak, with the hood up. She looks like she's lost. If the party asks her what's wrong, she'll ask them where the nearest castle is (Westruun). She tells them that she is the wife of the noble there and she was kidnapped by Vampires. She will ask the party to help her rescue her daughter from the vampires. If pressed, she does not know where they are, where she is, or what is exactly going on.

She is a former cultist. As a cruel joke, the cultists wiped her memory, told her this story and left her to die. A greater restoration spell is required to restore her memories. She has commoner stats, and is no good in battle.
