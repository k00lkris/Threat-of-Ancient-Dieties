> As you step into The Frosty Mug, a wave of warmth and conviviality washes over you. The tavern is bustling with patrons, the air filled with the sound of hearty laughter and clinking mugs. The interior is cozy, with wooden beams and stone walls adorned with old hunting trophies and vintage ale posters. A large fireplace crackles in one corner, casting a welcoming glow over the room. The scent of roasted meats and freshly baked bread wafts from the kitchen, mingling with the earthy aroma of ale. Behind the polished bar stands Garvin Frost, a burly man with a thick beard and a jovial demeanor, skillfully pouring drinks and engaging in cheerful banter with the customers.

### Brief Description of Garvin Frost

Garvin Frost is a stout, friendly man with a hearty laugh and a welcoming presence. His thick beard and ruddy cheeks give him a Santa Claus-like appearance, which is only enhanced by his jovial nature. Garvin has been running The Frosty Mug for years and takes great pride in his establishment, ensuring that every patron feels at home. His love for good food, fine ale, and lively conversation is evident in every aspect of his tavern. Despite his cheerful exterior, Garvin is also a shrewd businessman and a keen judge of character, always keeping an eye out for troublemakers while ensuring his regulars are well taken care of.

Garvin Frost ensures that The Frosty Mug is a welcoming place for all, providing not only food and drink but also a sense of community and cheer.
