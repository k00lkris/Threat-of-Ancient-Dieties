> As you step into The Golden Goose, you are greeted by an atmosphere of refined elegance and warmth. The interior boasts rich mahogany furniture, intricately carved wooden beams, and plush, deep-red carpets that lend a sense of luxury. Chandeliers with golden accents cast a soft, welcoming glow, illuminating the polished brass fixtures and framed artwork adorning the walls. The scent of roasting meats and freshly baked pastries wafts from the kitchen, mingling with the aroma of high-quality spirits. Behind the gleaming bar, Minerva Goldwing, a striking woman with an air of sophistication, oversees her establishment with a keen eye and a welcoming smile.

### Brief Description of Minerva Goldwing

Minerva Goldwing is an elegant and composed woman in her late forties, with a regal bearing and a keen intellect. Her silver hair is always impeccably styled, and her attire exudes both sophistication and practicality. Minerva's sharp blue eyes miss nothing, and she carries herself with a blend of warmth and authority. She has a reputation for running The Golden Goose with both strict standards and genuine care for her patrons. Minerva has a background in the noble courts, which she left to start her own business, bringing a touch of that high-society polish to her tavern and inn. Despite her refined appearance, she is approachable and always willing to lend an ear to her guests' stories and troubles.

Minerva Goldwing ensures that The Golden Goose maintains a standard of excellence, providing not only fine food and drink but also a touch of luxury and comfort for all its patrons.
