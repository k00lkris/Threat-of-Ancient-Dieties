### Brief Description of Horace Tinkerton

Horace Tinkerton is a gnome with an insatiable passion for all things mechanical. Standing just under three feet tall, he has wild, unkempt hair that seems to defy gravity and a pair of large, round spectacles perched on his nose. His hands are perpetually stained with oil and metal shavings, a testament to his tireless work in crafting and repairing his beloved mechanical creations. Horace is known for his cheerful demeanor, infectious enthusiasm, and endless patience, always eager to explain the intricate workings of his gadgets to anyone who shows interest. His shop, The Clockwork Wonders, is a reflection of his boundless creativity and expertise.

Horace Tinkerton ensures that every item in his shop is a marvel of craftsmanship and ingenuity, delighting customers of all ages with his wondrous mechanical creations.
