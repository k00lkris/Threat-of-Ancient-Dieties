> As you enter The Howling Wolf, the scent of leather and freshly oiled weapons fills the air, accompanied by the sound of metal clinking against metal. #### Owner's Description:
Hunter Greywind, the proprietor of The Howling Wolf, is a seasoned hunter with a weathered face and keen eyes that have seen many a hunt. With a deep respect for the wild and a passion for the hunt, Hunter is dedicated to providing his customers with the finest hunting gear and supplies available. He takes pride in his craft and is always eager to share his knowledge and expertise with fellow hunters.
