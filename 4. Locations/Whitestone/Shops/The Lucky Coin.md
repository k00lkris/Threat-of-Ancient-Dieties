#### Owner's Description:
Felix Goodfellow, the proprietor of The Lucky Coin, is a jovial and eccentric figure with a penchant for the peculiar. He has a twinkle in his eye and a mischievous grin, and he delights in regaling customers with tales of adventure and mystery. Felix is known for his vast knowledge of obscure relics and his uncanny ability to procure rare and elusive items from across the land.
