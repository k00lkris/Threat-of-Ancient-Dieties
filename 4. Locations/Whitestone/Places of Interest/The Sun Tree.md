**The Sun Tree** is a sacred tree in the town square of [Whitestone](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Whitestone "Whitestone") that was created by [Pelor](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Pelor "Pelor") at the intersection of powerful [ley](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Ley "Ley") lines. Although it was almost killed by the unholy desecration of the [Briarwoods](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Briarwoods "Briarwoods"), the Sun Tree was recovering by the time [Vox Machina](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Vox_Machina "Vox Machina") returned to Whitestone in "[Duskmeadow](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Duskmeadow_(episode) "Duskmeadow (episode)")" (1x57). After it recovered, the Sun Tree grew leaves and small white flowers at its crown.

[Keyleth](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Keyleth "Keyleth") sometimes uses druidic spells to converse with the Sun Tree. As an NPC, the Sun Tree is played by [Matthew Mercer](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Matthew_Mercer "Matthew Mercer").

### **Appearance**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcriticalrole.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSun_Tree%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D2&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

[![Keyleth-and-the-Sun-Tree-by-Kerri-Aitken](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/criticalrole/images/1/11/Keyleth-and-the-Sun-Tree-by-Kerri-Aitken.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/350?cb=20170220070231)

Fan art of [Keyleth](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Keyleth "Keyleth") and the Sun Tree, by [Kerri Aitken](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Art_by_Kerri_Aitken "Category:Art by Kerri Aitken").[[art 2]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-4)

The Sun Tree sits in the center of [Whitestone](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Whitestone "Whitestone"), in the town square. When Vox Machina first arrived in Whitestone, it was a large, leafless tree, about forty feet tall. The branches twisted out and hung loosely at the ends. Everything about the tree looked "droopy and desolate".[[3]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-5)

[Vox Machina](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Vox_Machina "Vox Machina") was unable to identify the type of tree, though they were able to tell that it was created and transported through a type of divine magic.

After the Briarwoods were defeated, the Sun Tree began to recover and leaf out. By the time of "[One Year Later...](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/One_Year_Later... "One Year Later...")" (1x95), it had become a lush, beautiful tree with little white flowers at its crown that bloomed year-round.

When questioned via the spell _Speak with Plants_, the Sun Tree is largely unconcerned with the world beyond its immediate vicinity. The Sun Tree speaks in a slow, relaxed manner, which the cast compared to a hackey-sack-playing, marijuana-smoking, Birkenstock-wearing UC Berkeley graduate.[[4]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-6)

## **Biography**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcriticalrole.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSun_Tree%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D4&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

### **Background**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcriticalrole.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSun_Tree%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D5&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

[![Pre-Whitestone Sun Tree by Kent Davis](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/criticalrole/images/4/42/Pre-Whitestone_Sun_Tree_by_Kent_Davis.jpg/revision/latest/scale-to-width-down/350?cb=20200729230400)

](https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/criticalrole/images/4/42/Pre-Whitestone_Sun_Tree_by_Kent_Davis.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20200729230400)

Fan art of the Sun Tree before Whitestone's construction, by [Kent Davis](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Art_by_Kent_Davis "Category:Art by Kent Davis").[[art 3]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-7)

The Sun Tree was planted during the [Calamity](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Calamity "Calamity") by [Pelor, the Dawnfather](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Pelor "Pelor"), at the location where [Tharizdun](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Tharizdun "Tharizdun") grievously wounded the goddess [Ioun](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Ioun "Ioun").[[5]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-Tal-Dorei_Campaign_Setting-51-----8) The Sun Tree sits atop a hill covering a ruined [Ziggurat](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Ziggurat "Ziggurat") dedicated to Ioun, and is planted there as a symbol of Pelor's protection.

Around 600 PD, an expedition sailing from [Port Damali](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Port_Damali "Port Damali") wrecked on the cliffs of the [Alabaster Sierras](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Alabaster_Sierras "Alabaster Sierras"). The expedition, led by the [De Rolo family](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/De_Rolo_family "De Rolo family"), made their way towards the valley in the center of the mountains, where they discovered the Sun Tree. Viewing the blessed tree as divine providence, they settled there, building the city of [Whitestone](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Whitestone "Whitestone").[[6]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-Tal-Dorei_Campaign_Setting-32-----9)

### **"[The Sun Tree](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/The_Sun_Tree_(episode) "The Sun Tree (episode)")" (1x28)**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcriticalrole.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSun_Tree%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

When [Vox Machina](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Vox_Machina "Vox Machina") first saw the Sun Tree, it was in poor health.[[7]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-10) Eight bodies had been hung from its branches, and the group realized that each body was dressed and colored to look like one of them, as a message from the Briarwoods.[[8]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-11) One of these bodies was that of [Laudna](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Laudna "Laudna").[[9]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-12)

When [Keyleth](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Keyleth "Keyleth") tunneled to the roots of the Sun Tree and attempted to communicate with the tree using _Speak with Plants_, she got no response as the Sun Tree was effectively dead at that point.[[10]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-13) The druid then began casting _Plant Growth_ over the next eight hours in an attempt to revive the Sun Tree, though she did not succeed.[[11]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-14)

After the Briarwoods were killed, [Keyleth](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Keyleth "Keyleth") cast _Commune with Nature_ to speak to the tree. She felt that a small spark of life had returned to the tree. She then asked the tree about influence from other planes of existence and found there was a presence nearby from the Plane of Shadow. For her final question, Keyleth asked the tree how it was feeling. She felt that its life force was now free and said "Good morning" to it. The tree responded "The sun just set. It's not morning."[[13]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-16)

## **Relationships**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcriticalrole.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSun_Tree%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D17&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

### **[Keyleth](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Keyleth "Keyleth")**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fcriticalrole.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FSun_Tree%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D18&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

When Vox Machina first encountered the tree, Keyleth tried casting _Speak with Plants_, but was unsuccessful as the tree was effectively dead.[[25]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-30) Once the [Briarwoods](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Briarwoods "Briarwoods") had been killed and the tree began to re-grow, Keyleth was able to use _Commune with Nature_ to finally contact the tree.[[26]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-31) Afterward, whenever Keyleth needed to _Transport via Plants_ in or out of Whitestone, she would pass through the Sun Tree and frequently have a brief conversation along the way. According to the tree, it feels a slight tickle when she casts that spell.[[27]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-32)[[28]](https://criticalrole.fandom.com/wiki/Sun_Tree#cite_note-33)

**Keyleth:**  Yeah? Have you been working out?

**Sun Tree:**  As much as I can. You know, I'm a tree.

”
