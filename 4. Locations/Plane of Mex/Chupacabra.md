
### **Chupacabra**
*Huge monstrosity, chaotic evil*

**Armor Class**: 17 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points**: 256 (27d12 + 108)  
**Speed**: 50 ft., climb 30 ft.

---

**STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA**  
--- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---  
24 (+7) | 16 (+3) | 19 (+4) | 6 (-2) | 14 (+2) | 8 (-1)

---

**Saving Throws**: Str +12, Dex +8, Con +9  
**Skills**: Perception +7, Stealth +8, Intimidation +6, Athletics +12  
**Damage Resistances**: Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks  
**Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 17  
**Languages**: Understands Common and Abyssal but can't speak  
**Challenge**: 15 (13,000 XP)

---

### **Traits**

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day):** If the chupacabra fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

**Heart Ripper (Recharge 6):** If the chupacabra reduces a creature to 0 hit points, it can rip out the creature's heart. The creature dies instantly, and it cannot be revived except by *true resurrection* or *wish*.

**Keen Smell:** The chupacabra has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on smell.

**Regeneration:** The chupacabra regains 10 hit points at the start of its turn. If it takes radiant or fire damage, this trait doesn't function at the start of the chupacabra's next turn. The chupacabra dies only if it starts its turn with 0 hit points and doesn't regenerate.

**Pounce:** If the chupacabra moves at least 20 feet straight toward a creature and then hits it with a claw attack on the same turn, that target must succeed on a DC 18 Strength saving throw or be knocked prone. If the target is prone, the chupacabra can make one bite attack against it as a bonus action.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack.** The chupacabra makes three attacks: one with its bite and two with its claws.

**Bite.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 26 (4d8 + 7) piercing damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 18 Constitution saving throw or suffer a debilitating heart pain, taking an additional 14 (4d6) necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Claw.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 7) slashing damage.

---

### **Legendary Actions**
The chupacabra can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time, and only at the end of another creature's turn. The chupacabra regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

- **Move.** The chupacabra moves up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Bite (Costs 2 Actions).** The chupacabra makes one bite attack.
- **Leap (Costs 3 Actions).** The chupacabra leaps to an unoccupied space within 30 feet. Each creature within 10 feet of the space where the chupacabra lands must succeed on a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw or take 21 (6d6) bludgeoning damage and be knocked prone.

---

### **Lair Actions (Optional)**
The chupacabra can take lair actions on initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties):

- **Blood Mist.** A thick mist of blood seeps into the area, obscuring vision. The area is heavily obscured within a 60-foot radius for 1 round.
- **Terrifying Howl.** The chupacabra lets out a bone-chilling howl. Each enemy within 120 feet that can hear it must make a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute. A frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

---

This Chupacabra would be a terrifying encounter for your party of level 5-6 characters. It is physically imposing, fast, and with a mix of strong regeneration and damaging attacks, it fits the fearsome reputation described by your party. The **Heart Ripper** ability gives it a brutal and terrifying edge, perfect for a monster of legend!
