### **The Plane of Mex**

The Plane of Mex is a harsh, unforgiving realm located on the edge of the Infernal Planes, where the heat is relentless and the winds howl through the desolate, desert-like landscape. It is a plane shaped by hardship, violence, and the constant struggle for survival, where trust is scarce and the fight for resources is constant.

---

### **Geography and Environment**

Mex is a vast, arid desert. The land stretches endlessly in all directions, with towering dunes and rocky outcrops dotting the otherwise desolate horizon. The temperature is scorching, with the heat of the plane coming close to the searing temperatures of the planes of Hell. The sun never sets in Mex, and the winds never stop howling, turning the world into a perpetual dust storm. 

Frequent dust storms rip across the plane, carrying with them the stinging, scalding sands that have worn down even the strongest of materials. These storms can last for days, blinding those caught in them, and burying anything in their path under tons of sand. The dust storms are accompanied by powerful gusts of wind that can tear apart the unprepared and are known to carry dangerous predators through the plane with them.

---

### **Water and Survival**

Water is the single most valuable resource on the Plane of Mex. The precious few oases that dot the barren desert are fiercely contested, often held by powerful gangs or cartels that control access to the resource. Rivers are non-existent, and rain is nothing more than a legend told by the few humanoids who still live here. 

The creatures that call this plane home have evolved to survive without much water, drawing moisture from their surroundings, or from the blood of their victims. The **chupacabra** is a prime example, a predator that feeds on the hearts and blood of its prey, allowing it to endure in this barren wasteland.

---

### **Villages and Settlements**

There are very few humanoid settlements in Mex. The constant dust storms, lack of water, and harsh climate have driven most away. The few who remain live in scattered villages, tucked away from the winds and hidden behind rocky outcrops or nestled in valleys where they can escape the worst of the storms. These villages are far from safe, however, as they are frequently raided by roving gangs or caught in the crossfire of the endless cartel wars.

The architecture of these villages is practical and built for survival. Homes are carved out of rock, sunk underground, or built with thick, sandblasted walls to protect their inhabitants from the constant wind and dust. The windows are small, the doors reinforced, and any opening is covered to prevent the fine, stinging sand from blowing in.

---

### **The Cartels and Gangs**

The majority of humanoid life in Mex is organized into small, violent gangs that fight over every scrap of territory and every precious drop of water. These gangs are ruthless, constantly warring with one another over water sources, resources, and dominance. 

#### **The Gangs**
- **Sand Serpents:** A vicious, nomadic gang that roams the desert, pillaging any village or settlement they come across. They are feared for their cruelty and their expertise in desert survival. Their banner is a coiling serpent made of sand, and they leave a trail of death and ruin in their wake.
- **The Dune Raiders:** Masters of guerrilla tactics, these raiders attack under cover of dust storms, plundering their enemies and leaving without a trace. They are known for using the environment to their advantage, ambushing their foes with deadly precision.
- **Bloodfang:** Known for their allegiance to the Chupacabra, these bloodthirsty marauders wear the skins of their prey and are said to offer sacrifices to the fierce predator in exchange for power.

---

### **The Major Cartel: El Cártel del Sol**

While smaller gangs skirmish across the plane, **El Cártel del Sol** is the ruling cartel that sits above them all. They control the only major city on Mex, **Ciudad del Sol**, and maintain a tenuous hold on the entire plane by sheer force. 

The cartel thrives in the chaotic, lawless environment of Mex, and they actively encourage the warring between lesser gangs. They offer no help to the struggling settlements and villages, instead taking pleasure in watching the destruction unfold. **Water**, the most precious resource on Mex, is hoarded by El Cártel del Sol, and they use it as leverage to control the populace.

---

### **Ciudad del Sol**

**Ciudad del Sol** is the only major city in Mex, and it is a sprawling, chaotic metropolis of crumbling buildings, narrow streets, and towering spires. At the heart of the city stands the **Palacio del Sol**, the stronghold of the cartel. It is a fortress of stone and steel, protected by cartel enforcers and powerful wards.

The city is a maze of oppression and despair. The citizens are heavily taxed, overworked, and oppressed, living in constant fear of the cartel. The cartel’s leadership is unstable, as anyone who kills the current leader of the cartel takes their place. This has resulted in a never-ending cycle of bloodshed and betrayal as ambitious lieutenants vie for control.

---

### **Leadership and Structure of El Cártel del Sol**

The leadership of the cartel is chaotic and unpredictable. Anyone who kills the cartel leader becomes the new ruler, resulting in a constant power struggle. The current leader, **Joaquín "El Rey Sangriento" Delgado**, holds his position through sheer force and brutality, but his rule is constantly challenged by underlings and rival factions within the cartel.

Beneath the leader are the **Capitanes**, ruthless enforcers who rule different sectors of the city with an iron fist. They enforce the law of the cartel, ensuring that no one steps out of line, and they take pleasure in the suffering of the people. It is a brutal hierarchy where only the strongest survive, and loyalty is fleeting.

---

### **Creatures of Mex**

The harsh environment of Mex has bred creatures that are equally fierce and resilient. Many of these creatures have adapted to the dry, scorching conditions, and some are drawn to the plane from other parts of the multiverse.

- **Chupacabras:** The apex predator of Mex. These creatures stalk the deserts and mountains, feeding on the hearts of their victims. They are feared by all, and some gangs even worship them as divine beasts.
- **Dust Elementals:** Born from the constant dust storms, these elementals wreak havoc across the plane. They are mindless forces of destruction, sweeping through villages and leaving nothing but ruin.
- **Desert Wyrms:** Massive, burrowing creatures that tunnel through the sand. These predators wait for unsuspecting travelers to stumble upon their lairs before attacking with terrifying speed and force.
- **Scorpionfolk:** A rare but dangerous breed of humanoid, half-man, half-scorpion, who live deep in the desert wastes. They are known for their poison-laden weapons and vicious territorial disputes.
  
---

### **Adventures in Mex**

Adventurers who find themselves in Mex will face a brutal and unforgiving landscape. The constant dust storms, the never-ending thirst, and the deadly predators make survival a challenge on its own. However, for those who seek riches or power, there are rumors of hidden **water caches**, **ancient artifacts**, and **forgotten temples** buried beneath the sand.

---

#### **Potential Adventure Hooks**
- **Water Wars:** The party is hired to protect a village’s water supply from a powerful gang intent on seizing it. Will they defend the village or be tempted by the wealth the gang offers in exchange for betrayal?
- **Ciudad del Sol:** The party is tasked with infiltrating Ciudad del Sol to assassinate the leader of El Cártel del Sol, but must navigate the dangerous politics of the city.
- **Chupacabra Hunt:** A rare bounty is placed on a particularly deadly Chupacabra terrorizing villages. The party must track and defeat this creature, all while dealing with other hunters, rival gangs, and the environment itself.
  
---

### **Atmosphere**

Mex is a place of constant tension. The heat, the dust, and the ever-present threat of violence create a world where survival is the only priority. The law of the land is simple: might makes right. Trust is a rare commodity, and every shadow could conceal a knife aimed at your back.
