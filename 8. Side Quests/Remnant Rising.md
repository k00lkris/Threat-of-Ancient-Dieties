### **Remnant Rising (High-Level Adventure, Level 10-15)**

_A deadly conspiracy festers in the shadows of Stilben as a Remnant sect known as **The Punctured Eye** methodically infiltrates and dismantles the city's power structure in their quest for a powerful artifact of Vecna._

---

## **Adventure Summary**

The players are drawn into a **web of assassination, infiltration, and high-stakes power struggles** in Stilben, as the **Punctured Eye**—a fanatical sect of the Remnants—has begun **"replacing"** key figures within the city's **guilds, political structures, and mercantile organizations.** Their goal: to secure an **artifact of Vecna** hidden deep within a powerful guild’s vault, using their insidious methods to **corrupt or eliminate anyone who stands in their way.**

When the players **discover the corpse of Artenn Gitelle,** the supposedly **living** leader of the Relic Seekers, they become entangled in a deadly **war of deception, paranoia, and murder.** The players must expose the Remnants before the entire city **falls under their control.**

---

## **Act 1: The Discovery**

### **Hook: A Body in the Swamp**

The adventure begins when the players are contacted by a **Clasp agent, a trusted Waterwatch officer, or a Silvercut Road merchant** who needs their help.

- **Read-Aloud Text:**  
    _The stench of decay clings to the air as you trudge through the marshlands west of Stilben. Your guide—a nervous man in Clasp leathers—gestures toward a shallow grave half-hidden by reeds. The body is fresh, barely decomposed, yet the face is unmistakable. This is Artenn Gitelle, guildmaster of the Relic Seekers… except you saw him just yesterday, alive and well in the city._

### **Clues at the Scene**

- **Medicine DC 15:** The body has only been dead for **a few days**.
- **Investigation DC 17:** The corpse's **right eye socket is empty**, surgically removed.
- **Arcana DC 18:** **Necrotic magic lingers** on the corpse, suggesting an unnatural transformation.
- **Insight DC 20:** Something is **deeply wrong**—the man they saw in Stilben is an imposter.

### **Call to Action**

- If **Artenn is dead, who is leading the Relic Seekers now?**
- The Clasp, Waterwatch, or Silvercut are all **deeply concerned**. They want answers—and they’re willing to pay handsomely for them.
- The players are **given an introduction** to meet with one of the faction leaders to investigate further.

---

## **Act 2: The Web of Lies**

### **Infiltrating the Guilds**

The players must navigate the **powerful factions of Stilben** to uncover the Punctured Eye's infiltration. Each guild or faction has a different reaction to their investigation.

#### **1. The Relic Seekers (Primary Target)**

- **Leader: "Artenn Gitelle"** (Actually a Remnant Doppelganger)
- The Relic Seekers were **smuggling an ancient artifact** through the city when things went wrong.
- **Clues:**
    - **Insight DC 17**: “Artenn” seems **off**—his mannerisms feel unnatural.
    - **Persuasion DC 19**: A nervous clerk reveals that “Artenn” **changed overnight**.
    - **Stealth DC 15 / Investigation DC 18**: Searching his **office** reveals documents with coded references to **"The Eye" and "The Hand."**

#### **2. The Waterwatch (Guard Force)**

- **Captain Julea Rennard** (Suspicious but fair)
- The Waterwatch has **found multiple bodies** matching high-profile figures who are still “alive” in Stilben.
- **Clues:**
    - **Intimidation DC 18**: A captured thief reveals whispers of **a cult that replaces people.**
    - **Investigation DC 20**: A ledger reveals **missing persons** among the guilds.

#### **3. The Clasp vs. The Myriad**

- The **Clasp** is furious, believing the **Myriad is responsible** for the disappearances.
- The **Myriad** is worried—their spies **vanish without a trace**.
- **Clues:**
    - The **Clasp’s underground safehouses** have been attacked and **left empty.**
    - The **Myriad’s leaders are too afraid to move openly.**
    - **Persuasion DC 18**: The players can convince **one faction** to help them.

### **The Reveal: "Artenn" Attacks**

When the players **push too far**, “Artenn” **attacks**.

- **Encounter:**
    
    - "Artenn" **transforms into a powerful undead Remnant lieutenant**.
    - **2–3 assassins** attack from the shadows.
    - The players **must defeat him** and **examine his body** to learn the truth:
        - **His missing eye has been replaced with a cursed stone.**
        - His **body shifts unnaturally**, revealing necrotic **ritual scars.**
- **The True Threat:** The Punctured Eye is **infiltrating the city** to find Vecna’s artifact—and the players are now on their hit list.
    

---

## **Act 3: The Punctured Eye's Stronghold**

### **Tracking the Cult**

With the pieces coming together, the players must **track the cult's true hideout.** They have **three potential leads:**

1. **An abandoned smuggling tunnel beneath the docks.**
2. **A ruined temple deep in the K’Tawl Swamp.**
3. **A noble estate on Stilben’s outskirts, recently purchased by an unknown buyer.**

Each location contains **traps, undead cultists, and elite Remnant assassins.**

### **The Stronghold of the Punctured Eye**

- The **heart of the cult** is a **desecrated temple to Ioun**, defiled by necrotic magic.
- A **massive vault** lies beneath the temple, sealed by **blood magic.**
- The artifact of Vecna is **inside**—and the cult’s leader is **trying to awaken it.**

---

## **Final Boss: The Punctured Eye’s High Priest**

The players **arrive too late**—the cult leader, **Vorthaal the Whispering**, has begun the final stage of the ritual.

- **Vorthaal, Undying Priest of Vecna** (CR 15)
    - An undead sorcerer **infused with Vecna’s essence**.
    - Uses **legendary actions** to manipulate shadows and strike with unseen force.
    - **Tactics:**
        - Summons **undead assassins** to fight in the darkness.
        - Warps reality to confuse the party.
        - If defeated, **his death triggers the artifact’s activation.**

---

## **Act 4: The Aftermath**

### **Options for Resolution**

1. **Destroy the Artifact:**
    
    - The players can **use divine energy** to shatter the artifact.
    - **The cult’s power collapses**—but some remnants **flee into the shadows.**
2. **Seal it Away:**
    
    - The artifact is **too powerful to destroy**.
    - The players must **hide it** before the Remnants strike again.
3. **Claim It for Themselves:**
    
    - If the party **dares**, they could **use the artifact’s power**.
    - This **attracts Vecna’s attention**—and makes them a target for **even darker forces.**

### **Rewards & Consequences**

- **Gold & Magic Items** from the cult’s treasure hoard.
- **Influence in Stilben**—the players become **legendary** for stopping the cult.
- **Future Threats:**
    - The Remnants **do not forget.**
    - The players have **earned Vecna’s attention**—for better or worse.

---

## **Conclusion**

This adventure **blends mystery, infiltration, and high-stakes combat**, with the players facing **an ancient conspiracy tied to Vecna himself.** The choices they make **will shape the future of Stilben**—and could draw them into **Vecna’s dark designs.**
