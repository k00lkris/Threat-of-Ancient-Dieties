### **Side Quest: The Lost Relics of the Swamp**

_A mission of intrigue, mystery, and danger deep in the K’Tawl Swamp_

---

### **Hook & Setup**

The party is approached by one of the following factions with a job offer:

1. **The Relic Seekers** – A secretive organization that deals in ancient artifacts and magical relics. They were the **original clients** of the smuggling deal and now fear their shipment has been intercepted.
    
    - **Contact:** **Saelir Vaelis**, an elven historian with a sharp mind and a sharper tongue. He believes the Clasp has double-crossed them and wants the relics recovered—at any cost.
2. **The Bronzesmiths** – A rival group of smugglers and artifact traders that see this as an opportunity to **steal the artifacts** and humiliate the Relic Seekers.
    
    - **Contact:** **Garth “Iron-Jaw” Brennig**, a burly human mercenary who offers the players a _better deal_ if they deliver the relics to the Bronzesmiths instead.
3. **The Clasp** – The original smugglers who **lost contact** with their own operatives in the swamp. The Clasp wants the party to recover the relics and—more importantly—**find out what happened** to their missing crew.
    
    - **Contact:** **Delwyn “Silk” Juno**, a smooth-talking half-elf Clasp agent, who offers a hefty reward for their return.

Regardless of who hires them, all leads point to **L’Arkhelle**, a **huge, half-mad tiefling bridge guard**, who was the last person to see the Clasp couriers before they vanished.

---

### **Act 1: Tracking the Smugglers**

The party’s investigation begins with **L’Arkhelle**, who guards a decrepit wooden bridge leading deeper into the swamp.

#### **L’Arkhelle’s Information (Skill Checks & Clues)**

L’Arkhelle is paranoid and **not entirely sane**, but he knows more than he lets on. The party must persuade, bribe, or intimidate him to talk.

- **Insight (DC 14)** – L’Arkhelle is hiding something, but it’s fear-based. He’s terrified of what he saw.
- **Persuasion (DC 12)** – He will reveal that the smugglers **paid him off and crossed the bridge two weeks ago**, heading south.
- **Intimidation (DC 15)** – He panics and admits he saw **“something unnatural”** emerge from the fog after the smugglers crossed—something that made no sound.
- **Bribery (5 gp minimum)** – He reveals that **the couriers had a guide**, a local named **Orlen Marshfoot**, who led them deeper into the swamp.

If pressed further, he describes **the fog moving unnaturally**, whispering voices, and **shadowy figures watching from the trees**. He refuses to step foot into the swamp.

---

### **Act 2: Into the Swamp**

The players track **Orlen Marshfoot** (DC 14 Survival or Investigation), whose shack lies abandoned **half a day’s journey into the swamp**.

#### **The Shack Encounter**

The shack is **ransacked and eerily quiet**. Inside, the players find:

- **Bloodstains and drag marks leading into the swamp.**
- **Scraps of parchment detailing the stolen artifacts**, including descriptions of ancient Syngornian relics—some with **powerful enchantments**.
- **A carved wooden effigy of a withered hand** (Arcana DC 15 reveals it as a **ward against spirits**).

A **survival check (DC 13)** reveals tracks leading **deeper into the fog**, where the swamp becomes unnaturally cold.

---

### **Act 3: The Lost Smugglers**

The players eventually find the **remains of the Clasp smugglers**—**their bodies drained of blood and covered in strange, arcane markings**. The stolen **artifacts are missing**.

As the players investigate, **they are attacked by spectral beings**—**The Mistbound**, ancient Syngornian spirits bound to the swamp.

#### **Combat Encounter: The Mistbound**

- **2 Mistbound Revenants (CR 4) per 5 players**
- **1 Mistbound Wraith (CR 6) as a leader**

**Abilities:**

- **Mist Walk:** They move through fog as difficult terrain and can teleport short distances.
- **Spirit Drain:** A melee attack that siphons life energy.
- **Whispering Horror (Wraith only):** Can cause fear-based hallucinations.

If defeated, the players find clues indicating that **someone disturbed an ancient elven ruin**—and the artifacts **may have reawakened something terrible**.

---

### **Act 4: The Ruins and the Choice**

Deeper in the swamp, they find the hidden Syngornian ruins, where the missing artifacts are laid **upon an ancient altar**, radiating power.

**At the ruins, they discover:**

- **A dying survivor of the Clasp smugglers**, who mumbles about **a terrible presence watching them from the mist**.
- **An ancient inscription**, warning against **removing the relics** and disturbing the spirits.

**Final Decision:**

- **Return the artifacts** to their original resting place (risking the ire of the faction that hired them).
- **Take the artifacts for their employer** (potentially angering the spirits).
- **Attempt to cleanse the site**, but doing so may require **a blood sacrifice**.

Regardless of the choice, **something in the swamp is still watching them… and it may not be satisfied with their decision.**

---

### **Potential Rewards & Consequences**

- **The Relic Seekers** reward them with **750 gp and a magic item** if they return the relics.
- **The Bronzesmiths** will pay **1000 gp for the relics** but will disappear without a trace afterward.
- **The Clasp** offers **a permanent connection with their network** in addition to **gold and favors**.
- **If they anger the spirits**, they **gain a supernatural mark**—something will come for them later.

---

### **Follow-up Plot Hooks**

- **The Whispering Mist**: Something ancient still stirs in the swamp. The players’ involvement may have awakened a slumbering horror.
- **The Clasp’s Revenge:** If the party double-crosses the Clasp, **they will be marked for death** in Stillben.
- **An Unfinished Ritual:** The Syngornian ruins may contain more secrets, and the artifacts could be key to something greater.
