### **Side Quest: Strife in the Shadows**

_A high-stakes political and criminal intrigue adventure in the underbelly of Stilben_

---

## **Act 1: Caught in the Crossfire**

### **Hook & Setup**

As the party navigates the streets of Stilben, they hear **shouts, the clang of steel, and the crack of gunfire**. Rounding a corner, they witness a **full-on street battle**—**Clasp and Myriad enforcers** brutally cutting each other down in broad daylight.

Suddenly, a **wounded Clasp member stumbles into the party** before being cut down by a Myriad agent, who then turns toward them. Before they can react, Waterwatch officers arrive, blowing whistles and calling for **the arrest of everyone involved**.

#### **Combat Encounter: The Street Brawl**

- **2 Clasp Enforcers (CR 3)**
- **2 Myriad Assassins (CR 4)**
- **1 Myriad Shadowblade (CR 5) leading the Myriad side**
- **Waterwatch Guards (Non-Hostile unless attacked; they attempt to arrest the party post-fight)**

The Waterwatch assumes **the party is part of the fight** and moves to detain them. They will have to:

- **Talk their way out (Persuasion DC 18)**
- **Escape (Stealth DC 16 or Acrobatics DC 14)**
- **Bribe the guards (500 gp minimum)**
- **Get arrested (leading to Act 2’s prison escape/political maneuvering)**

If they fight the Waterwatch, **they will become fugitives in Stilben** and have to flee or hide.

---

## **Act 2: Caught Between Two Criminal Empires**

Whether they escape or get arrested, word spreads fast. Within **hours**, representatives from both **The Clasp** and **The Myriad** contact them separately.

### **Option 1: The Clasp’s Offer**

- Contact: **Alyssa Thorncloak**, a half-elf spymaster
- The Clasp sees them as **potential assets** and offers **safe haven** in exchange for their help in eradicating the Myriad presence in Stilben.
- **Job Offer:** Eliminate **Dario "The Fox" Esteban**, a Myriad lieutenant running **a secret smuggling operation** out of a riverside warehouse.

### **Option 2: The Myriad’s Offer**

- Contact: **Joren Vesker**, a smooth-talking human “businessman”
- The Myriad views them as **wild cards** who could tip the balance of power in Stilben.
- **Job Offer:** Assassinate **Clasp Captain Aster Bale**, who oversees **Stilben’s dockside operations**.

### **Option 3: Neutrality or Playing Both Sides**

- If the party refuses both offers, **they are now enemies to both factions** and must figure out how to clear their name and escape Stilben.
- They could **play both sides**—taking jobs from both but ultimately betraying one.

---

## **Act 3: The Job (or the Betrayal)**

The party chooses a **job** (or attempts to deceive both sides). The mission could involve:

### **1. The Clasp Mission (Attack the Myriad Warehouse)**

- **Infiltration Options:**
    
    - Sneak in through the **sewer tunnels (Stealth DC 16)**
    - Bribe a **dock worker (50 gp)**
    - **Ambush a Myriad patrol** and steal their uniforms (Deception DC 18)
- **Combat Encounter:**
    
    - **Dario "The Fox" Esteban** (CR 7, Mastermind Rogue)
    - **4 Myriad Enforcers (CR 3 each)**
    - **2 Arcane Operatives (CR 4, Warlocks who enhance Myriad agents with magic)**
- **Key Twist:** Inside, the party finds **a captured Clasp agent** being tortured. Rescuing them **cements loyalty to the Clasp**.
    

### **2. The Myriad Mission (Assassinate Captain Bale)**

- **Infiltration Options:**
    
    - Pose as **Clasp recruits (Deception DC 18)**
    - Break into Bale’s manor **through an underground passage**
    - **Poison Bale’s wine** (requires alchemical supplies)
- **Combat Encounter:**
    
    - **Captain Aster Bale (CR 7, Duelist & Gunslinger)**
    - **3 Clasp Enforcers (CR 3 each)**
    - **2 Elite Clasp Snipers (CR 4)**
- **Key Twist:** The Myriad **double-crosses them**, intending to eliminate them **after the job** to cover their tracks.
    

---

## **Act 4: Aftermath & Consequences**

### **Potential Outcomes**

1. **Ally with the Clasp:**
    
    - The party **gains safe passage** through Clasp-controlled territories.
    - The Myriad marks them as **enemies**.
    - The Waterwatch might **overlook their involvement**, but only if bribed.
2. **Ally with the Myriad:**
    
    - The Myriad grants **gold, rare contraband, and a contact in Wildemount**.
    - The Clasp **places a bounty** on their heads.
3. **Betray Both Sides:**
    
    - The party uncovers **evidence** that could **destroy** both factions.
    - The Waterwatch **clears their name** in exchange for information.
    - The party **becomes legendary criminals** in Stilben, famous (or infamous).
4. **Flee the City:**
    
    - They barely escape with their lives, but **both factions will remember them**.
    - The Waterwatch may **still suspect** them.

---

### **Follow-up Hooks**

- **The Clasp or Myriad offers them bigger jobs**—perhaps abroad.
- **A bounty hunter is hired** to track them down.
- **They uncover a secret third party** manipulating both factions (a noble, a rival guild, or even an outside kingdom).

---

### **Rewards**

- **Gold:** 750–1,500 gp depending on their choices.
- **Magic Items (from Myriad or Clasp vaults):**
    - _Cloak of Shadows_ (Advantage on Stealth checks)
    - _Poisoner’s Blade_ (Dagger that applies poison on crits)
    - _Smuggler’s Ledger_ (Gives insight into secret dealings across Tal’Dorei)
