Here’s a detailed **harpy nest-clearing adventure** for your campaign, set near Whitestone. This can serve as a perfect side-quest or even a diversion after the Hargraves encounter, giving the party a classic monster-hunting experience.

### **Adventure Overview**

The people of Whitestone have recently been plagued by **harpy raids**. These winged terrors are notorious for luring travelers off the cliffs and attacking caravans. The problem has gotten worse, with a significant nest located in the cliffs near Whitestone. A local merchant offers a handsome reward for any adventurers willing to clear out the nest.

---

### **Quest Hook**

While resting in **Whitestone** after their encounter with Hargraves, the party hears a rumor at the market square or a local inn (such as The Silver Chalice) about harpies attacking travelers and caravans on the cliffs nearby. A **merchant** they’ve encountered before approaches them, offering to pay **500 gold pieces** for anyone who can eliminate the harpy threat, as it's endangering trade and travel between Whitestone and the port cities. 

- The party could also encounter survivors of a harpy attack in Whitestone, possibly desperate travelers or wounded merchants who barely survived being lured to the edge of a cliff by the harpies’ song.

**Quest Objective:** Eliminate the **harpy nest** on the cliffs near Whitestone.

---

### **The Cliffs and Journey to the Nest**

The harpy nest is located in the steep, jagged cliffs a few miles northeast of Whitestone, overlooking the ocean. The cliffs themselves are treacherous, full of narrow paths, loose stones, and steep drops. The journey to the nest will take about half a day, and there are **several challenges** the party will face along the way.

**Skill Challenges**:

1. **Navigating the Cliffs (DC 14 Survival or Perception check)**:
   - The cliffs are perilous, and the party needs to avoid areas prone to rockslides and crumbling ledges. If the players fail, they trigger a **rockslide** or cause part of the cliffside to break away, forcing them to make a **DC 13 Dexterity saving throw** or take **2d6 bludgeoning damage** from falling debris.

2. **Strong Winds (DC 13 Strength or Acrobatics check)**:
   - The closer the party gets to the harpy nest, the stronger the winds become, making it difficult to keep balance. Failing this check means a character risks being knocked prone or even **sliding toward the edge of a cliff**. A player could fall, but a **DC 15 Dexterity saving throw** can be used to grab onto the edge before plummeting.

3. **Hearing the Harpies’ Song (DC 15 Wisdom saving throw)**:
   - As they near the nest, the harpies start singing to lure the party toward dangerous areas of the cliffs. Each player must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or become **charmed** by the harpy song. A charmed player moves toward the cliff’s edge. They can repeat the saving throw each round to break free.

---

### **The Harpy Nest**

Once the party navigates the cliffs and approaches the harpy nest, they find it tucked into a **series of caves** and **rocky outcrops** jutting out from the cliffs, overlooking the crashing waves below.

The nest is a **large, open cave** system with smaller alcoves. The harpies have made it their home, weaving together nests of bones, stolen goods, feathers, and detritus. The air smells foul, and the ground is littered with the remains of their victims.

#### **Key Areas of the Nest**:

1. **The Outer Perch:**
   - This is where the harpies keep a lookout. The path here is narrow, and the party will likely encounter **scouting harpies** who swoop in to attack.

2. **The Main Nest:**
   - The largest chamber in the cave, where the bulk of the harpies reside. There are about **6-8 harpies** here, along with their **matriarch** (a **harpy matriarch** with increased hit points and spell-like abilities).

3. **The Treasure Trove:**
   - In the back of the nest, the harpies have a stash of loot taken from fallen travelers. This treasure is guarded by **1-2 giant vultures** the harpies have tamed to act as guard animals.

---

### **Enemies**

#### **Harpies** (8 total):
- These standard harpies will engage the party in the outer perch and in the main nest. They will use their **Luring Song** to draw the players into precarious positions near cliff edges, attacking with their claws and clubs.

#### **[[Harpy Matriarch]]**:
- The matriarch is larger and more powerful than the others, with greater intelligence and cunning. She has **additional hit points** (around 65 HP) and uses **spells like Hold Person, Silence**, and **Mirror Image** to confuse and restrain the party while commanding her underlings.
  
- **Tactics**: The harpy matriarch will sing to lure the most vulnerable members of the party off the cliff and will use the terrain to her advantage, flying up and out of reach of melee combatants. She will also taunt the party, promising to "show them the beauty of the sea from up close" as they teeter near the edge.

#### **Giant Vultures** (2):
- These vulture-like monstrosities defend the treasure trove. They have **keen sight** and can spot the party from a distance, swooping down to attack with their beaks and talons.

---

### **Cliffside Dangers**

1. **Falling Debris**: While battling harpies or the vultures, the cliffs themselves can become dangerous. A critical failure during combat could cause the rocks beneath a player to crumble, making them roll a **DC 14 Dexterity saving throw** to avoid falling or taking **2d6 bludgeoning damage** from loose rocks.

2. **Wind Gusts**: The high winds near the cliff edges make ranged attacks harder. **Ranged weapon attacks** suffer disadvantage unless the player is within the shelter of the nest caves.

3. **Falling Off the Cliff**: Players charmed by the harpies or caught in the wrong place can fall off the cliff. **Falling damage** (1d6 per 10 feet, maximum 20d6) is deadly if they fall the full height, but clever players might use ropes, spells like **Feather Fall**, or quick thinking to save their companions.

---

### **Rewards**

- **500 gp reward** from the merchant upon their return.
- **Treasure Trove in the Nest**:
  - 300 gp in various coins.
  - 3 **gems worth 50 gp each**.
  - A **magical longsword** (with a minor enchantment, like +1).
  - **Scroll of Feather Fall** – Found tucked in the remains of a previous victim.
  - **Ring of Water Walking** – Found on a skeletal finger near the nest’s treasure hoard.
  - Various trinkets, jewelry, and mundane goods looted from travelers.

### **Experience Points**

- Each harpy is worth **200 XP**, and the matriarch is worth **700 XP**. The giant vultures are worth **450 XP** each. Defeating the entire nest and rescuing any surviving captives nets additional **300 XP** as a story reward.

---

### **Complications and Side Quests**

- **Captive Merchant**: Among the treasure and bones of fallen travelers, the party discovers a **merchant captive** who has managed to survive in the caves, held prisoner by the harpies. If rescued, the merchant offers a **rare magical item** (like a Bag of Holding) as a reward.
  
- **Cursed Treasure**: Some of the treasure taken by the harpies might be cursed, inflicting the party with a minor curse that they must later dispel, leading them to another adventure.
  
- **Reinforcements**: If the party decides to leave and return later, the harpies may call for reinforcements from other nearby nests, increasing the difficulty if they return.

---

### **Conclusion**

Upon their return to Whitestone, the players are celebrated as heroes by the merchant who offered the quest, and the people of the city appreciate their bravery. They might even be invited to a **feast in their honor** or receive a **small discount** at local shops as a thank you.

If the party leaves the nest unscathed, they gain valuable experience and perhaps new allies. However, if they struggle or fail, the harpy menace could grow even stronger, and future caravans might be lost to their savage attacks.

This adventure can be scaled for difficulty by adjusting the number of harpies and incorporating environmental hazards, making it a thrilling cliffside battle that plays into the harpy’s natural advantages.
