### **Vornelith Blackthorn - Necromancer**
Vornelith Blackthorn is the necromancer responsible for the ritual causing blood to appear in the Greyfield. He has ties to the darker forces of necromancy and seeks to complete a ritual to resurrect a powerful undead being, using blood from the graves in the Greyfield to fuel his magic.

---

**Medium humanoid (human), neutral evil**

---

**Armor Class**: 15 (Mage Armor)  
**Hit Points**: 78 (12d8 + 24)  
**Speed**: 30 ft.

---

**STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA**
--- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---
10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 16 (+3)

---

**Saving Throws**: Int +7, Wis +4  
**Skills**: Arcana +7, Deception +6, Stealth +5  
**Damage Resistances**: Necrotic  
**Senses**: Passive Perception 11  
**Languages**: Common, Draconic, Infernal  
**Challenge**: 6 (2,300 XP)

---

### **Traits**

**Spellcasting**:  
Vornelith is a 9th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following spells prepared:

- **Cantrips** (at will): *Chill Touch*, *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*, *Toll the Dead*  
- **1st level** (4 slots): *Mage Armor*, *Magic Missile*, *Disguise Self*, *Shield*  
- **2nd level** (3 slots): *Mirror Image*, *Hold Person*, *Invisibility*  
- **3rd level** (3 slots): *Counterspell*, *Vampiric Touch*, *Animate Dead*  
- **4th level** (3 slots): *Blight*, *Greater Invisibility*  
- **5th level** (1 slot): *Cloudkill*

---

**Reanimate Minions** (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest):  
As an action, Vornelith can raise two skeletons or zombies from nearby corpses. The undead are under his control for 1 hour, after which they crumble to dust.

**Dark Devotion**:  
Vornelith has advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**:  
Vornelith makes two melee attacks or casts one spell and makes a melee attack.

**Dagger**: *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack*: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. *Hit*: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage.

**Necrotic Blast**: *Ranged Spell Attack*: +7 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. *Hit*: 21 (6d6) necrotic damage.

**Summon Blood Ghosts (Recharge 6)**:  
Vornelith can summon two **blood ghosts** to aid him in combat. The blood ghosts appear in an unoccupied space within 30 feet and act immediately after Vornelith's turn. They remain for 1 hour or until destroyed.

---

### **Reactions**

**Shield** (1st-Level Spell):  
When hit by an attack, Vornelith can cast *Shield* to gain +5 to his AC until the start of his next turn, potentially causing the attack to miss.

**Parry Magic**:  
If a spell would deal damage to him and he can see the caster, Vornelith can use his reaction to halve the damage.

---

### **Legendary Actions**  
(Vornelith has 1 legendary action per round, and they reset at the start of his turn.)

- **Dark Grasp**: Vornelith can force one creature within 30 feet to make a DC 15 Strength saving throw or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage and be restrained until the start of his next turn.
  
---

### **Tactics:**
- Vornelith will cast *Mage Armor* before combat to increase his AC.
- He prefers to stay at range, using spells like *Vampiric Touch* to keep himself sustained and *Animate Dead* to summon more minions.
- If threatened, he will cast *Greater Invisibility* or *Mirror Image* to protect himself and keep the pressure on the party with necrotic damage spells.

---

### **Blood Ghosts**
The **blood ghosts** are malevolent spirits of those whose blood was drained in Vornelith's rituals. They are incorporeal and filled with anger and pain, drawn to fresh blood.

---

**Medium undead, chaotic evil**

---

**Armor Class**: 13  
**Hit Points**: 45 (7d8 + 14)  
**Speed**: 0 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

---

**STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA**
--- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---
10 (+0) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 7 (-2) | 10 (+0) | 15 (+2)

---

**Damage Resistances**: Acid, Fire, Lightning, Thunder; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks  
**Damage Immunities**: Cold, Necrotic, Poison  
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Grappled, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Prone, Restrained  
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10  
**Languages**: Understands Common but cannot speak  
**Challenge**: 2 (450 XP)

---

### **Traits**

**Incorporeal Movement**:  
The blood ghost can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

**Blood Drain (Recharge 5-6)**:  
As an action, the blood ghost can target one creature within 5 feet of it. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 18 (4d8) necrotic damage, and the blood ghost regains hit points equal to half the necrotic damage dealt.

**Ghostly Visage**:  
As a bonus action, the blood ghost can momentarily make its face appear as that of a creature's loved one or a friend. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened of the blood ghost for 1 minute. A frightened target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

---

### **Actions**

**Life Drain**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +5 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (3d6 + 3) necrotic damage. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken. This reduction lasts until the target finishes a long rest. The target dies if this effect reduces its hit point maximum to 0.

---

### **Encounter Balance**
- For a party of 5 level 5 characters, a challenging encounter would include **Vornelith Blackthorn** and **3 blood ghosts**. The combination of his necrotic abilities, spellcasting, and the blood ghosts’ draining effects would make for a formidable fight.
- The blood ghosts’ **Life Drain** and **Blood Drain** abilities will force players to manage their resources and tactics carefully.
- This encounter can be further balanced by using the terrain of a haunted graveyard or ritual site, where the blood ghosts can use their **Incorporeal Movement** to move through obstacles, making them harder to pin down.
