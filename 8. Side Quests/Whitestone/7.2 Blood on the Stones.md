### **Act 1: The Call to Investigate**

**Hook:**  
Members of the Paleguard approach the players with a request to investigate the mysterious bloodstains that have appeared in the Greyfield for three consecutive nights. The blood appears on gravestones and mausoleums, with no visible source. No one has been able to hear or see anything suspicious despite enhanced patrols and magical detection spells.

- The Paleguard Captain, **Captain Soren**, tells the party they are concerned about this mystery and offers a reward for solving it.
- He believes the matter may be tied to an ancient curse or perhaps some dark magic rooted in Whitestone’s bloody history, but no solid evidence has been found.

**Clues at the start:**  
- **Blood Spatter Analysis:** The blood appears to be fresh and is splashed across the gravestones and mausoleums with little consistency in pattern, as if flung or splashed from a height or from multiple directions. The amount is more than a small cut would produce, but not enough to indicate a fatal injury.
- **Examine the Blood:** Investigation or Medicine checks reveal the blood is from different sources: it appears to be human blood mixed with traces of celestial and fiendish taint (for those with magical detection capabilities).
- **Magical Residue:** Detect Magic reveals a faint lingering aura of **Necromancy**, though too weak to trace. This fades after a few minutes.
- **Tracks and Surveillance:** There are no footprints or signs of forced entry, and despite enhanced Paleguard patrols, no one has seen any individuals near the sites.

---

### **Act 2: Interviewing Suspects**

The party will have to investigate in Whitestone, interviewing residents, the Paleguard, and other key NPCs. Each person provides different clues or red herrings.

**Suspects to Interview:**

1. **Old Man Mathis:**  
   A strange, reclusive hermit who lives near the edge of the Greyfield. Mathis claims to hear whispers from the graves at night. He insists the blood is the doing of "restless spirits" or “the hunger beneath the stones.”  
   - **Clue:** Mathis mentions seeing pale figures “gliding” between the graves on the nights the blood appeared. He describes them as "not quite there," hinting at some illusion or projection.

2. **Varya Blackwood (Gravekeeper):**  
   The diligent and hard-working gravekeeper of Greyfield. Varya insists no one has tampered with the graves and is deeply offended at the idea of desecration. She admits she hasn’t seen anything suspicious and points to the ancient crypts where older magics lie dormant.  
   - **Clue:** Varya notices a certain gravestone, **Elara Windsail’s** mausoleum, has been touched by the blood more frequently than others. The Windsail family has a long history in Whitestone, with ties to the Raven Queen.

3. **[[Evelyn Windrider]]:**  
   Evelyn is concerned but frustrated by the lack of progress. She believes the blood may be part of some dark ritual and is quick to assume foul play. She insists on providing the party with support from the Paleguard, though she’s more of a red herring than a true lead.  
   - **Clue:** She mentions some townsfolk who speak of strange sounds—wings flapping or soft whispers—near the Greyfield. However, these could just be local superstitions.

4. **Rosalind Hallow (Priestess of the Raven Queen):**  
   Rosalind tends to the temple near Greyfield and serves the Raven Queen. She is deeply concerned about the appearance of blood near the graves and worries it may signify a **breach between the material and the Shadowfell**. She is unaware of any current rituals involving blood, but warns the players that dark forces in Whitestone’s past could be stirring again.  
   - **Clue:** Rosalind suggests the blood may be part of a **necromantic ritual**, specifically something designed to call or bind the spirits of the dead.

---

### **Act 3: Unraveling the Mystery**

After gathering information, the players can return to the Greyfield at night to set up a **stakeout**. Through clever planning, the use of magic, and skill checks, they can eventually spot **ethereal figures** that seem to move through the cemetery, appearing to flick blood onto the gravestones. These figures are **blood ghosts**, created from a mixture of human blood and a necromantic spell tied to the **Elara Windsail mausoleum**.

**What’s really happening:**

1. **The Ritual and Purpose:**  
   A **cult of necromancers** who worship Vecna (or another dark entity) has been performing rituals in secret, using the blood to slowly awaken an ancient guardian buried beneath the cemetery. The necromancers are using the blood ghosts to perform their dark rites, flicking blood onto certain gravestones as part of a ritual to **open a portal to the Shadowfell**.

2. **The Leader:**  
   The leader of the cult, **Vornelith Blackthorn**, a skilled necromancer, has been using illusion magic to avoid detection. He uses a combination of **greater invisibility** and **illusionary magic** to mask his actions, sending out blood ghosts to complete the ritual. His ultimate goal is to raise an ancient **shadow guardian** and unleash it upon Whitestone.

---

### **Final Confrontation: The Blood Ritual**

**Encounter:**  
When the players finally figure out the ritual’s source and confront Vornelith, he will be found performing the final stage of the ritual in **Elara Windsail’s mausoleum**. The mausoleum contains a **hidden ritual chamber** where a dark altar is smeared with the blood of the living. The **blood ghosts** swirl around the room, and Vornelith is moments away from completing the ritual.

**Combat:**  
- The players must fight **Vornelith Blackthorn**, his necromancer acolytes, and the **blood ghosts** in a climactic battle.
- If the players allow Vornelith to finish the ritual, he will summon the **Shadow Guardian**, a powerful undead entity that could wreak havoc on Whitestone.
- Destroying the altar will disrupt the ritual and banish the blood ghosts.

---

### **Rewards and Outcomes**

- **Gold and Magic Items:**  
   The cultists have treasures tied to their dark arts, including **necromantic tomes**, **scrolls**, and a **dark relic** that can be tied into future storylines.
- **Prestige in Whitestone:**  
   If the players successfully stop the ritual, they are hailed as heroes by the Paleguard and receive rewards from the **de Rolo family**.
- **Future Adventure Hooks:**  
   The cult’s leader, Vornelith, may have ties to **Lord Hargraves** or other enemies, giving the players a lead on their next quest.

---

### **Possible NPCs to Include:**
- **Rosalind Hallow (Priestess of the Raven Queen)**: Could assist the players in breaking the blood ritual or provide guidance.
- **Evelyn Windrider**: Could be involved in the final confrontation, potentially sacrificing herself to save the town.
- **Old Man Mathis**: Could know more than he lets on and provide cryptic clues about the blood ghosts.

This mystery is filled with eerie atmosphere, ancient secrets, and a sense of escalating danger as the players investigate the strange occurrences in the Greyfield, all while uncovering the truth behind the necromantic ritual and stopping an ancient threat from awakening.


### **Revised Role for Evelyn Windrider:**

While Evelyn Windrider is not part of the Paleguard, she’s well-connected within Whitestone. As someone who runs a shop (Windrider's Wares) and works within **Castle Whitestone**, she has access to important information and the trust of some of Whitestone’s most influential citizens.

**Evelyn's Involvement in the Mystery:**
- Evelyn might know some of the townsfolk who frequent her shop and could provide subtle clues or even alibis.
- She could be a trusted confidante who helps the party analyze the situation, providing insight based on her knowledge of arcane items, herbs, and magic.
- Evelyn could also supply the party with useful resources (such as potions or scrolls) to help with their investigation.

---

### **How Evelyn Can Assist the Players:**

**Scene Idea 1: Consulting Evelyn**  
When the players begin to investigate the bloodstains at the Greyfield, Evelyn may provide a fresh perspective. They can approach her in her shop, **Windrider's Wares**, to get her thoughts.

- **Read-Aloud Text:**
  
  *The soft chime of a bell rings as you step inside Windrider's Wares, a quaint shop filled with herbs, crystals, and various arcane trinkets. The scent of dried flowers and incense fills the air as Evelyn Windrider, her long silver hair cascading over her shoulders, greets you with a warm smile.*  
  *"Ah, it's you again. I heard about the strange happenings in Greyfield," she says softly. "What brings you here this time? Perhaps I can offer some advice... or at least some potions to keep you safe?"*

- **Clue or Information Evelyn Can Provide:**
  - Evelyn knows of a **rare herb** that can enhance spells related to **spiritual detection**, helping the party when they perform rituals or detect magic in the Greyfield. She offers to give the players the herb if they think it will help.
  - Evelyn has heard strange rumors from customers who visit her shop. Some claim to have seen **shadows moving** through Greyfield on the nights when the blood appeared.

**Scene Idea 2: Evelyn’s Personal Connection to the Incident**  
Perhaps Evelyn had a distant ancestor, a **member of the Windsail family** or someone with close ties to Whitestone’s history, who is buried in the Greyfield. This could give her a personal investment in the mystery and a reason to support the players more actively.

- **Possible Side Quest or Clue:**  
   Evelyn mentions her ancestor, **Elara Windsail**, who is buried in Greyfield, noting that her gravestone has been one of the ones marked by blood. She asks the players to investigate further, wondering if her ancestor's grave has been disturbed. If the players agree, this could lead them to the **Elara Windsail mausoleum**, which is important to the necromancer’s ritual.

---

### **Final Showdown Involvement:**
While Evelyn wouldn’t be a front-line combatant, she could offer magical support if the players allow her to come with them. Her knowledge of arcane energy could be crucial in **disrupting the blood ghosts or the ritual**, though she would likely stay back during direct combat.

---

With these changes, Evelyn can still play a pivotal role in the mystery, acting as a knowledgeable civilian rather than a member of the Paleguard. She becomes a valuable ally without necessarily being part of the law enforcement side of things. This could also help cement her importance as a recurring NPC that the players can turn to for arcane knowledge and support throughout the campaign.
