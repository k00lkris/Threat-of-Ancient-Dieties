### **Scene 4: Entering the Mausoleum**

After defeating or driving off the **blood ghosts**, the players will naturally be drawn to the **Elara Windsail mausoleum**. This will lead them into the final confrontation with **Vornelith Blackthorn**.

---

**Read-Aloud Text (Approaching the Mausoleum):**

> _The old stone doors of the mausoleum creak open as you push them aside, revealing a dark interior. The air is thick with dust and the faint scent of decay. Inside, stone sarcophagi rest against the walls, their surfaces cracked and worn with age. But something else draws your attention—dark magic pulses from beneath the floor, and a faint, unsettling light flickers from a passageway below._

### **Room Descriptions and Population**

---

### **A1: Entrance Hall**

The players enter through the secret passage in **Elara Windsail's mausoleum**, which leads down to a narrow, spiraling staircase. At the bottom, they find themselves in the **Garden of Remembrance**, a somber and peaceful place filled with graves of other members of the Windsail family, dimly lit by soft magical light. Past the graves, they approach the entrance to a much larger tomb, which begins in **Room A1**.

---

**Read-Aloud Text for A1:**

>*You step into a cold stone chamber, illuminated by the flickering light of candles set in sconces along the walls. Directly before you stands a towering statue of the Raven Queen, her dark wings outstretched, eyes fixed forward in judgment. Her expression is serene, yet foreboding, as though she watches the passage of both time and souls. The air here feels thick, almost reverent, as if the weight of centuries presses down upon you.*

---

### **A2: The Sarcophagus Room**

This large chamber has columns flanking both sides, creating a regal atmosphere. In the center lie **four sarcophagi**, each ornately carved, with family names engraved in ancient script. These belong to important members of the Windsail family. A **second statue of the Raven Queen** dominates the eastern wall, its eyes gazing down upon the sarcophagi, as if the deity herself were overseeing their eternal rest. The soft glow of **lit candles** between the second, third, and fourth sarcophagi casts flickering shadows across the room. On the southern wall, two doors lead to **Room A3** and **Room A4**, and a door on the eastern wall leads to **Room A5**.

---

**Read-Aloud Text for A2:**

>*This room spans before you, its grandeur heightened by the columns that stand tall on either side. Four sarcophagi rest in the center of the chamber, their stone lids etched with delicate carvings. Candlelight flickers between the sarcophagi, casting long, dancing shadows on the walls. In the far east, a statue of the Raven Queen watches over the room, her cold, hollow eyes seemingly ever-vigilant.*

---

**Encounters in A2:**

- **Trigger:** If the players attempt to open any of the sarcophagi, they will awaken the spirits entombed inside, angered by the disturbance.
  
**Combat Encounter:**
- **3 Revenants** (one per opened sarcophagus) guard the tomb and rise to defend their resting places. They attack the party relentlessly unless they are appeased in some way (perhaps with an offering or a successful Persuasion or Religion check to show respect for their rest).

---

**Loot in A2:**

- **Sarcophagus 1:**  
   Inside is the preserved body of an ancient **Windsail Knight**, still clad in his ceremonial armor.
   - **Loot:** **+1 Full Plate Armor**, **Raven Queen Holy Symbol** (uncommon), 200 gp scattered across the bottom.

- **Sarcophagus 2:**  
   This sarcophagus contains a **Windsail Matriarch**, her body wrapped in rich silks, holding an ancient heirloom.
   - **Loot:** **Ring of the Raven Queen** (rare), 3 pieces of **fine jewelry** (150 gp each).

- **Sarcophagus 3:**  
   Inside lies an old priest of the Raven Queen, whose skeletal form still clutches an **ancient tome**.
   - **Loot:** **Scroll of Banishment** (rare), **Tome of Forgotten Souls** (grants +1 to Religion checks related to the Raven Queen).

- **Sarcophagus 4:**  
   A more recent Windsail, laid to rest with personal items rather than riches.
   - **Loot:** **Scroll of Shield** (uncommon), **100 gp**, a personal **diary** (contains a few minor details about the Windsail family history).

---

### **A3: The Office**

This room appears to have been **refashioned into a private study or office**. Unlike the tomb, it exudes a sense of modern use. Shelves are lined with books and curiosities, and a desk sits toward the eastern wall, its surface covered with scattered papers. A **grandfather clock** ticks steadily against the east wall, and there are **paintings** on both the north and east walls depicting various members of the Windsail family. A strange feeling lingers in the room, as if this space is being actively used for dark purposes.

---

**Read-Aloud Text for A3:**

>*This room is starkly different from the rest of the tomb. It feels lived in, modern, and disturbingly practical. A desk sits toward the far wall, papers scattered across its surface, and shelves crammed with tomes and artifacts line the walls. A soft, rhythmic ticking emanates from the grandfather clock in the corner, and the air feels oppressive, as though dark thoughts have lingered here for far too long.*

---

**Skill Challenges in A3:**
- **Investigation DC 15:** Scouring through the papers reveals that this office has been used to study necromantic rituals. There are notes about the Windsail family, particularly about **Elara Windsail**, detailing her connection to the Raven Queen and her bloodline’s importance in necromantic magic.
  
- **Arcana DC 17:** A closer look at the bookshelf reveals hidden magical glyphs among the tomes, designed to conceal dangerous information or summon a defensive ward if the wrong book is moved.

---

**Items on the Bookshelf:**

1. **Tome of Vecna’s Cults** (uncommon): Provides a +1 bonus to Arcana checks related to necromancy.
2. **Scroll of Necrotic Ward** (rare): A protective ward against necrotic energy.
3. **Book of Shadows** (rare): A tome bound in black leather that contains dark secrets. Grants a temporary +2 to Intelligence but comes with a curse.
4. **Map of Whitestone’s Catacombs** (rare): Reveals hidden sections of Whitestone’s underground passages.
5. **Scroll of Dispel Magic** (uncommon).

---

**Vecna's Book (on the west wall):**

- **Description:** The book on the west wall is an **ancient tome dedicated to Vecna**, filled with dark rituals and heretical knowledge. Simply touching it gives the reader a sense of overwhelming dread.
   - **Effect:** **Curse of Vecna**: Any creature that opens this book without taking precautions (e.g., casting protection spells or using a Religion check DC 18) suffers 3 levels of **exhaustion**. However, the book contains a ritual that can help in defeating necromancers by countering their undead minions.

---

### **A4: Alchemy Room**

This room has clearly been refashioned for **alchemy and dark rituals**. Shelves upon shelves are crammed with **jars of strange substances**, **vials of glowing liquids**, and **ancient books** on necromantic alchemy. The air smells faintly of sulfur and decay. This room is vital to whoever has been working here, as it provides the tools needed for rituals and experimentation.

---

**Read-Aloud Text for A4:**

>*The walls of this room are lined with shelves, each one cluttered with jars, vials, and strange glowing liquids. The air is thick with the smell of sulfur, and the faint hum of magic can be felt in the air. Books on dark rituals and alchemical processes sit stacked on a large table, while various instruments of an alchemist’s craft rest in neat rows. The room pulses with an aura of forbidden knowledge.*

---

**Items on the Shelves:**

1. **Jar of Preserved Eyes** (uncommon): Used for necromantic scrying spells.
2. **Potion of Necrotic Resistance** (rare): Grants resistance to necrotic damage for 1 hour.
3. **Vial of Shadow Essence** (rare): Can be used to craft shadow-based spells or inflictions.
4. **Scroll of Animate Dead** (rare).
5. **Handful of Grave Dirt** (uncommon): A component for necromantic rituals.
6. **Windsail Family Relic** (rare): A small, enchanted amulet that grants +1 to saving throws against undead creatures.
7. **Book of Necrotic Elixirs** (uncommon): Grants proficiency in alchemical crafting related to necromancy.
8. **Potion of Greater Healing** (uncommon).
9. **Jars of Preserved Blood** (rare): Blood from celestial and fiendish creatures, essential for certain necromantic spells.
10. **Shadow Weave Cloth** (rare): An enchanted cloth used in the creation of shadow-based armor or clothing.
### **Room Descriptions, Encounters, and Treasures**

### **Room A5: Hallway with Statues**

This hallway is grand, lined with a pair of **statues of suits of armor**, one against the east wall and the other against the west wall. The statues flank an archway that leads further south to **Room A6**. These statues seem ancient, but they are clearly well-maintained. The dim lighting causes shadows to stretch unnaturally long, giving the room a sense of foreboding.

---

**Read-Aloud Text for A5:**

>*You step into a hallway, flanked on either side by towering statues of ancient suits of armor. Their hollow visors seem to watch your every step, and the sound of your footfalls echoes uncomfortably in the narrow space. Through the archway at the end of the hall, a faint red light flickers, leading deeper into the crypt.*

---

**Encounter for A5:**

- **Statue Trap**: As the players pass through the archway into **Room A6**, the statues animate and **attempt to block the party’s escape** if they try to retreat.
  - **Trigger**: The statues will animate only if the players attempt to flee back through this hallway.
  - **Animated Armor Stat Blocks**: Use **2 Animated Armors** to block the party’s retreat. They only engage if the party attempts to retreat into Room A5.
  - **Tactics**: The statues attempt to grapple and block the exit, while not dealing too much damage.

---

### **Room A6: Pillars and the Bloody Grate**

This room is imposing, with **six stone pillars** rising from the floor, three on either side of the chamber. In the center of the room, a **large iron grate** is set into the floor, smeared with **dried blood**. The walls are covered in intricate carvings of the **Raven Queen**, and her symbols seem to pulse with a faint energy. The bloodstains on the grate suggest that something or someone has been sacrificed here.

---

**Read-Aloud Text for A6:**

>*The chamber opens up before you, the ceiling supported by six looming pillars of stone. At the center of the room lies an iron grate, its surface slick with congealed blood. Carvings of the Raven Queen adorn the walls, her cold, judging eyes watching over the space like a silent sentinel. The air feels heavy with the weight of old magic, and the faintest sound of dripping blood echoes through the chamber.*

---

**Encounter for A6:**

- **Summoning Trap**: The grate in the center of the room is a **magical trigger**, causing several **Wraiths** to be summoned if the players step on or investigate it.
  
  **Combat Encounter:**
  - **Wraiths** x2 and **Specters** x3 are summoned from the grate, drawn by the blood that stains it.
  - **Tactics**: The wraiths will attack with hit-and-run tactics, using the pillars to their advantage to dart in and out of melee range. The specters will focus on flanking the party members and draining their vitality.

**Loot for A6 (on the bodies of the wraiths and specters):**
- **Cloak of Shadows** (rare): This magical cloak grants the wearer advantage on Stealth checks and allows them to use the **invisibility** spell once per long rest.
- **80 gp** scattered around the grate in old, bloodstained coin.

### **Room A7: Alchemist's Laboratory**

This L-shaped room has clearly been repurposed into an **alchemist’s workshop**. Along the western wall, an ornate marble **alchemical altar** stands, dimly lit by the flickering light of two candles. Between them are two small statues: one depicting a severed hand, and the other, a glaring eye. There is a **partition** separating the hallway and the opening to the south, giving the space an eerie, claustrophobic feeling.

**Read-Aloud Text for A7:**

>*The air in this chamber is thick with the scent of chemicals and decay. On the far wall sits a marble altar, lit by the soft glow of two candles. Between them, a pair of statues—the severed hand and the glaring eye—seem to stare at you, exuding a palpable sense of menace. Shelves lined with vials and alchemical tools cover the walls, and the faint sound of bubbling liquids fills the space. Beyond a partition, you sense more of the room yet to be explored.*

---

**Encounter for A7:**

**Combat Encounter:**
- **Crawling Claw** x2 (weakened animated versions of the severed hand statue).
- **Trap**: The trap also causes the **Eye of Vecna Statue** to emit a burst of **necrotic energy** (DC 15 Constitution save, half damage on a success, 4d6 necrotic damage).

**Room A7 Final Encounter:**

This is where the **final conflict** will take place, with **Vornelith** waiting for the players. There are 12 sarcophagi lining the walls and in the center of the room. Two of the central sarcophagi resonate with **dark magical energy**, each near a bloodstained altar. On the north altar sits a single **severed eye**, while on the southern altar rests a **severed hand**—symbols of **Vecna**’s dark power.

At the center of the room stands a figure cloaked in shadow—Vornelith Blackthorn, his eyes gleaming with malicious intent as he raises a hand to greet you with a smile devoid of warmth. "You should not have come this far," he says, his voice cold and hollow.*

- **Blood Ghosts** x3 (accompanying Vornelith in battle)

- If the party disrupts the altars (by destroying the eye or hand), Vornelith will summon more **Blood Ghosts** to protect the room.

### **Room A10: Dungeon with Cells**

Room A10 has been converted into a small **dungeon**, with **five narrow cells** on the west and east walls. The air here is damp and oppressive, with the faint smell of mildew and rot permeating the space. Each cell is enclosed by **iron bars**, with a heavy iron door in the middle of each. Chains and manacles are bolted to the walls of the cells, some of them still swinging gently, as if recently disturbed. This room has clearly seen use in more recent times, likely for imprisoning captives or for darker purposes. The walls are cold stone, and the floors are covered with dirt and grime.

---

### **Read-Aloud Text for Room A10:**

>*You step into a dimly lit chamber, the stale air heavy with the scent of rot and mildew. Along both the west and east walls, five narrow cells stand, their rusted iron bars ominously enclosing what appears to have once been prisoners. Chains hang loosely from the stone walls, and in the farthest cell, you see fresh scratch marks across the floor, as though someone tried to claw their way to freedom. The silence is broken only by the occasional drip of water from the ceiling, landing in shallow puddles on the dirt-strewn floor.*

---

### **Features and Details in Room A10:**

- **Cells**: The five cells are each 5 ft. by 5 ft., designed to hold prisoners for extended periods. All of the doors are locked (DC 15 to pick the lock or DC 17 to break open the doors).
  - **Cell 1**: The iron door to this cell has deep scratch marks, and dried blood is smeared across the floor. A rusty iron **manacle** hangs from the wall, broken off at the chain.
  - **Cell 2**: Inside this cell, a **rotting blanket** is draped over a pile of straw. A faint magical aura lingers here (Detect Magic reveals remnants of **Divination** magic).
  - **Cell 3**: A pile of bones rests in the corner of this cell. A **Perception DC 15** reveals that the bones belong to a human, though there is evidence of **necromantic energy** that has reanimated these bones in the past. A **Medicine DC 13** check reveals that the bones have been gnawed on by small creatures, likely rats.
  - **Cell 4**: This cell contains the body of a long-dead prisoner, still shackled to the wall. The prisoner’s skeletal hand clutches a small **iron key** (which unlocks one of the other cells or the cabinet in A3). There is also a **note** on a crumpled piece of parchment.
  - **Cell 5**: This cell is completely empty, though the stone wall behind it is slightly out of alignment. A **DC 15 Investigation** reveals that this is a secret compartment where **illegal substances** or **contraband items** could have been hidden (currently empty).

---

### **Notable Interactions and Clues in A10:**

1. **The Crumpled Note (Cell 4)**: The note is addressed to someone named **"Arlan,"** who was imprisoned here. It details Arlan's desire to escape, but mentions strange dreams of **"a hand and an eye that watch over me."** The note mentions how Arlan feared a "dark sorcerer" who imprisoned him in this place and speaks of necromantic experiments.

2. **Arcane Residue (Cell 2)**: A faint residue of **Divination magic** lingers in the air here. It appears that someone used this room to spy on or observe something remotely. This could point to Vornelith or another necromancer experimenting on prisoners or keeping watch over them. This residue is weak and fading, but it could provide insight into the dark rituals occurring here.

3. **Secret Compartment (Cell 5)**: The out-of-alignment stones in the back of Cell 5 conceal a secret compartment, though the compartment has long been emptied. This area may have been used to store **forbidden items or artifacts**. A **Perception DC 15** or **Investigation DC 12** will reveal scratches on the floor near the wall, indicating that something heavy was once dragged away from this space.

---

### **Encounter in Room A10:**

- **Prisoner’s Ghost** (optional): One of the cells (either **Cell 4** or **Cell 3**) is haunted by a **restless spirit** of a former prisoner who was experimented on by Vornelith.
  
**Encounter Details:**
- **Restless Spirit**: The ghost of the prisoner emerges from the cell and attacks the players for disturbing its final resting place. This **Restless Spirit** is vengeful but confused, lashing out at the party.
  
**Combat Encounter:**
- **Ghost** (MM, CR 4): The ghost attempts to possess one of the party members or use its **Horrifying Visage** ability to instill fear.
  
**Loot from the Ghost (if defeated):**
- **Amulet of the Forgotten** (rare): Grants resistance to necrotic damage and advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

---

### **Final Room Layout (A10 Dungeon):**

- **Cells 1–5**: Each cell offers clues or remnants of what took place here.
- **Dungeon Feel**: This room conveys the darker side of the tomb, where the necromancers imprisoned people, used them for experimentation, or kept them hidden for their own purposes. Some may have been driven mad, experimented upon, or simply left to die.
- **Secret Clues**: Players can discover evidence of necromantic experiments and connections to Vecna. Clues about a larger conspiracy can also be hidden here to push the overarching narrative further.

With this dungeon section and its small cells, the players will be able to uncover more of the twisted activities that took place here and gather more evidence of necromantic activity as they push deeper into the crypt.
