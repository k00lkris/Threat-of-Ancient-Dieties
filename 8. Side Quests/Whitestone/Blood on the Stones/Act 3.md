### **Act 3: The Stakeout**

This is where the investigation finally comes to a head, as the players stake out the cemetery in the dead of night to catch the true culprits. They've gathered enough information to know something is happening, but now they'll finally witness the **blood ghosts** in action, which will lead them to the **Elara Windsail mausoleum** and the final confrontation with **Vornelith Blackthorn**.

---

### **Scene 1: Setting Up the Stakeout**

The players have gathered their clues and decided to watch over the cemetery for any suspicious activity. The stakeout should build tension, slowly leading up to the eerie appearance of the blood ghosts.

---

**Read-Aloud Text:**

>_The Greyfield Cemetery is eerily silent as the night deepens. Gravestones cast long shadows in the pale moonlight, and the only sounds are the distant rustling of leaves and the soft whistle of the wind. The chill in the air seems to sink deeper into your bones as you take up positions near the mausoleums and gravestones, watching and waiting for any sign of movement._

_Time drags on, and the stillness becomes almost unbearable. Just when you start to doubt whether anything will happen tonight, you notice something. Shadows moving—not just the shifting of the moonlight—but real, dark figures slipping silently between the gravestones._

---

### **Scene 2: The Blood Ghosts Appear**

The **blood ghosts** materialize and begin their ritual. The players should see the ghosts flicking blood onto the gravestones and mausoleums, with their movements appearing almost choreographed. Their attention will eventually focus on the **Elara Windsail mausoleum**, but this won’t happen immediately. At first, they’ll move between various gravestones, keeping the players in suspense.

---

**Read-Aloud Text (Blood Ghosts' First Appearance):**

>_From the shadows, they emerge. Pale, translucent figures, barely more than whispers in the night air. Their forms glide unnaturally between the gravestones, leaving no trace of their passage. But as they move, you see something strange—blood, fresh and bright, splashing across the gravestones in their wake. It's as if the blood is flicked from their spectral forms, leaving crimson marks on the stones._

_The ghosts seem to follow a pattern, moving with eerie precision, as though carrying out some dark and ancient ritual. They pass by several gravestones, lingering for a moment before moving on._

---

### **Skill Challenge: Avoiding Detection**

Before they engage the ghosts, the players may want to observe them without being seen or heard. This should be a chance for the players to use stealth, magic, or other skills to avoid detection while they watch the ghosts perform their ritual.

- **Stealth (DC 14):** The players must remain hidden as they watch the **blood ghosts**. If they succeed, the ghosts remain unaware of their presence.
- **Arcana or Religion (DC 16):** A player who studies the **blood ghosts** will realize that they are **necromantic constructs**, bound to perform a ritual that is building toward something. The blood being splashed on the gravestones is part of a larger process.
- **Insight (DC 15):** The players can intuit that the **blood ghosts** seem to be converging on something, as though they are being drawn toward a specific point in the cemetery.

---

### **Scene 3: The Focus on Elara Windsail’s Mausoleum**

After a few minutes of watching the **blood ghosts**, the players will notice that the spectral figures are drawn to one mausoleum more than any other—**Elara Windsail’s**. This will be the key clue that leads them to confront **Vornelith Blackthorn** in the final confrontation.

---

**Read-Aloud Text:**

>_The blood ghosts move silently through the cemetery, but something has changed. Their path is no longer random. Their movements are converging, drawing them closer to one mausoleum in particular. Elara Windsail’s name is etched into the stone, old and weathered by time. As the ghosts approach, they begin to flick more and more blood onto the stone, as if the ritual is nearing its climax._

_You feel the weight of dark magic in the air, thick and oppressive, as the blood ghosts circle the mausoleum in an unsettling display. It’s clear now—something is happening here, something tied to this place, this grave._

---

### **Combat Encounter: Blood Ghosts**

The players may decide to act now, interrupting the **blood ghosts’ ritual** before it can progress any further. This will lead to a combat encounter with several **blood ghosts**, but with a limited number so as not to overwhelm the party just yet. The real challenge will come in the final battle with **Vornelith Blackthorn** in the mausoleum itself.

#### **Blood Ghost Combat**

- **Number of Blood Ghosts:** 4 Blood Ghosts
- **Tactics:**
    - The **blood ghosts** will move in and out of gravestones, using cover and their spectral nature to remain difficult to pin down.
    - They will focus on **sapping** the players’ strength and health, using their **Life Drain** abilities to weaken the party before the final confrontation.
    - They may attempt to **flee** or **fade into the shadows** if the players prove too strong, but they will regroup inside the mausoleum if chased.

**Read-Aloud Text (Combat Begins):**

>_As you move to engage, the ghosts turn toward you with unnerving speed. Their translucent forms flicker in and out of sight, their faces twisted into silent, otherworldly expressions of malice. One by one, they drift toward you, their hands outstretched as if to drain the very life from your bodies._
