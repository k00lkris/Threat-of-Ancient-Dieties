### **Revised Act 1: The Call to Investigate**

---

### **Scene 1: Soren’s Plea for Help**

The party is preparing to leave Whitestone when **Captain Soren** approaches them, clearly concerned about the well-being of Whitestone’s citizens. With the lie about the Chupacabra planted in his mind, he is more desperate than ever to get to the bottom of the strange occurrences in **Greyfield**. He offers them both aid in their future journey and promises of great reward if they solve this mysterious threat.

---

**Read-Aloud Text:**

>*As you gather your things and prepare to depart Whitestone, the morning air is thick with mist, and the sound of your footsteps echoes off the cobblestone streets. Just as you turn toward the road leading out of town, you hear hurried steps approaching from behind. Turning around, you see Captain Soren, his armor clinking as he strides toward you with a grim look on his face.*
>
*"Please, wait,"* he calls out, stopping just short of you. *"I’ve heard you’re planning to leave Whitestone, but we need your help. It’s this damned Chupacabra—ever since you told the Paleguard, the people are in a panic. With the Chroma Conclave’s devastation still fresh in everyone’s mind, Whitestone can’t afford another threat. If this creature is as fierce as you say, it could tear through the town unnoticed, leaving bodies in its wake."*
>
*He looks at you with weary eyes.* *"I’ll be honest—none of us in the Paleguard know how to handle this, and we’ve run out of ideas. If you’re willing, Lord Percival and Lady Vex'ahlia would reward you handsomely for putting an end to this. We can even offer some assistance for your journey to Turst Fields once this is done."*

**Skill Challenge: Assessing the Paleguard**

- Players might want to **insight** whether the Paleguard know more than they’re letting on or are hiding something.
    - **Insight DC 13:** Captain Soren is genuinely concerned but frustrated that his guards can’t figure it out. He seems tired and maybe a little frightened by the strange circumstances.
    - **Insight DC 18:** There’s a sense of urgency because the Paleguard fear public outcry. If this isn’t solved soon, it could make them look incompetent.

**Options for Response:**

- The party may agree to investigate immediately, or if they press for more information, the Paleguard can mention the cryptic nature of the blood (they’ve had no leads and no idea what’s happening).
- Captain Soren will provide a map of the Greyfield and offer to escort them there.
---

### **Scene 2: Investigating the Greyfield Cemetery**

The party accepts Captain Soren's request (or at least agrees to investigate), and he personally leads them to **Greyfield Cemetery**. The tone here is to build tension, as the players already know their Chupacabra lie is false, but something sinister is indeed going on. They are faced with investigating the **graveyard**, with a chance of minor combat encounters to emphasize the danger.

---

**Read-Aloud Text:**

>*Captain Soren leads you through Whitestone’s twisting streets until the Greyfield Cemetery comes into view, shrouded in low-hanging mist. It’s an eerie sight—the stone markers of Whitestone’s fallen jut from the earth like skeletal fingers, their surfaces worn by time and weather. As you approach, the stillness of the air seems unnatural, as if the land itself is holding its breath.*
>
*Small splashes of **fresh blood** decorate the gravestones in random streaks and spots, like a macabre artist’s canvas. Though you know the story of the Chupacabra was false, this chilling sight confirms that something is indeed happening here.*
>
*Captain Soren halts at the entrance of the cemetery.* *"I’ll leave you to it,"* he says, his voice barely above a whisper. *"But be careful. Whatever’s doing this... it hasn’t been seen or heard by anyone. And no magic we’ve tried has been able to track it."*

---

### **Skill Challenges: Investigating the Graveyard**

This portion will involve the players actively searching the graveyard for clues. We’ll incorporate multiple skill checks here, along with a chance for combat with **minor threats** that roam the cemetery—likely to reinforce the feeling of dread and danger in the area.

**Key Investigation Points:**

1. **Blood Spatters**  
    - **Investigation DC 14:** The blood on the gravestones looks fresh but seems **randomly flung**, rather than from an actual injury. 
    - **Medicine DC 15:** The blood comes from **multiple sources**—some of it is human, but there are hints of **celestial or fiendish taint** in the mix.
    - **Arcana DC 16:** There are faint remnants of **necromantic energy** around the blood splatters, but it's too weak to trace to any direct source.

2. **Gravestone Markings**
    - **Perception DC 13:** One gravestone seems to have been **touched more frequently** by blood than others—the grave of **Elara Windsail**. A closer look reveals small, claw-like scratches around the stone.
    - **Religion DC 14:** The **Windsail family** was known for its connection to the **Raven Queen**, and their legacy in Whitestone ties to older magics.
    - **Insight DC 16:** The claw marks could be part of an ancient **necromantic ritual** designed to either **call or control spirits**.

3. **Unseen Watchers**
    - **Perception DC 17:** A faint rustling, as though someone or something is moving unseen through the mist.
    - **Arcana DC 15:** Detecting faint **invisible entities** moving between the graves, out of sight but definitely present.

**Clue Drop:** The party begins to realize this is no mere accident—someone or something is orchestrating a dark ritual. The celestial and fiendish traces suggest a broader scheme is in play, potentially tied to necromantic forces. The party may now decide to question the Paleguard or other key NPCs for more information.

### **Scene 3: Exploring the Cemetery for Additional Clues**

If the players want to dig deeper, they may choose to search the mausoleums, gravestones, or speak to witnesses nearby.

**Read-Aloud Text:**

>_The mausoleums of the Greyfield stand silent and imposing, their weathered stone facades pocked with age. A closer look reveals strange markings on some of the stones, faint scratches as if from claws or talons. The Paleguard watch uneasily as you move about, their nervous whispers hinting at the fear that lies beneath their professional exterior._

**Skill Challenge: Inspecting the Mausoleums or Markings**

- **History DC 13:** The Windsail family was known for their connection to the Raven Queen and have been important figures in Whitestone for centuries.
- **Arcana DC 17:** The scratches around the mausoleum’s entrance may be tied to an older, dormant necromantic ward designed to **awaken something beneath the graves**.

### **Combat Encounter: Spectral Hands**

While the players are investigating, they may trigger a small **combat encounter** to emphasize the threat. The entities causing the disturbances are **Spectral Hands**, disembodied hands from necromantic rituals that are disturbing the graveyard. They could rise from beneath the gravestones or from cracks in the mausoleums.

---

**Read-Aloud Text:**

>*As you lean in to inspect a gravestone, you feel a cold chill creep up your spine. Before you can react, the earth around the grave begins to tremble. Suddenly, **disembodied hands**, pale and ghostly, claw their way up through the soil, grasping at the air and reaching for you with a hunger that chills your bones.*

---

### **Combat Stats for Spectral Hands:**

- **Armor Class:** 12
- **Hit Points:** 16
- **Speed:** 20 ft.
- **Attack:** Grasping Claw +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., 1d6+2 necrotic damage.
- **Special Abilities:**
  - **Life Drain (Recharge 5-6):** The spectral hand grabs hold of a creature. The target must succeed on a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or take 2d6 necrotic damage and have their hit point maximum reduced by the same amount until they complete a long rest.
  - **Ghostly Appearance:** The hands are **resistant to non-magical damage**.

---

### **Clue Unlocked from Combat:**

- **Arcana/Insight DC 15 (After Defeating the Spectral Hands):** The **Spectral Hands** were not acting independently—they were summoned by someone or something to create a distraction and further unnerve those investigating the cemetery. There may be **someone orchestrating** these attacks from behind the scenes, and **necromantic** energy is present.

---

### **Combat Encounter: Pale Wraith**

While the party investigates, their presence stirs an ancient **Pale Wraith**, a spirit who was summoned by the lingering **necromantic magic**. The **wraith** attacks to protect the ritual site, though it is only a minor guardian tied to the greater ritual.

---

**Read-Aloud Text:**

_As you study the blood and gravestones, the air grows colder, the temperature plummeting unnaturally. The mist thickens, swirling with a malevolent presence. Suddenly, the cold coalesces into a figure—thin, tall, and wreathed in pale, translucent robes. Its face is hidden beneath a cowl, but you feel its eyes on you, filled with malice and hatred. Without a word, it glides toward you, its skeletal hands outstretched, hungry for your life force._

---

**Combat Stats for Pale Wraith:**

- **Armor Class:** 13
- **Hit Points:** 45
- **Speed:** 0 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)
- **Attack:** Life Drain +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., 2d8+3 necrotic damage.
- **Special Abilities:**
    - **Ethereal Jaunt (Recharge 5-6):** The Pale Wraith can shift onto the **Ethereal Plane** as a bonus action, allowing it to avoid damage and pass through solid objects.
    - **Life Drain (Recharge 5-6):** If the wraith hits a creature with its attack, the creature must make a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have its **hit point maximum** reduced by an amount equal to the damage taken.


### **Act 1 Conclusion:**

At the end of Act 1, the players will have uncovered enough information to suspect that the blood and spectral disturbances are tied to a **necromantic ritual**, and all signs point to the **Elara Windsail Mausoleum**. This sets the stage for them to delve deeper in **Act 2**, where they can question key NPCs about the Windsail family and the dark magic at play.
