#### **1. Elspeth Thorne (A Local Apothecary)**

**Location:** **Elspeth’s Apothecary**, near the Greyfield.

**Read-Aloud Text:**

>*The smell of herbs and dried flowers fills the air as you step into Elspeth’s Apothecary. Elspeth Thorne, a wizened old woman with sharp eyes and a constant frown, watches you from behind the counter. "I’ve heard you’re investigating the strange happenings in Greyfield," she says, her voice raspy but firm. "People think it’s spirits or magic, but I’m not so sure."*

**What Elspeth Knows:**

- Elspeth believes the blood and disturbances are the work of a **gang of thieves** or vandals. She doesn’t buy into the idea of spirits or magic and insists it’s the work of **living people** trying to stir up trouble.
- She mentions that a group of **ruffians** were spotted near the Greyfield several weeks ago, and she wonders if they’re behind the vandalism. She can give the players directions to a **hideout** near the outskirts of Whitestone where these ruffians were last seen.
  
**Skill Checks:**

- **Insight DC 12:** Elspeth truly doesn’t believe the disturbances are magical and is convinced it’s the work of troublemakers. She isn’t hiding anything, but her stubbornness might mislead the players into thinking this is a dead end.
- **Persuasion DC 14:** If convinced, Elspeth will mention that a **strange figure** was seen near the cemetery on the nights of the disturbances. She doesn’t know much more but suggests checking with **Old Man Mathis**, who lives nearby.

**False Lead or Misdirection:**

- The **gang of ruffians** is a false lead. If the players investigate, they will find a group of petty criminals, but none of them are involved in the necromantic ritual. This misdirection should take time, and while the players may not face a major combat encounter here, they’ll feel like they’ve hit a dead end.

**False Lead or Misdirection:**

#### **3. Old Man Mathis (The Hermit)**

Old Man Mathis can still serve as a key source of misinformation and cryptic clues. His mention of **pale figures** and **shadows** will unsettle the players but mislead them into thinking the threat is more supernatural than it actually is.

**Location:** His shack near the Greyfield.

**Read-Aloud Text:**

**What Mathis Knows:**

- Mathis believes the **spirits of the dead** are **restless** and that they are trying to **reach out** from beneath the graves. He talks about **shadows** moving between the gravestones at night and whispers in a language he doesn’t understand.
- He mentions seeing **pale figures** gliding through the cemetery on the nights the blood appeared, but he can’t describe them clearly.
- Mathis is convinced that **something is buried** beneath the cemetery, trying to **wake up**, but he’s too vague to give specific details.

**Skill Checks:**

- **Insight DC 12:** Mathis is clearly paranoid, but he believes what he’s saying. His cryptic warnings about **spirits** and **shadows** are genuine, but his descriptions are too scattered to be fully reliable.
- **Persuasion DC 14:** If the players push him, Mathis will mention that the **Elara Windsail mausoleum** has been **particularly active** at night, but he’s too afraid to investigate further.

**Misdirection:**

- Mathis will make the players think that the **pale figures** are **spirits or ghosts**, leading them to believe the threat is more supernatural than it is. His mention of **restless spirits** could make them think there’s a haunting or curse at work, leading them to focus on the **wrong kinds of enemies** (e.g., ghosts or wraiths).
