## Profile

A trained spellcaster employed for diversion, concealment, and escape routes. Veilcasters' arcane skills help explain why the Myriad can protect its networks; **this does not establish that magic was used to forge the letters**.

## Tactics

- Favors fog, disguise, and retreat over lethal force.
- Uses hypnotic pattern only when the situation justifies dangerous crowd control; bystanders may be affected.
- Preserves spell slots if escape or negotiation is feasible.
- Can be interrogated if captured; has no automatic access to every Myriad secret.
