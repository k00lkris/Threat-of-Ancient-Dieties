
### **Harpy Matriarch**
*Medium monstrosity, chaotic evil*

---

**Armor Class**: 14 (natural armor)  
**Hit Points**: 65 (10d8 + 20)  
**Speed**: 20 ft., fly 40 ft.

---

**STR** | **DEX** | **CON** | **INT** | **WIS** | **CHA**
--- | --- | --- | --- | --- | ---
12 (+1) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4)

---

**Saving Throws**: Dex +6, Cha +7  
**Skills**: Deception +7, Persuasion +7, Perception +4  
**Damage Resistances**: Thunder  
**Condition Immunities**: Charmed  
**Senses**: Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14  
**Languages**: Common, Elvish, Sylvan  
**Challenge**: 5 (1,800 XP)

---

### **Traits**

**Luring Song**. The Harpy Matriarch sings a magical melody. Every humanoid and giant within 300 feet of the matriarch that can hear the song must succeed on a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw** or be charmed until the song ends. The harpy matriarch must take a bonus action on its subsequent turns to continue singing. The matriarch can stop singing at any time. The song ends if the matriarch is incapacitated.

While charmed by the matriarch, a target is incapacitated and ignores the songs of other harpies. If the charmed target is more than 5 feet away from the matriarch, the target must move on its turn toward the matriarch by the most direct route. It doesn’t avoid opportunity attacks but before moving into damaging terrain, such as lava or a pit, a target can repeat the saving throw. A target can also repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns. If the saving throw is successful, the effect ends on it.

A target that successfully saves is immune to this matriarch’s song for the next 24 hours.

---

**Innate Spellcasting**. The Harpy Matriarch’s innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 15). The matriarch can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- At will: *Thaumaturgy*  
- 2/day each: *Hold Person, Mirror Image, Silence*  
- 1/day: *Major Image, Suggestion*

---

**Keen Hearing**. The Harpy Matriarch has advantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks that rely on hearing.

---

**Commanding Presence**. Allies within 30 feet of the Harpy Matriarch have advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

---

### **Actions**

**Multiattack**. The Harpy Matriarch makes two attacks: one with her claws and one with her club.

**Claws**. *Melee Weapon Attack*: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit*: 8 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage.

**Club**. *Melee Weapon Attack*: +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.  
*Hit*: 7 (1d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

---

**Luring Song (Recharge 5–6)**. The Harpy Matriarch sings a magical melody to lure creatures. This attack functions as described above in the **Luring Song** trait, and affects any humanoid or giant within 300 feet who can hear it.

---

### **Reactions**

**Parry**. The Harpy Matriarch adds 2 to its AC against one melee attack that would hit it. To do so, the harpy must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

---

### **Legendary Actions (Optional for Boss Encounters)**

The Harpy Matriarch can take 2 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature’s turn. The matriarch regains spent legendary actions at the start of its turn.

1. **Claw Attack**: The matriarch makes one claw attack.
2. **Disengage**: The matriarch moves up to half its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
3. **Lure (Costs 2 actions)**: The matriarch forces a creature charmed by its Luring Song to move 10 feet closer to it.

---

### **Tactics**

- The Harpy Matriarch will typically start combat by casting **Mirror Image** to create duplicates, making it harder for the players to land attacks. 
- She will use **Hold Person** to lock down any heavy hitters or spellcasters, isolating them for her minions.
- Her **Luring Song** will target ranged attackers or spellcasters, forcing them to walk into dangerous positions, ideally near the cliff edge.
- The matriarch uses her **Parry** reaction to block melee attacks and will retreat into the air if heavily injured, using her flight and ranged spell abilities to keep her enemies at bay.

---

**Challenge Notes**:

The Harpy Matriarch is designed to be a **challenging CR 5** encounter for a party of **5 level 5 adventurers**. She combines powerful crowd control spells, a deadly song ability, and tactical intelligence to keep the players on their toes.
