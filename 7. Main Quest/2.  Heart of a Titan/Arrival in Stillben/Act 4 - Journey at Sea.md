### **Act 4: The Journey at Sea**

With the party magically transported to **Stillben**, their next step is to secure passage to the **Shattered Isles**, where the Titan’s Heart is rumored to be hidden. The journey at sea will take time, giving room for **roleplay, skill challenges, and random encounters** that make the voyage dynamic and unpredictable.

---

## **Part 1: Life at Sea (Skill Challenges & Duties)**

Depending on the **ship and crew** the players hire, they may be required (or volunteered) to **help run the ship**. Below are common **duties at sea**, the skill checks required, and the **consequences of failure**.

### **1. Navigating the Waters** (_Wisdom (Survival) DC 14_)

📌 _Task:_ Chart the correct course through open waters, avoiding reefs, shifting currents, and dangerous weather.  
✅ _Success:_ The ship stays on course, and the journey is uneventful.  
❌ _Failure:_ The ship veers slightly off course, adding **1d6+2 additional days** to the journey and increasing the chances of running into a **random encounter**.

### **2. Hoisting the Sails & Managing the Rigging** (_Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 12_)

📌 _Task:_ Climb the rigging, secure sails, and adjust ropes during strong winds.  
✅ _Success:_ The ship makes good time.  
❌ _Failure:_ The crew loses control of a sail, causing a minor **equipment failure** (advancing a random encounter or taking **1d4 days longer**).

### **3. Rowing in Dead Winds** (_Strength (Athletics) DC 15_)

📌 _Task:_ When the ship enters an area of dead wind, the crew must row to make progress.  
✅ _Success:_ Progress is slow but steady.  
❌ _Failure:_ The ship barely moves for **1d4 hours**, increasing chances of an encounter.

### **4. Cooking for the Crew** (_Wisdom (Survival) or Intelligence (Cook’s Utensils) DC 13_)

📌 _Task:_ Prepare a meal that boosts crew morale.  
✅ _Success:_ The crew is satisfied, and the party earns favor with them.  
❌ _Failure:_ The meal is barely edible, causing morale to drop (**Disadvantage on crew-related Persuasion checks for 24 hours**).

### **5. Keeping Watch for Trouble** (_Wisdom (Perception) DC 14_)

📌 _Task:_ Scan the horizon for **hostile ships, sea creatures, or approaching storms**.  
✅ _Success:_ The party spots trouble early, giving them **advantage on initiative or preparation**.  
❌ _Failure:_ They fail to see **an incoming threat**, causing an ambush or surprise round.

### **6. Repairs & Maintenance** (_Intelligence (Tinker’s Tools) or Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) DC 14_)

📌 _Task:_ Fix a leak, patch a sail, or repair rigging before it worsens.  
✅ _Success:_ The ship remains in top condition.  
❌ _Failure:_ The damage worsens, **reducing speed** or **forcing an unscheduled stop** at a nearby island.

### **7. Gambling with the Crew** (_Charisma (Deception) or Intelligence (Gaming Set) DC 12_)

📌 _Task:_ Engage in dice or card games with the sailors for fun or profit.  
✅ _Success:_ The player wins **2d20 gold** and earns the respect of the crew.  
❌ _Failure:_ They lose **2d20 gold** and may offend the wrong person (**leading to a confrontation**).

## **Part 2: Random Encounters at Sea**

Below are a mix of **environmental, combat, and social encounters** the players may experience. You can roll a **1d10** or **choose** encounters that fit the tone.

### 🌊 **Environmental Encounters**

🔹 **1-2: Sudden Storm** - Black clouds roll in, bringing **high winds, violent waves, and torrential rain**. The party must make **Dexterity (Acrobatics) DC 15** to stay balanced and **Strength (Athletics) DC 14** to secure the ship. Failure may cause **crew members to go overboard**.  
🔹 **3: Dead Waters** - The ship is trapped in an area of the ocean where **no wind blows**. The crew must **row** to escape, or the ship remains motionless for **1d6+1 days**.  
🔹 **4: Strange Fog** - An eerie **green mist** rolls in, obscuring vision and causing whispers to echo from the water. If the players do not find a way to dispel the mist, they may **sail directly into a hidden danger**.

### ⚔️ **Combat Encounters**


### 🦑 **1. Swarm of Lesser Krakens (CR 7)**

- **Encounter Setup:** A group of three **Lesser Krakens** (baby krakens) attack the ship, using their **tentacles** to pull sailors into the water.
- **Mechanics:**
    - Each kraken has **75 HP, AC 14**, and **tentacle attacks** (2d6+4 bludgeoning, DC 13 Strength save to avoid being grappled).
    - They retreat if reduced to half HP.
    - Players can use **ballistae, fire, or magic** to fight them off.

---

### 🦈 **2. Blood Frenzy Sharks (CR 6)**

- **Encounter Setup:** The ship **passes through a wreckage**, and **blood in the water** attracts a school of **Blood Frenzy Sharks**. The crew **panics** as the sharks attempt to **ram the hull** and **pull sailors overboard**.
- **Mechanics:**
    - **5 Large Blood Frenzy Sharks** (AC 12, 55 HP).
    - They have **advantage on attacks** against creatures missing HP.
    - Players must **secure loose cargo**, **heal wounded crew**, and **fight them off before they break the hull**.

---

### 🐙 **3. The Drowning Horror (CR 8)**

- **Encounter Setup:** A **shadowy mass** lurks beneath the ship, **dragging sailors into the dark** with **phantasmal tendrils**. It **feeds on fear** and **drags victims below** before vanishing.
- **Mechanics:**
    - The **Drowning Horror** attacks using **shadowy tentacles** (DC 14 Strength save or be pulled into the water).
    - Players must use **light, radiant damage, or divine magic** to **expose** it and **banish it**.
    - It is not a fight to the death; it's an **exorcism-style survival battle**.

---

### 🦀 **4. Giant Brine Crabs (CR 7)**

- **Encounter Setup:** As the ship sails over **shallow reefs**, a **massive crab colony** mistakes the ship for **food** and climbs aboard.
- **Mechanics:**
    - **3 Giant Brine Crabs** (AC 16, 80 HP).
    - They have **crushing claws** (2d10+3) and can **throw debris** from the deck.
    - If players break their **hard shells (AC 18 but breakable with blunt force)**, their **AC drops to 12**.
    - The battle **ends if they are lured back into the water with food or magic**.

---

### 🏴‍☠️ **5. Smugglers on the Open Sea (CR 6-7)**

- **Encounter Setup:** A **smuggler ship** approaches under the pretense of trade but **attacks suddenly**.
- **Mechanics:**
    - **Captain Garrik Hallow & 6 Smugglers** (AC 13, 45 HP each).
    - They use **poisoned crossbows & alchemist’s fire**.
    - Players can **fight or negotiate**—but if the smugglers lose, they reveal **clues about hidden treasure** nearby.

### 🏴‍☠️ **Social & Mystery Encounters**

🔹 **8: A Castaway** - The crew spots **a lone figure waving from a tiny island**. The castaway is **either a stranded sailor, a criminal in exile, or a creature in disguise**.  
🔹 **9: Hidden Treasure Map** - A sailor offers to sell the party **an old, faded treasure map**. If the players follow it, they might find **valuable treasure** or **a deadly trap**.  
🔹 **10: A Dying Sailor’s Warning** - A crew member falls ill and, in their fevered state, **whispers cryptic warnings** about the Shattered Isles: _“They are still there… waiting beneath the stone…”_

### **Part 3: Arrival at the Shattered Isles**

When the players finally reach their destination, they see **a cluster of jagged islands rising from the ocean, shrouded in mist**.

📌 _Next Step:_

- The ship **anchors offshore**, as the **waters are too treacherous** for large vessels.
- The party must either **row ashore**, **swim**, or **find another means of landing**.
- Strange **ruins** and **carved stone formations** suggest that something **ancient** lies buried beneath the islands.

---

### **Final Notes**

This setup allows for **engagement with ship life, unique roleplay opportunities, and a sense of peril while at sea**. It also ensures **the players don’t just “skip” through the journey** but rather experience the tension and dangers of traveling **to a lost and dangerous place**.
