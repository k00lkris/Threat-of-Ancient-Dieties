### **Act 3: Arrival in Stillben**

With the **Transport via Plants** spell, the party will step through the Sun Tree and emerge in Stillben, a **murky, humid port city nestled at the edge of the K’Tawl Swamp.** Unlike the crisp mountain air of Whitestone, they are immediately greeted with the **scent of brackish water, the buzz of insects, and the humid weight of the swampy climate.**

#### **Arrival at the Stillben Transport Point**

Keyleth would have chosen a tree within Stillben’s **central market district**, a relatively safe and populated area where travelers wouldn't be immediately ambushed. The moment the players step through, they’ll feel the shift in the air—**hot, damp, and heavy**, with the distant sound of creaking docks, merchant bartering, and swamp creatures croaking in the distance.

### **What the Players Need to Do in Stillben**

- **Find a ship to charter passage to the Shattered Isles.**
- **Resupply and prepare for an extended voyage.**
- **Gather information about the Shattered Isles and potential dangers.**
- **Potentially interact with factions, black-market dealers, or information brokers.**

---

### **Exploring Stillben (Key Locations & Encounters)**

Stillben is a **dangerous, corruption-ridden city**, known for its **crime, smugglers, and mercenaries**. The players have a few options on how to approach securing a ship.

#### **1. The Docks & Ship Charters**

- The **Docks** are where most ships are moored.
- The players will need to negotiate with **captains, merchants, or criminals** to arrange passage.
- The **price of a voyage** will depend on how well they can haggle, their reputation, and whether they’re willing to take risks with shadier captains.

**Skill Challenges for Negotiation:**

- **Persuasion DC 14:** Secure a fair price and a competent, neutral captain.
- **Intimidation DC 16:** Coerce a smuggler into lowering their price.
- **Deception DC 15:** Convince a ship crew that they have high-value goods or are important envoys, gaining a discount.
- **Investigation DC 15:** Find rumors of a reliable ship, rather than a scammer or pirate.

---

#### **2. The Mudtop Ward (Black Market & Smugglers)**

The **Mudtop Ward** is **Stillben’s seedy underbelly**, known for crime and corruption. Here, the players can:

- **Find illegal ship captains** who operate outside the law.
- **Trade on the black market** for illicit goods or information.
- **Run into trouble** if they aren’t careful.

**Encounters in the Mudtop Ward:**

- **A thief tries to pickpocket one of the players.**
- **A shady informant offers “secrets about the Shattered Isles” for a steep price.**
- **A fight breaks out between two rival smuggling crews.**

---

#### **3. The Swamp’s Shadow (Tavern & Gathering Info)**

A **notorious tavern** where **sailors, pirates, and bounty hunters** gather.

- This is where the party can pick up **rumors about the Shattered Isles** and potential ship captains.
- The **drunken sailors** can provide useful information—if the players buy them drinks.

**Rumors the Players Might Hear:**

- “A ghost ship was seen near the Shattered Isles… full of **undead sailors** with glowing eyes.”
- “A crew went missing after anchoring near the Isles. **Only one survivor** came back, and he’s raving mad.”
- “There’s a **sea monster** out there that drags ships under.”
