### **Act 2: Reporting Back & Departure to Stillben** _(Revised Version)_

---

### **Scene 1: Reporting to Captain Soren & the de Rolos**

As the party emerges from the crypts, **Captain Soren** and his Paleguard await them at the Greyfield gates. A few guards whisper among themselves, eyes darting between the party’s battered state and the ancient mausoleums behind them.

#### **Read-Aloud Text:**

_"You return from the depths of the crypts, the weight of battle still clinging to you like the dust of the tombs. Captain Soren, his polished armor now dulled with worry, steps forward, searching your faces for answers. His expression is a mixture of exhaustion and hope."_

_"It’s done, then?"_ he asks, voice low but steady. _"What was behind all of this?"_

#### **Key Notes:**

- **Soren listens carefully** as the party recounts their findings about **Vornelith Blackthorn** and the necromantic ritual.
- If the **Vecna amulet** or **necromantic writings** are presented, Soren **visibly pales**, recognizing the danger. He immediately insists that this needs to be taken to **Percival and Vex’ahlia**.
- The **Paleguard reward** is given in full (gold or magical compensation).
- The **Chupacabra posters** are still hilariously displayed across town, adding comedic contrast to the grim investigation.
- Soren then informs them that **Keyleth has already arrived in Whitestone**, as he had made a **promise** to the party for magical transport.

---

### **Scene 2: The de Rolos' Response**

Back at **Castle Whitestone**, the party is brought before **Percival and Vex’ahlia de Rolo** in their private study. Maps, documents, and arcane tomes are spread across the desk—evidence of their ongoing battle against **Lord Hargraves and the Annunaki cult**.

#### **Read-Aloud Text:**

_"Percival’s gloved fingers tap against the edge of the desk as he listens to your report, his sharp mind already dissecting the implications. Across from him, Vex’ahlia leans back in her chair, arms crossed, one boot resting against the table’s edge as she watches you with keen, golden eyes."_

_"A necromantic cult beneath our own graveyard,"_ Percy mutters, pinching the bridge of his nose. _"Of course."_

_"More filth clawing their way out of the past,"_ Vex adds. _"But if this was only a piece of something bigger, we don’t have time to waste."_ She leans forward. _"You need to go. Stillben’s your next stop, yes?"_

They **don’t delay** the party further. **Keyleth is already waiting for them** at the **Sun Tree**, as she has used it before to teleport in and out of Whitestone.

---

### **Scene 3: Meeting Keyleth & The Sun Tree**

The Sun Tree looms over Whitestone’s courtyard, its radiant golden leaves shimmering even in the dimming afternoon light. Standing at its base, one hand pressed to the bark, is **Keyleth, the Voice of the Tempest**. The **archdruid of the Air Ashari**—now a legend in her own right—whispers softly to the tree in **Druidic**, her fingers tracing delicate patterns over its gnarled surface.

#### **Read-Aloud Text:**

_"The Sun Tree stands tall and eternal, its golden leaves shifting slightly in a breeze that doesn’t quite reach you. At its base stands a tall red-haired woman, a crown of antlers woven into her braided locks. Her hand rests against the bark, and her lips move in quiet conversation—not to the guards, not to you, but to the tree itself."_

_"Ah, come on, buddy,"_ Keyleth murmurs, exasperated. _"I know it’s been a while, but don’t be difficult, okay? Just a quick favor?"_

At first, it seems like she’s speaking to no one—until Celestriel, instinctively attuning to nature, **hears the Sun Tree’s voice**.

#### **If Celestriel Uses Speak with Plants:**

The **Sun Tree’s voice** echoes in her mind—not a regal, ancient entity, but a **chill, laid-back surfer dude** with a distinctly **Southern California** drawl.

_"Whoa-ho, little breezy girl! What’s up, Celestriel? You hearin’ me? No way, man, that’s wild. You’re like, totally tapping into my vibe, dude."_

_"Listen, Kiki here’s askin’ me to open up a shortcut for you guys, but man, like, nobody ever asks how **I’m** doing, you know? Like, I’m just vibing here in the wind, spreading the shade, listening to people’s drama. It’s all good, but like, maybe next time you swing by, bring me a lil’ tree snack? Some mulch? Maybe a hug?"_

Keyleth, completely unaware of what **Celestriel hears**, sighs. _"I swear, sometimes I think this tree is screwing with me."_

---

### **Scene 4: Transport to Stillben**

After the Sun Tree agrees to open a passage, **Keyleth presses her palm against the bark**, and the **center of the tree splits open**, revealing a swirling **portal of green and gold energy**.

#### **Read-Aloud Text:**

_"Keyleth steps back, giving a satisfied nod as the bark twists and shifts, revealing a swirling gateway of golden-green light. The scent of damp earth and moss drifts through the air, carrying the unmistakable humidity of K’Tawl Swamp beyond."_

_"Alright, it’s a straight shot to Stillben. Shouldn’t take more than a few seconds,"_ Keyleth says. _"Just, uh... hold your breath if you don’t like the smell of swamp air."_

With one last farewell to Whitestone, the **party steps forward**, crossing through the portal and into **Stillben—where their next adventure begins.**
