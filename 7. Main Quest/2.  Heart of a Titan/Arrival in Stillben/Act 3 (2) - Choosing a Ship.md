### **Ship Captains of Stillben**

The players will have several options for securing passage to the **Shattered Isles**. Each captain offers a different type of voyage—some safer, some riskier, and others with hidden dangers.

---

### **1. Captain Dorian "The Tidehawk" Maldrake** (Reliable & Expensive)

- **Ship:** _The Azure Gale_ (A sleek, fast sailing ship, built for speed and maneuverability)
- **Background:** A **former naval officer** turned **independent merchant captain**, Maldrake is **a strict but fair leader** who follows the law. He only takes on reputable passengers and is **highly respected among sailors**.
- **Personality:** Professional, disciplined, and unwaveringly **honest**. Prefers efficiency over small talk.
- **Voyage Cost:** **200 gp per person** (Safe and direct route, includes food & lodging)
- **Conditions:**
    - **Won’t smuggle goods or illegal cargo.**
    - **Refuses to transport criminals or those with a bad reputation.**
    - Will only take the **most direct and safest route**, avoiding riskier waters.
- **Pros:**
    - **The safest option.**
    - **Well-stocked ship with expert navigators.**
    - **Loyal and disciplined crew.**
- **Cons:**
    - **Very expensive.**
    - **Not flexible on terms.**
    - **No tolerance for shady dealings or secrets.**

---

### **2. Captain Elira “The Viper” Thorne** (Smuggler & Risky)

- **Ship:** _The Whisper Tide_ (A **modified sloop**, small and quick, built for evading patrols)
- **Background:** A **notorious smuggler and information broker**. Known for getting people where they need to go—**for a price**. Rumors say she once **betrayed the Clasp** and lived to tell the tale.
- **Personality:** Charming, **cunning, and highly unpredictable**. Enjoys making people sweat before striking a deal.
- **Voyage Cost:** **50 gp per person** (Riskier route, might involve deception or combat)
- **Conditions:**
    - **Won’t ask questions if the players don’t ask questions.**
    - Will **hide cargo or people** to avoid detection, for a price.
    - Might take **side jobs** along the way if the deal is good enough.
- **Pros:**
    - **Cheap and fast.**
    - **Knows secret routes.**
    - **Has connections in the criminal underworld.**
- **Cons:**
    - **Smuggling risks** (might be intercepted by authorities).
    - **Possible betrayal** (she’s loyal to money, not people).
    - **Secret agenda?** (She might be working for a third party).

---

### **3. Captain Garrik “Storm-Eye” Renshaw** (Superstitious but Experienced)

- **Ship:** _The Gilded Leviathan_ (A sturdy merchant vessel, enchanted against storms)
- **Background:** A **seasoned sailor and storm chaser**, Garrik is well-known for his **deep respect for the sea** and **paranoia about curses**. He **refuses to set sail without performing rituals to appease the ocean’s spirits.**
- **Personality:** Gruff but wise, with a **deep respect for tradition**. Has **strange superstitions** and requires **his crew to follow odd rituals**.
- **Voyage Cost:** **120 gp per person** (Moderate risk, but with strong magical protection)
- **Conditions:**
    - **Demands passengers participate in superstitious rituals before sailing.**
    - **Refuses to transport anything he believes is cursed.**
    - **If omens are bad, he will delay the voyage—no exceptions.**
- **Pros:**
    - **Highly experienced navigator.**
    - **Ship has magical protections against storms and sea creatures.**
    - **Crew is loyal and battle-ready.**
- **Cons:**
    - **Prone to paranoia and delays.**
    - **Might refuse passage if he believes the players are “unlucky.”**
    - **Slightly more expensive.**

---

### **4. Captain Luthar "The Grave Tide" Voss** (Pirate & Extremely Dangerous)

- **Ship:** _The Widow’s Fang_ (A **captured pirate brig**, heavily armed but crewed by cutthroats)
- **Background:** A **brutal pirate captain**, Luthar Voss once sailed under the feared **Crimson Abyss**, Hurk’s former pirate crew. He now commands **a rogue band of ex-pirates**, taking on mercenary jobs in Stillben.
- **Personality:** **Arrogant, ruthless, and utterly merciless.** He loves **the thrill of battle** and has **no qualms about double-crossing passengers**.
- **Voyage Cost:** **30 gp per person** (Extremely high risk, potential combat)
- **Conditions:**
    - **Expect betrayals and backstabbing.**
    - **Will turn on the players if he thinks they’re worth more as hostages.**
    - **Has enemies in Stillben and the open seas.**
- **Pros:**
    - **By far the cheapest option.**
    - **Ship is well-armed with ballistae and cannons.**
    - **Will fight any threats head-on.**
- **Cons:**
    - **Extremely dangerous to trust.**
    - **Might attract bounty hunters or naval patrols.**
    - **Could turn into a fight-for-your-life scenario.**

---

### **5. Captain Solenna "Silent Gale" Marrowind** (Mysterious & Enigmatic)

- **Ship:** _The Veilborn_ (A **ghostly, ethereal ship**, rumored to sail between worlds)
- **Background:** Solenna is **a sea-witch** who commands a ship **that should not exist**. Sailors whisper that **she is neither fully alive nor dead**, and that she has **seen things beyond the material plane.**
- **Personality:** **Calm, eerie, and detached from mortal concerns.** Speaks in **cryptic riddles** and **never raises her voice**.
- **Voyage Cost:** **??? gp** (She **doesn’t take gold**—her price is something else…)
- **Conditions:**
    - **She will only accept the players if they agree to “owe her a favor.”**
    - **Strange things happen aboard her ship—ghostly whispers, shifting realities.**
    - **Nobody who sails with her ever remembers the full journey.**
- **Pros:**
    - **Bypasses physical dangers—no naval patrols, no pirate attacks.**
    - **Can take them directly to the Shattered Isles in half the time.**
    - **May offer hidden knowledge or strange boons.**
- **Cons:**
    - **The players must promise her something… and she will collect.**
    - **Possible permanent side effects from the journey.**
    - **Who—or what—is she really?**

---

### **Choosing Their Captain**

Each **captain** offers a **unique sailing experience**, with varying degrees of risk, reward, and potential **side adventures**.

- If they want **safety and professionalism**, Captain Maldrake is the best choice.
- If they want **speed and secrecy**, Captain Elira Thorne can smuggle them in.
- If they want **an experienced but odd captain**, Garrik Renshaw provides reliable transport.
- If they want **the thrill of danger**, Captain Luthar Voss will take them there… for a price.
- If they want **a surreal, supernatural journey**, Captain Solenna Marrowind offers something beyond the material world.
