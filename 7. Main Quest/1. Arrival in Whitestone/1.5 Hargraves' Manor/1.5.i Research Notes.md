### **Hargraves’ Research Notes on the Annunaki**

---

**Date:** 3rd of Duscar, Year 843  
**Subject:** The Annunaki – Truth Beyond Myth?

I have long heard whispers of the **Annunaki**, their existence largely dismissed by historians and scholars as myth or legend. However, I remain convinced that they were not mere figures of stories told to children. They were **real**, far more powerful than even the most ambitious mortal minds can comprehend. There are too many similarities between the old texts—too many signs that point to their existence. I must delve deeper into this, peel back the layers of history, and uncover the truth.

The **Annunaki**, as it stands, were born from the union of **humans** and **celestial beings**, the so-called children of gods. If this is true, they are the perfect beings—**divine**, untainted by the fallibilities of mortals. They must return, for they are the key to rebuilding our broken world.

---

**Date:** 16th of Horisal, Year 843  
**Subject:** The Forgotten Age

I have uncovered ancient scrolls that describe a time when the Annunaki **walked among mortals**. They were seen as kings, rulers, and benevolent leaders. They brought forth an era of **unmatched prosperity and peace**, a **Golden Age** of civilization. But something went wrong… they were betrayed, struck down by the very mortals they sought to rule. Those few Annunaki who survived vanished from history, either sealed away or destroyed. Their influence was lost, and the world spiraled into chaos.

Perhaps it is no coincidence that in their absence, we have seen the rise of great terrors: the **Chroma Conclave**, the **Whispered One**. Humanity alone is flawed—incapable of maintaining order. The Annunaki must return to save us from ourselves.

---

**Date:** 27th of Misuthar, Year 843  
**Subject:** Resurrection Ritual

It seems there is a way to bring them back—a ritual, long forgotten, known only to those who walked the path of the Annunaki. I have obtained fragments of this ritual through ancient texts and whispers from those loyal to my cause. However, the process is **delicate** and requires certain **artifacts**—relics of unimaginable power, tied to both life and death. Without these, the Annunaki cannot be fully restored. Their power must be channeled through these items, drawing upon the essence of the divine and necromantic.

I have begun gathering the pieces, but time is not on my side. I sense that others—perhaps even the gods themselves—seek to stop me.

---

**Date:** 14th of Unndilar, Year 843  
**Subject:** Artifacts of Resurrection

The resurrection ritual requires a set of **six** key artifacts, each imbued with incredible power. These artifacts must be gathered before the ritual can be performed. I have cataloged the necessary components:

1. **The Titan's Heart** – The heart of a fallen Titan, a being once feared by both gods and mortals. The heart holds the raw essence of creation and destruction. If properly harnessed, it will provide the foundation for the Annunaki’s rebirth.
   
2. **A Celestial’s Tear** – The tear of a **celestial being**, said to be filled with the divine energy of the heavens themselves. The tear will restore the divine essence within the Annunaki.

3. **The Eldritch Crystal** – A powerful gem of eldritch origin, capable of bridging the gap between the material plane and the Shadowfell. It is already in our possession thanks to Tillie’s recent efforts. I must ensure its protection at all costs.

4. **An Ancient Relic of the Annunaki** – A long-lost artifact that once belonged to the Annunaki. Its location remains uncertain, but all evidence points to the **Throne of the Arch Heart**. My spies search the **Ironseat Ridge** for clues.

5. **The Blood of a Black Dragon** – Black dragons, with their necrotic connection to death and decay, are tied to ancient and forbidden magic. The blood of one such creature will be used to fuel the ritual's necromantic energy.

6. **A Soul Gem** – The final piece, a gemstone capable of trapping a soul. It is the **vessel** that will contain the essence of the Annunaki until they are fully restored. I am currently negotiating its acquisition with a powerful necromancer.

Once these items are gathered, the ritual can commence. The Annunaki will be reborn, and their reign of benevolent order will begin anew. It is not the world that rejects them but rather **petty mortals**, clinging to their fleeting power. They do not see the greater purpose, the order the Annunaki could bring. But I do.

---

**Date:** 22nd of Unndilar, Year 843  
**Subject:** Opposition

I have become aware of certain individuals—adventurers, no doubt—who seem to be investigating my work. They possess one of our **cultist amulets**, and though they seem unaware of its true nature, I cannot ignore the possibility of interference. Perhaps the de Rolos of Whitestone are behind this. Percy has always been meddlesome. 

I must hasten my efforts and remain vigilant.

---

**Date:** 4th of Brussendar, Year 843  
**Subject:** A Revelation

A fortunate turn of events! I have overheard a conversation that has accelerated my plans. The **heart of a Titan**—one of the six necessary artifacts—has been located! These adventurers, the same ones investigating my operations, seem to have discovered its whereabouts. They spoke openly of it in **Whitestone Castle**, no doubt unaware that I was listening.

This is an incredible boon. I will use this information to secure the Titan’s Heart before they can act. The islands they spoke of… the **Shattered Isles**. I will dispatch my agents at once.

The ritual is closer than ever. Soon, the Annunaki will return, and with them, the world will finally know true peace. My new **friends**, should they come to me, will soon understand their place in this grand design.

- Hargraves sees the Annunaki as a misunderstood, benevolent force capable of restoring order, though his methods and interpretation are clearly twisted.
