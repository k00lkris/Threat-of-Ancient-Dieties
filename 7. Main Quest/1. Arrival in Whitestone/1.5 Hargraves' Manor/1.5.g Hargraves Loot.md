Here is a list of items and gold that the players might find in **Hargraves' Secret Study**:

---

### **Gold & Valuables:**
1. **Coin Pouch** – Contains 150 platinum pieces (1,500 gp).
2. **Hidden Safe** (DC 15 Investigation) – Contains 500 gp, 300 sp, and a small pouch with 5 diamonds (worth 100 gp each).
3. **Ornate Jewelry Box** – Inside are:
   - A silver ring set with a ruby (worth 250 gp)
   - A golden amulet (non-magical) with the Hargraves family crest (worth 300 gp)
   - A pair of emerald earrings (worth 150 gp each)

---

### **Books & Papers:**
1. **[[1.5.h Ledger of Transactions|Ledger of Transactions]]** (DC 13 Investigation) – A detailed book of payments and purchases, many from unsavory contacts. This could implicate Hargraves in underworld dealings.  
2. **Research Notes on the Annunaki** (found on his desk) – Handwritten pages detailing his obsessive study of the **Annunaki**. It references key artifacts and ancient rituals.
3. **Ancient Manuscript** – A large, worn scroll written in Draconic. It seems to recount the history of a long-lost civilization, possibly tied to the Annunaki. Some pages are missing, and others are indecipherable without proper magical translation.
4. **Torn Letters** (DC 12 Investigation to reassemble) – Scraps of letters from **other cultists** referencing a hidden base in the Shadowfell and the recovery of artifacts, further implicating Hargraves in the cult’s activities.
5. **Shipping Documents** – Receipts and logs for shipments of mysterious materials and relics from across Tal'Dorei and Wildemount. Some reference a mysterious buyer only identified as **"The Shadowed Hand."**

---

### **Magical & Rare Items:**
1. **Scroll of Counterspell** – A magical scroll written in neat script, usable by a spellcaster to cast **Counterspell** once.
2. **Potion of Greater Healing** – A vial with glowing red liquid.
3. **Wand of Magic Missiles** (Uncommon) – A well-used but functional wand. It has 3 charges remaining.
4. **A Tome of Forgotten Secrets** (Arcane Lore, DC 17 to decipher) – A dark leather-bound book containing spells and rituals of a sinister nature. It includes a necromantic spell, **Speak with Dead**.
5. **Amethyst Scrying Orb** – A polished orb about the size of a grapefruit. It is clear it has been used for **scrying** purposes, and is engraved with symbols of the Annunaki. Worth 2,000 gp but could be dangerous if activated improperly.
6. **Ring of Mind Shielding** (Rare) – A silver ring set with a purple gem that grants immunity to mind-reading and allows the wearer to store their consciousness in the ring when they die.

---

### **Artifacts & Ritual Items:**
1. **A Fragment of a Ritual Circle** (Drawn on the floor) – This seems to be part of a larger **necromantic ritual** Hargraves was working on. It is incomplete but could be used to infer the scope of his plans.
2. **Cultist Robes** – Two sets of dark robes embroidered with arcane symbols, clearly used in cult rituals.
3. **Bloodstone Dagger** – A ceremonial dagger with a blood-red gemstone embedded in the hilt. The blade hums with faint necromantic energy (non-magical but worth 150 gp to collectors).

---

### **Miscellaneous:**
1. **Hidden Compartment in Desk** (DC 14 Investigation) – Contains a hidden **locket** with a portrait of a woman (possibly a loved one or family member) and a lock of hair.
2. **Key to Hargraves' Private Chambers** – A small bronze key that unlocks a private area of the manor.
3. **Spyglass** (Worth 1,000 gp) – A beautifully crafted spyglass with intricate engravings.
4. **Fine Writing Set** – Contains gold-tipped quills, high-quality ink, and parchment. Worth 50 gp.

---

This collection of items provides both direct clues to Hargraves' plans as well as valuable and magical loot for the players. Some of these items can help move the story forward (like the **Research Notes on the Annunaki** or the **Shipping Documents**), while others provide tangible rewards for the party.
