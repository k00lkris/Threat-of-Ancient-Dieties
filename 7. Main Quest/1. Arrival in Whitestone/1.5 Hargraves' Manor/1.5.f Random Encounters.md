### Random Encounters in Hargraves' Manor

1. **Patrolling Cultist Guard:**
   - **Description:** The players hear the clanking of armor and hushed whispers. A single cultist guard is patrolling the halls, looking for intruders.
   - **Encounter:** 1 [[Cultist Guard]] (as previously statted).
   - **XP:** 700 XP split among the players.

2. **Secret Conversation:**
   - **Description:** The players overhear a hushed conversation behind a closed door. Two cultist guards discuss Hargraves' latest orders and the presence of intruders.
   - **Encounter:** 2 [[Cultist Guard|Cultist Guards]].
   - **XP:** 1,400 XP split among the players.
   - **Bonus:** Players might glean important information if they listen before engaging.

3. **Ambushed by Traps:**
   - **Description:** As the players enter a room, they trigger a hidden trap set by Hargraves' loyal servants.
   - **Encounter:** 
     - **Trap 1:** Poison Dart Trap (DC 15 to spot, DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, 3d6 poison damage).
     - **Trap 2:** Falling Net Trap (DC 15 to spot, DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, restrained condition until freed).
   - **XP:** 300 XP split among the players for disarming or surviving the traps.

4. **Summoning Circle:**
   - **Description:** The players stumble upon a room with a summoning circle in progress. A cultist is in the middle of summoning a shadowy creature.
   - **Encounter:** 1 [[Cultist Guard]] and 1 [[Shadow]] (Monster Manual).
   - **XP:** 1,000 XP split among the players.

5. **Investigating Cultists:**
   - **Description:** A group of cultists is searching the manor, suspicious of the players' presence.
   - **Encounter:** 3 Cult Fanatics (Monster Manual).
   - **XP:** 2,700 XP split among the players.

6. **Arcane Laboratory:**
   - **Description:** The players enter an arcane laboratory filled with magical experiments. A homunculus and a cultist mage guard the room.
   - **Encounter:** 1 [[Cultist Guard]] and 1 Homunculus (Monster Manual).
   - **XP:** 1,100 XP split among the players.

7. **Startled Servant:**
   - **Description:** A loyal servant of Hargraves spots the players and tries to raise an alarm.
   - **Encounter:** 1 Commoner (servant) and 1 [[Cultist Guard]] who responds to the alarm.
   - **XP:** 700 XP split among the players.

8. **Haunted Hallway:**
   - **Description:** The players encounter a ghostly apparition of a former victim of Hargraves' cult.
   - **Encounter:** 1 Ghost (Monster Manual).
   - **XP:** 2,000 XP split among the players.

9. **Forbidden Library:**
   - **Description:** The players find a hidden library with forbidden tomes. A cultist scholar and a book guardian (animated book) protect the area.
   - **Encounter:** 1 [[Cultist Guard]] and 1 Animated Object (book) (Monster Manual).
   - **XP:** 1,300 XP split among the players.

10. **Ritual in Progress:**
    - **Description:** The players come across a room where a dark ritual is being performed. The lead cultist is channeling dark energy while two others guard.
    - **Encounter:** 1 Cultist Priest (use Priest stats from the Monster Manual) and 2 [[Cultist Guard|Cultist Guards]]
    - **XP:** 3,100 XP split among the players.
