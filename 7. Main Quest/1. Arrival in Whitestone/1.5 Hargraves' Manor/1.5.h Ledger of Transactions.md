### Key Details from the Ledger:

- **The Obsidian Veil** and **The Shadowed Hand** seem to be involved in supplying Hargraves with illegal or dangerous magical items and arcane secrets.
- **Tillie Trefusis** has been instrumental in helping Hargraves locate critical artifacts for the resurrection ritual, such as the **Eldritch Crystal**.
- Hargraves has paid multiple mercenaries and smugglers to move black-market goods and rare magical components across Exandria, using secretive and shady channels.
- His growing paranoia is evident, as he continues to hire additional security forces.
- **Cassandra Velvet** has helped smuggle dangerous materials and cultist-related items into Whitestone and other cities.
