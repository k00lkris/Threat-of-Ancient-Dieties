### Asking Around Town for Rumors
This part of the adventure is more role-playing and information-gathering focused, so the XP should reflect the effort and success in gathering useful information.

- **Role-playing and Gathering Information:** Award XP for each significant piece of useful information the players gather. This can include overhearing rumors, talking to key NPCs, and piecing together clues.

  - **Minor Information (e.g., common gossip, less relevant details):** 25 XP per player.
  - **Moderate Information (e.g., leads that point them towards the manor, information about Hargraves’ suspicious activities):** 50 XP per player.
  - **Major Information (e.g., key clues that directly point to incriminating evidence or important locations within the manor):** 100 XP per player.

### Searching the Manor
Exploring and uncovering secrets within Hargraves' manor should be rewarded based on the complexity and importance of the discoveries.

- **Exploring and Investigating:**
  - **Minor Discoveries (e.g., finding secret doors, small clues):** 50 XP per player.
  - **Moderate Discoveries (e.g., uncovering hidden rooms, finding important documents):** 100 XP per player.
  - **Major Discoveries (e.g., finding Hargraves’ personal journal, critical evidence of his plans):** 200 XP per player.

### Encounter with Hargraves and His Guards
Since this is a combat encounter, the XP should be based on the challenge presented by Hargraves and his guards. Given that Hargraves will escape once his HP drops below half, the encounter's XP will focus on the guards and the challenge of facing Hargraves.

- **Defeating the Cultist Guards:**
  - Each Cultist Guard (CR 3): 700 XP each
  - Total for 3 Cultist Guards: 2,100 XP
  - Split among 5 players: 420 XP per player

- **Confronting Hargraves:**
  - Since Hargraves will not be fully defeated, but the players still engage him and force him to retreat, award partial XP for the challenge.
  - **Partial XP for Hargraves (CR 7):** 1,450 XP
  - Split among 5 players: 290 XP per player
