### **Secret Study:**

**Scene:**
The players are in Lord Hargraves’ secret study, a room hidden behind a bookshelf or concealed door. Here, they discover his personal journal and other damning pieces of evidence detailing his plans to resurrect the Annunaki.

**Timing:**
As the players are engrossed in reading the journal and examining the artifacts, Hargraves enters the room, his expression a mix of fury and desperation. He has been alerted by one of his loyal servants or a magical alarm that someone has breached his most private sanctum.

**Read-Aloud Text for the Confrontation:**
> *As you pore over the journal, piecing together the sinister plans and intentions of Lord Hargraves, the door behind you creaks open. The air grows cold, and you feel a chill run down your spine. You turn to see Lord Hargraves standing in the doorway, his eyes blazing with anger and determination. Clad in an elegant but imposing robe, he clutches a staff that crackles with dark energy. "So, you've decided to play the role of detectives in my home," he sneers, his voice dripping with contempt. "But this is where your meddling ends."*

### Details for the DM:

- **Combat or Negotiation:** Depending on the players' approach, this confrontation could lead to a battle, a tense negotiation, or a combination of both.
- **Guards and Servants:** Hargraves might have summoned additional guards or trusted servants to aid him in dealing with the intruders.
- **Escape Plan:** Hargraves should have a means of escape, such as a teleportation spell or a hidden passage, allowing him to flee if the confrontation turns against him. This ensures that he remains a recurring antagonist for the players.

### Combat Strategy

1. [[Lord Bartholomew Hargraves|Lord Hargraves: ]]
   - Uses **Greater Invisibility** or **Mirror Image** at the start of combat to protect himself.
   - Focuses on controlling the battlefield with spells like **Hypnotic Pattern** and **Hold Person**.
   - Uses **Counterspell** to disrupt the players’ powerful spells.
   - When threatened in melee, uses **Parry** and **Uncanny Dodge** to reduce damage.

2. [[Cultist Guard|Cultist Guards:]]
   - Use **Reckless Attack** to maximize their damage output.
   - Cast **Armor of Agathys** at the start of combat to gain temporary hit points and deal damage to attackers.
   - Use **Hold Person** to paralyze key players, giving the guards advantage on attacks.
   - Use **Hellish Rebuke** as a reaction when hit, to deal additional damage to attackers.

### Adjustments

- **If the encounter is too difficult:** Reduce the number of cultist guards to two or decrease their hit points.
- **If the encounter is too easy:** Add more cultist guards or give Hargrave additional reinforcements.
