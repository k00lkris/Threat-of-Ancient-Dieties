### Hargraves Manor - List of Rooms and Locations

**Ground Floor:**
1. **Entrance Hall** - Grand, welcoming area with a large chandelier and ornate decorations.
2. **Drawing Room** - Comfortable seating area for guests, with fine furnishings and a fireplace.
3. **Library** - Shelves filled with books on various subjects, including some hidden texts on the Annunaki.
4. **Dining Room** - Large table capable of seating many guests, often used for formal dinners.
5. **Kitchen** - Well-equipped with modern cooking utensils, a large stove, and a pantry.
6. **Butler’s Pantry** - A small room for the butler to prepare and store items needed for serving meals.
7. **Ballroom** - A large, open space for hosting events and dances.
8. **Guest Parlor** - A comfortable room for informal gatherings with guests.
9. **Study** - Lord Hargraves’ private workspace, filled with papers and documents.
10. **Conservatory** - A glass-walled room filled with exotic plants and comfortable seating.

**First Floor:**
1. **Master Bedroom** - Luxurious room with a large bed, private seating area, and en-suite bathroom.
2. **Lady Hargraves’ Bedroom** - Elegantly decorated room with fine furnishings and personal touches.
3. **Guest Bedrooms** (x3) - Well-appointed rooms for visitors, each with its own unique decor.
4. **Nursery** - A room designed for young children, with toys and a small bed.
5. **Servant Quarters** - Modest rooms for the household staff, located in a separate wing.
6. **Laundry Room** - Equipped with washing facilities and storage for linens.
7. **Armory** - A secure room for storing weapons and armor, including some magical items.
8. **Secret Study** - Hidden behind a bookshelf in the library, containing forbidden texts and cult artifacts.
9. **Gallery** - A hallway lined with portraits and art pieces collected by the family.

**Basement:**
1. **Wine Cellar** - Storage for fine wines and spirits, with a tasting area.
2. **Storage Room** - General storage for household items and supplies.
3. **Dungeon** - Hidden beneath the manor, used for holding prisoners or conducting secret rituals.
4. **Servant’s Passage** - A network of hidden corridors used by the staff to move discreetly.
5. **Alchemy Lab** - A secretive lab filled with alchemical supplies and experiment stations.

**Exterior:**
1. **Courtyard** - A beautifully landscaped area with a fountain and seating for outdoor gatherings.
2. **Stables** - Housing for the family’s horses and carriages, maintained by stablehands.
3. **Gardens** - Expansive, meticulously kept gardens with a variety of plants and walking paths.
4. **Greenhouse** - A glass structure for cultivating rare and exotic plants.
5. **Guardhouse** - A small building for the manor’s guards, equipped with basic amenities.
6. **Guest Cottage** - A separate building for hosting long-term guests, offering privacy and comfort.
