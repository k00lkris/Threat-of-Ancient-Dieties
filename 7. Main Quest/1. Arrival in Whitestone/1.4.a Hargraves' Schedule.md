### Lord Hargraves' Daily Itinerary

**Morning:**
1. **6:00 AM - 7:00 AM:** **Morning Ritual**
   - Hargraves begins his day with a private meditation session in his study. During this time, he consults ancient texts and cult-related manuscripts. He might also perform a small, concealed ritual to commune with dark forces.

2. **7:00 AM - 8:00 AM:** **Breakfast**
   - Hargraves has breakfast in the dining hall with his household staff. This is a good time for the players to observe his interactions with others and perhaps plant listening devices.

3. **8:00 AM - 10:00 AM:** **Advisory Meeting**
   - Hargraves meets with Percival de Rolo to discuss matters of state and governance. He is careful to maintain his facade during these meetings. The players might notice subtle manipulations in his advice aimed at destabilizing Whitestone.

**Midday:**
4. **10:00 AM - 12:00 PM:** **Research and Correspondence**
   - Hargraves retires to his study to engage in correspondence with other cult members and research. This is an opportune time for the players to intercept letters or decipher his cryptic notes.

5. **12:00 PM - 1:00 PM:** **Lunch**
   - Hargraves has lunch in his private quarters. Players might find it useful to observe from a distance to see if he meets with any unusual visitors.

6. **1:00 PM - 3:00 PM:** **Inspection of the Grounds**
   - Hargraves conducts a tour of Whitestone Castle's grounds, checking on various projects and defenses. Players might use this time to search his quarters or study.

**Afternoon:**
7. **3:00 PM - 5:00 PM:** **Secret Meeting**
   - Hargraves has a clandestine meeting with cult operatives in a hidden chamber beneath the castle. This is a critical moment for the players to gather evidence of his cult activities, as he discusses plans and rituals openly with his associates.

8. **5:00 PM - 6:00 PM:** **Library Visit**
   - Hargraves visits the castle library to consult specific books on ancient magics and history. The players could use this time to search his study for incriminating evidence.

**Evening:**
9. **6:00 PM - 8:00 PM:** **Dinner**
   - Hargraves dines with other nobles or influential figures. This social setting might reveal his connections and influence over others.

10. **8:00 PM - 10:00 PM:** **Evening Walk**
    - Hargraves takes an evening walk around Whitestone, often visiting the Sun Tree. He uses this time to subtly perform rituals or leave offerings. Players could follow him to uncover these secretive acts.

11. **10:00 PM - 11:00 PM:** **Night Ritual**
    - Before retiring for the night, Hargraves performs another private ritual in his study. This might include contacting dark entities or preparing for future cult activities. Players might catch him in the act if they can sneak into his quarters unnoticed.

**Night:**
12. **11:00 PM - 6:00 AM:** **Sleep**
    - Hargraves retires to his chambers for the night. This is a time when players could risk a break-in to search for hidden documents or artifacts.

### Points of Interest for Spying:

- **Study:** Contains cult-related texts, correspondence, and ritual materials.
- **Hidden Chamber:** Location of secret meetings with cult operatives.
- **Library:** Where he researches dark magics and ancient histories.
- **Sun Tree:** Potential location for clandestine rituals and offerings.
