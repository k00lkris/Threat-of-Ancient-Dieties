### Third Act: The Return of Tharizdun

**Overview:**
In the third act, the players discover that Lord Hargraves has been manipulated by Tharizdun, the Chained Oblivion, into collecting the artifacts and performing the resurrection ritual. This ritual, rather than reviving the Annunaki as Hargraves believes, is actually a ploy to release Tharizdun from his ancient prison. Upon the ritual's completion, Tharizdun is freed, unleashing chaos upon the world. The players must now embark on a high-stakes campaign to stop Tharizdun and seal him away once more, taking them from level 15 to 20.

### Key Elements of the Third Act

1. **Revelation and Pursuit:**
   - **Objective:** Discover the truth about Tharizdun's manipulation and pursue Hargraves to stop the ritual.
   - **Encounters:** Uncovering the deception, racing against time, and confronting Hargraves' elite guards.

2. **The Ritual and Tharizdun's Release:**
   - **Objective:** Witness the release of Tharizdun and survive the initial chaos.
   - **Encounters:** Battling Hargraves, cultists, and the initial wave of Tharizdun's power.

3. **Gathering Allies and Resources:**
   - **Objective:** Rally allies and gather powerful artifacts to counter Tharizdun.
   - **Encounters:** Diplomacy, strategic battles, and retrieving powerful artifacts from dangerous locations.

4. **Confronting Tharizdun's Minions:**
   - **Objective:** Defeat Tharizdun's powerful minions who are spreading chaos.
   - **Encounters:** Epic battles against powerful entities, each controlling a key location.

5. **Sealing Tharizdun Away:**
   - **Objective:** Perform a powerful ritual to seal Tharizdun back into his prison.
   - **Encounters:** Navigating the final confrontation, overcoming Tharizdun's direct influence, and completing the ritual.

- **Encounters:** Confronting elite cultists guarding the final clues.

### Integrating the Third Act

**Clues and Leads:**
- Provide the players with hints about Tharizdun's manipulation through visions, dreams, and revelations from captured cultists.

**Challenges:**
- Each stage of the journey should present unique challenges, including high-stakes battles, strategic planning, and moral choices.

**Moral Choices:**
- Present the players with difficult decisions, such as sacrificing powerful artifacts for the greater good or choosing between saving allies and completing their mission.

By introducing Tharizdun as the true antagonist and structuring the third act around his return, you create a high-stakes and epic conclusion to your campaign, challenging your players to rise to the occasion and save the world from the Chained Oblivion.
