### Required Items for the Resurrection Ritual

The resurrection ritual to bring back the Annunaki requires several rare and powerful components, each with its own significance and history. These items are scattered across Exandria and are guarded by various entities or hidden in dangerous locations. Below are the items required for the ritual and which one Hargraves has successfully acquired:

1. **Heart of a Titan**:
   - **Description:** The powerful and pulsating heart of a long-dead titan, imbued with immense energy and the essence of ancient giants.
   - **Significance:** Provides the raw power needed to resurrect the Annunaki, drawing upon the primal forces of creation.
   - **Status:** Not yet obtained. Hargraves has learned of its location and is actively seeking it.

2. **Celestial Tear**:
   - **Description:** A tear shed by a celestial being, crystallized into a rare and ethereal gem.
   - **Significance:** Represents divine approval and purity, crucial for the ritual's success.
   - **Status:** Not yet obtained. The tear is rumored to be guarded by a powerful celestial being or hidden in a sacred temple.

3. **Eldritch Crystal**:
   - **Description:** A crystal infused with eldritch magic, often found in places where the veil between worlds is thin.
   - **Significance:** Channels the arcane energies necessary to bridge the gap between the mortal realm and the divine.
   - **Status:** Acquired. This is the ancient artifact Hargraves has successfully obtained, giving him a significant advantage.

4. **Ancient Relic of the Annunaki**:
   - **Description:** A relic belonging to the Annunaki, such as a piece of their armor, weapon, or a personal belonging.
   - **Significance:** Acts as a focal point for the resurrection, connecting the ritual to the essence of the Annunaki.
   - **Status:** Not yet obtained. This relic is likely hidden in an ancient ruin or protected by powerful guardians.

5. **Blood of a Dragon**:
   - **Description:** Blood from a dragon, preferably one of great age and power.
   - **Significance:** Adds the strength and ferocity of dragons to the ritual, ensuring the Annunaki return with their full might.
   - **Status:** Not yet obtained. The blood must be taken from a living dragon, making it a dangerous and challenging task.

6. **Soul Gem**:
   - **Description:** A gem capable of trapping and holding a soul, often used in necromantic practices.
   - **Significance:** Provides the necessary vessel to house the spirit of the Annunaki during the resurrection.
   - **Status:** Not yet obtained. The gem is likely held by a powerful necromancer or hidden in a dark, forbidden place.
