## **Session Plan: From Whitestone to Stillben**

### **Act 1: The Aftermath in the Crypts**

The session starts with the players still in the crypts after defeating Vornelith Blackthorn and his blood ghosts. Before they leave, let’s give them a chance to:

- **Investigate the tomb further**: There may be hidden treasures, remnants of old rituals, or final clues about the blood ghosts.
- **Loot the Necromancer’s Remains**: Perhaps he carried some notes on his work, or a connection to other dark forces (like Lord Hargraves or the Cult of the Annunaki).
- **Spiritual Significance**: A Raven Queen-related event may occur as they prepare to leave. The whispers of the dead, or an ominous warning, hinting that Vornelith was only a piece of something larger.

💡 **Prep Needed:**

- A few final treasure items.
- A potential vision or cryptic message from the Raven Queen or her servants.

---

### **Act 2: Reporting to the Paleguard & the De Rolos**

Once the players return to the surface, they will need to report back to **Captain Soren** and possibly the **De Rolos**.

- **Captain Soren:** Will be in awe of what they found, but also concerned about necromantic activity in Whitestone.
- **Percival & Vex'ahlia:** If the players choose to inform them, they will reward them for dealing with the threat but also show concern about the growing signs of cult activity.

💰 **Potential Rewards for the Side Quest**

- **Gold (from the Paleguard)**
- **A special item from Whitestone’s armory (e.g., an enchanted cloak or weapon)**
- **The promise of future aid from the De Rolos, such as reinforcements or supplies**

🛠 **Prep Needed:**

- Captain Soren’s reaction & dialogue.
- The De Rolos’ response and potential future involvement.

---

### **Act 3: Preparing to Leave Whitestone**

Before leaving for Stillben, the players may:

- **Stock up on supplies** (final chance to visit shops, say goodbye to NPCs).
- **Follow Up with Evelyn Windrider** (if they trust her, she may offer additional insight or items).
- **Face Any Lasting Consequences** (e.g., townsfolk spreading rumors about their deeds).
- **Choose Their Mode of Travel** (this will influence Act 4).

🛠 **Prep Needed:**

- Shops & supplies.
- Any potential consequences of their previous actions.
- Evelyn’s final words if they visit her.

---

### **Act 4: The Journey to Stillben**

They have a couple of options:

1. **Travel by Land**
    
    - This is the longer route but safer.
    - It would take **roughly 20 days** by carriage.
    - There could be **random encounters along the way** (bandits, monsters, environmental hazards).
    - Potential stop in **Turst Fields** or **Westruun**.
2. **Travel by Magical Means**
    
    - **Captain Soren** may offer to **teleport them** part of the way (e.g., to Westruun or even Stillben) as part of their reward.
    - **The De Rolos** could offer **an airship or griffon ride** if they think the party’s mission is urgent.
    - This option reduces random encounters but skips much of the journey.

🛠 **Prep Needed:**

- If **traveling by land**, roll for potential **random encounters**.
- If **magical assistance** is offered, decide the limitations.

---

### **Act 5: The First Leg of the Journey**

If the players take the **land route**, we’ll need:

- **A meaningful random encounter** (one that isn’t just a combat but has story implications).
- **A moment for character development** (campfire discussions, dreams, or memory triggers about their past as The Reapers).
- **An obstacle** (broken carriage wheel, river crossing, bad weather).

💡 **Possible Encounters:**

1. **The Obsidian Veil approaches them**: They still believe the players are The Reapers and have a **job for them**—to hunt Vincinte Laurent.
2. **Bandit Ambush**: A group of well-organized bandits attempt to rob them, possibly knowing that they just earned a big reward.
3. **A Feral Chupacabra Attack**: What if, by some **strange twist of fate**, an actual creature resembling their fabricated **Chupacabra** appears?

🛠 **Prep Needed:**

- Choose an encounter that adds intrigue rather than just combat.

---

### **End of the Session Goal**

- The session should end **somewhere along the journey**—whether in **Turst Fields**, **Westruun**, or the **wilds on the way to Stillben**.
- Set up **the next big event** to hook them into the next session.

---

## **Final Prep Checklist**

✅ Flesh out the **crypts' final treasures & lore clues**.  
✅ Plan **reactions from NPCs** (Captain Soren, Percy, Vex’ahlia, Evelyn).  
✅ Decide **rewards** (gold, magic items, favors).  
✅ Set up **travel choices** (overland vs. magical aid).  
✅ Choose **one key encounter** for the journey.  
✅ Identify a **good stopping point** for the session.
