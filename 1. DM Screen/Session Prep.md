# Twelfth Session

The last session ended with the party being trapped upstairs with Hargraves and his guards downstairs.

Hargraves is going to try and coax the party downstairs by any means possible. 

Possible Hargraves dialog:
>"This is all so unnecessary, isn’t it? We don’t have to be enemies. After all, I believe we share more common ground than you think. You’ve uncovered much already, but I don’t want to see you harmed."

>"You see, I value loyalty and intelligence. And you, my new friends, have both in spades. Now, I _could_ pursue more... _hostile_ routes, but where would that get us? Instead, I extend to you a hand of friendship. If you come out, I promise you this: I will not harm you."


- **If they ask about the amulet or artifacts:**
    
    - **Hargraves:**  
        >_"Ah yes, the amulet. A beautiful piece, isn’t it? It’s merely a tool, nothing more. You’ve stumbled upon it, but I see no reason we can’t use it together. After all, I’m not without resources, and you... well, you’re clearly capable of finding things I need."_
- **If they ask about his plans or the cult:**
    
    - **Hargraves (with a slight chuckle):**  
        >_"Plans? I assure you, I have only the noblest of intentions. A better world, a more _orderly_ world. One free from chaos and strife. You’ve seen the darkness that festers in this land. Together, we could usher in something grand. But only if we work _together_."_
- **If they demand more assurances of their safety:**
    
    - **Hargraves (confidently):**  
        >"I’ve already told you: I will not harm you. You have my word. We’re friends now, aren’t we? I’ve no reason to lie to you. I’ve been very clear in my intentions."_

### **The Betrayal:**

Once the conversation reaches a point where the players feel like they might have a way out or believe Hargraves, he turns the situation against them with a chilling play on words. This should happen after they are lulled into a false sense of security.

**Hargraves (walking toward the exit, speaking almost casually):**  
>"_You know, it’s refreshing to meet such resourceful people. I could have used friends like you in the past."_

He reaches the door, resting his hand on the knob as he glances back with a sly smile.

**Hargraves (in a tone full of false sincerity):**  
>"_I meant what I said earlier. I cannot harm my friends._"

He pauses, a cold gleam in his eyes as he turns to one of his guards.

**Hargraves (with a wicked smirk):**  
>_"Kill my friends."_
