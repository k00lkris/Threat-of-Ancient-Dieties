### Cult of the Annunaki

**Description:**
The Cult of the Annunaki is a secretive and ancient organization dedicated to resurrecting the Annunaki, powerful beings believed to be the offspring of humans and angels. The cult's members are fanatically loyal, driven by a belief that the return of the Annunaki will bring about a new era of divine rule and order. They see the Annunaki as misunderstood and wrongfully demonized entities who will bring true peace and prosperity to Exandria.

**Hierarchy:**
- **High Priests:** At the top of the hierarchy are the High Priests, individuals who have dedicated their lives to the cult's goals. They possess extensive knowledge of dark rituals and ancient lore.
- **Cult Leaders:** Below the High Priests are the Cult Leaders, responsible for overseeing regional activities and recruiting new members.
- **Acolytes:** The rank-and-file members of the cult, known as Acolytes, carry out the day-to-day tasks, including performing rituals, gathering artifacts, and conducting covert operations.
- **Cultist Guards:** Specially trained members who serve as the cult's enforcers and protectors. They are highly skilled in combat and fanatically loyal.

**Symbol:**
The cult's symbol is an intricate design resembling an eye with rays extending outward, representing the watchful and omnipresent nature of the Annunaki. This symbol is often seen on amulets, banners, and tattoos worn by cult members.

**Beliefs:**
- **Divine Right:** The cult believes that the Annunaki are destined to rule Exandria, bringing order and enlightenment. They view the current mortal rulers as corrupt and inadequate.
- **Purification:** The cult advocates for the purification of the world, seeing the eradication of those who oppose the Annunaki as a necessary step to achieve their utopia.
- **Resurrection Ritual:** The cult is dedicated to gathering ancient artifacts and performing complex rituals to bring the Annunaki back to life. They believe these beings will restore the world's balance.

**Activities:**
- **Artifact Hunting:** The cult relentlessly searches for powerful artifacts needed for their resurrection rituals. These artifacts are often hidden in dangerous or sacred places.
- **Recruitment:** Cult members actively recruit new followers, preying on those who feel disenfranchised or seek purpose.
- **Sabotage and Espionage:** The cult engages in covert operations to undermine current rulers and institutions, paving the way for the Annunaki's return.

**Headquarters:**
The cult's primary headquarters is hidden deep within the Shadowfell, a realm of darkness and despair. Here, they conduct their most secretive and powerful rituals, shielded from prying eyes.

### Cultist Hand Gesture

**Description:**
The cultists of Lord Hargraves' secret society use a subtle yet distinctive hand gesture to identify one another. The gesture involves the following steps:

1. **Touch Thumb to Forefinger:** The cultist touches the tip of their thumb to the tip of their forefinger, forming a circle.
2. **Raise Middle Finger:** The middle finger is extended upward, forming a slight curve.
3. **Fold Ring and Pinky Fingers:** The ring finger and pinky are folded down towards the palm.
4. **Twist the Wrist:** The wrist is twisted slightly inward, so the circle made by the thumb and forefinger faces outward, almost like a small eye.

This gesture is designed to resemble an eye, symbolizing the cult's watchfulness and secrecy.

### Read-Aloud Text for Recognition:

  >*As you move through the manor, you notice a group of cultists exchanging a peculiar hand gesture. One touches the tip of their thumb to their forefinger, forming a small circle, while raising the middle finger slightly. The ring and pinky fingers are folded towards the palm, and the wrist is twisted inward, making the circle face outward like a watchful eye.*

**If the players attempt to mimic the gesture:**
- **Perception Check:** Have the cultists make a Perception check (DC 15) to notice if the gesture is slightly off or hesitant.
- **Investigation Check:** If the players pass the initial check, cultists might still be suspicious and ask a probing question or require a verbal password.

### Consequences:

**If the players fail to perform the gesture correctly:**
- The cultists will immediately become suspicious and may challenge the players.
- Depending on the situation, they might sound an alarm, call for reinforcements, or confront the players directly.

**If the players perform the gesture correctly but arouse suspicion:**
- Cultists may ask for the verbal password or initiate a secret handshake to further verify the players' authenticity.
- The players will need to be quick-witted to navigate the additional scrutiny.

### Example Scenario:

**Cultist Guard Interaction:**
>*You approach a group of cultist guards standing near a doorway. One of them looks up and notices you, his eyes narrowing. He performs the hand gesture, the 'watchful eye,' and waits for you to respond.*

If the players respond correctly:
- The cultist guard nods approvingly and allows them to pass, though he keeps a wary eye on them.

If the players respond incorrectly:
- The cultist guard's eyes widen in suspicion. "Who are you?" he demands. "You don't belong here!" He reaches for his weapon, and the other guards tense up, ready for a fight.
