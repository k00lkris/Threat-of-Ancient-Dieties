### Encounter: Lost Child in the Woods

#### Scene Setup:
While traveling along the Alabaster Trail between Turst Fields and Whitestone, the players come across a distressed halfling child wandering alone in the woods. The child appears frightened and lost, clutching a torn and weathered doll in their hands.

#### Role-playing and Interaction:
- **Approaching the Child:** As the players approach the child, they can see tears streaming down their face. The child is unable to provide clear directions or information about how they became separated from their family.
- **Comforting the Child:** The players have the opportunity to calm the child and offer reassurance, attempting to gain their trust and learn more about their situation. The child may respond positively to gestures of kindness and empathy.

#### Investigation:
- **Searching for Clues:** The players can search the surrounding area for clues or signs of the child's family or any nearby settlements. This might involve tracking footprints, examining nearby landmarks, or using other skills such as Survival or Investigation to gather information.
- **Child's Story:** Through gentle questioning and conversation, the players may learn more about the child's background, including their name, where they were traveling from, and any notable landmarks or features they remember from their journey.

#### Challenges:
- **Navigating the Woods:** The dense foliage and winding paths of the forest can make navigation challenging. The players must use their survival skills to avoid getting lost themselves while also guiding the child to safety.
- **Encountering Wildlife:** The woods are home to various creatures, some of which may pose a threat to the child or the players. The players must remain vigilant and protect the child from any potential dangers they encounter along the way.

#### Resolution:
- **Reuniting the Child:** The players' primary objective is to reunite the lost child with their family or bring them to safety. Depending on the information gathered during their investigation, they may lead the child back to their family's camp, escort them to the nearest settlement, or seek out other travelers who can provide assistance.
- **Gratitude and Reward:** Once the child is safely reunited with their family, the players may receive expressions of gratitude from the child's parents or guardians, along with a small reward or token of appreciation for their help.

This encounter provides an opportunity for the players to demonstrate their compassion and problem-solving skills as they navigate the challenges of finding and rescuing a lost child in the wilderness. It emphasizes the importance of empathy and teamwork while offering a break from more combat-focused encounters.
