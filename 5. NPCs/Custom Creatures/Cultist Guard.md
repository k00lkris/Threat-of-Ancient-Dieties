**Saving Throws:** Str +4, Dex +4, Wis +2
**Skills:** Athletics +4, Perception +2, Intimidation +4
**Senses:** Passive Perception 12
**Languages:** Common, Infernal
**Challenge:** 3 (700 XP)

---

**Traits:**

**Dark Devotion:** The cultist guard has advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

**Multiattack:** The cultist guard makes two melee attacks.

**Reckless Attack:** At the start of its turn, the cultist guard can gain advantage on all melee weapon attack rolls during that turn, but attack rolls against it have advantage until the start of its next turn.

**Spellcasting:** The cultist guard is a 4th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit with spell attacks). The cultist guard has the following warlock spells prepared:
- Cantrips (at will): Eldritch Blast, Thaumaturgy
- 1st level (3 slots): Hellish Rebuke, Armor of Agathys
- 2nd level (2 slots): Mirror Image, Hold Person

---

**Actions:**

**Melee Weapon Attack:** *Longsword:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d8 + 2) slashing damage.

**Ranged Weapon Attack:** *Hand Crossbow:* +4 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 6 (1d6 + 2) piercing damage.

---

**Reactions:**

**Hellish Rebuke (1st-level spell; 1/day):** When the cultist guard takes damage from a creature it can see within 60 feet of it, it can use its reaction to cast Hellish Rebuke.

---

### Tactics and Challenge

**Tactics:** 
- The cultist guards will use their Reckless Attack to deal significant damage while relying on their spells like Mirror Image to reduce the likelihood of being hit.
- They will cast Armor of Agathys before engaging in melee to provide extra survivability and deal retaliatory damage.
- They will use Hold Person to lock down key targets and reduce the effectiveness of the party.
- Hellish Rebuke serves as a strong deterrent against attacking the cultist guards directly.

**Challenge Adjustment:**
- With their spellcasting abilities and offensive traits, three cultist guards working together can provide a formidable challenge for a party of 5 level 5 characters.
- They can coordinate their actions to maximize the impact of Hold Person and use Mirror Image strategically to avoid damage.
