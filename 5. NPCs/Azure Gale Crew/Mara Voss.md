## Profile

A firm first mate supervising discipline, watch rotations, and sail handling. Challenges the captain in private, but runs a disciplined deck.

**Running this NPC:** First Mate aboard Captain [[Captain Dorian Maldrake]]'s vessel. A recurring supporting character, not a predetermined combatant or casualty.
