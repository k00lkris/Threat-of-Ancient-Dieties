## Profile

An enthusiastic young deckhand, curious about the Reapers' adventures and magical abilities. Competent in the rigging but newer to ocean voyages.

**Running this NPC:** Deckhand aboard Captain [[Captain Dorian Maldrake]]'s vessel. A recurring supporting character, not a predetermined combatant or casualty.
