## Profile

A genial, gossipy galley cook with two pet parrots. Keeps the crew fed and morale afloat; offers casual conversation and stories.

**Running this NPC:** Cook aboard Captain [[Captain Dorian Maldrake]]'s vessel. A recurring supporting character, not a predetermined combatant or casualty.
