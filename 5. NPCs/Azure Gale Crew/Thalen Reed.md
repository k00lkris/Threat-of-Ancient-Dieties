## Profile

An elderly, exacting navigator who knows currents, charts and seafarers' legends. Knows frightening stories about the Salty Dogs, but not their curse's cause.

**Running this NPC:** Navigator aboard Captain [[Captain Dorian Maldrake]]'s vessel. A recurring supporting character, not a predetermined combatant or casualty.
