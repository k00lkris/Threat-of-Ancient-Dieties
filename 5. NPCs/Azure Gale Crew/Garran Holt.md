## Profile

Quiet, practical carpenter who hears every creak in the hull. A natural contact for Notos's interest in tools, materials and salvaging.

**Running this NPC:** Shipwright aboard Captain [[Captain Dorian Maldrake]]'s vessel. A recurring supporting character, not a predetermined combatant or casualty.
