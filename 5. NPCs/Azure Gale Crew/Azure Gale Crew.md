## Crew life and chain of command

Maldrake makes final decisions. Mara supervises deck work and watches. Thalen advises on navigation. Garran handles repairs. Bram runs the galley. Pip assists in rigging and lookout duties. Other sailors fill out the ship's watches and operational needs.

## Boarding impressions — optional

- Mara checks that passengers are clear of the loading rig.
- Thalen leans over a sea chart, muttering about currents.
- Bram calls out that warm food won't wait for stragglers.
- Pip peers down from the rigging and asks about the Reapers' magic.
- Garran taps a timber, listening for an ominous hollow note.

## DM guidance

These are **people on the ship**, not mandatory plot hooks or predetermined victims. Use them in natural interactions and when naval emergencies call for their expertise. Stat blocks on each NPC page support fast rulings.

Thalen knows frightening sailor folklore about the [[The Crimson Abyss|Crimson Abyss]], but does not know the true nature of the ghost pirates' curse.
