# The Crimson Abyss — The Salty Dogs

> [!danger] DM ONLY — Campaign lore and developing story hooks
> This page separates **established history** from **ideas still under consideration**. The crew's presence does not require a predetermined encounter or player response.

## At a glance

- **Ship:** *Crimson Abyss*
- **Crew:** The Salty Dogs
- **Origin:** Pirates operating from the Menagerie Coast, including Port Damali
- **Current nature:** A feared ghost ship, still sailing the seas with its dead crew
- **Reputation:** Ruthless pirates in life; more vicious and terrifying in death
- **Connection:** [[Hurk Dingleback]] — former crewmate and sole known survivor of the wreck
- **Related plot:** [[Disappearance of Hurk and Heijla]]

## Established history — DM canon

The Salty Dogs were violent, notorious pirates who raided ships and coastal settlements. They were not sympathetic outcasts: their reputation for brutality was earned.

Hurk joined their crew aboard the *Crimson Abyss* while young and rose through the ranks through his **strength and ruthlessness**. This is stated in [[Hurk Dingleback|Hurk's character backstory]].

During a fierce storm, **lightning struck the *Crimson Abyss* and the ship sank**. Hurk miraculously survived, washing ashore on the Menagerie Coast with no memory of his former life. He later worked as a caravan guard and joined the Reapers.

The ship and the Salty Dogs **are genuinely spectral**. They roam the seas and are feared among sailors. They were ruthless in life and are **worse in death**. The ghost ship's existence is not merely a false tavern rumor.

The Salty Dogs **do not know the cause of their curse**. They generally assume the gods condemned them to sail forever because of the violence and cruelty they committed in life. They have no confirmed understanding of what, if anything, might end their punishment. They are not consciously hunting Hurk to solve it.

## Their behavior in undeath

The crew remain dangerous pirates, not penitents. They can assault, terrorize, and plunder passing ships. A meeting with the *Crimson Abyss* should be frightening even to experienced sailors; its reputation precedes it.

**Hurk's presence is the one established potential check on a full assault.** Since Hurk is no longer with the Reapers, a confrontation aboard the *Azure Gale* would be perilous. Whether the party can **parley**, flee, fight, or find an unexpected solution remains a matter of play, not a prewritten outcome.

Do **not** assume the ghosts are friendly, honorable, or interested in redemption. Likewise, avoid deciding in advance that all negotiations must fail.

## What sailors believe — in-world folklore

The storm and shipwreck are remembered in the sea shanty about the Salty Dogs and the *Crimson Abyss*. Unlike an ordinary exaggerated ghost story, the song's central claims of sinking and haunting are true.

Sailors fear the *Crimson Abyss* and may react with dread, prayer, evasive maneuvers, or panic at its sight. The shanty is a useful way for that reputation to enter the story organically, without requiring exposition.

**The Salty Dogs themselves interpret their condition as possible divine punishment.** This belief is not proof that the gods actually cursed them.

### Sea shanty — recurring refrain

> Yo-ho, yo-ho, the Salty Dogs we be,  
> With a bottle of rum and a treasure chest, we roam the endless sea.  
> Yo-ho, yo-ho, through storm and waves we fight,  
> With the Crimson Abyss and a pirate’s kiss, we haunt the seas by night.

The complete four-verse shanty was written for the campaign and can be used as an in-world performance or sailors' warning.

## Hurk's connection

Hurk is the only known survivor of the *Crimson Abyss*'s sinking. His survival, amnesia, and former ruthlessness are established by his character page.

Hurk and Heijla are currently missing after events involving the Myriad in Stillben; their disappearance is addressed in [[Disappearance of Hurk and Heijla]]. Hurk's player has left the campaign, so Hurk can continue as an NPC if the Reapers ever pursue his fate.

**Developing theory, not yet established:** The spectral crew's inability to rest may be connected to the crew being incomplete because **Hurk survived**. Neither the pirates nor Hurk knows this. We have *not* decided the exact supernatural mechanism, whether he must return, whether he must die, or what ending the curse would mean.

This possibility should remain a **DM mystery** until developed further.

## Possible future side quests — brainstorming only

### Encounter at sea: the Crimson Abyss

On the proposed **7–10-day voyage** aboard Captain Dorian Maldrake's *Azure Gale*, the Reapers might encounter the ghost ship. The experience can establish the Salty Dogs' terror and perhaps introduce their connection to Hurk, but **an encounter is not mandatory**. Let the party choose how to react; negotiation is possible but uncertain.

The Salty Dogs are **not** searching the ocean with knowledge that Hurk is their missing key. Their raids continue because they are brutal pirates, not because they are following a known quest to break their curse.

### Return to Stillben: find Hurk

If the Reapers eventually learn or suspect that Hurk matters to the ship's fate, they could choose to investigate his disappearance in Stillben and the Myriad connection. This might also reopen Heijla's storyline.

This is **optional** and need not happen before the Shattered Isles quest. No rescue outcome, ghost-ship resolution, or route back to Stillben is predetermined.

### Potential resolution

A future meeting between Hurk and the Salty Dogs could reveal the true nature of the curse and provide a satisfying conclusion for Hurk as a retired player character. **Do not establish that returning Hurk automatically frees the ghosts.** The price and consequences remain to be designed.

## Open questions for the DM

- What supernatural force actually bound the crew and the ship?
- Why did Hurk alone survive, and how is his survival connected to the curse?
- What does the ghostly captain remember about Hurk?
- Does the crew know or believe Hurk died in the storm?
- Could a parley with the spectral pirates succeed, and on what terms?
- Would any eventual release end the crew's undeath, return the ship to the depths, or have another consequence?
