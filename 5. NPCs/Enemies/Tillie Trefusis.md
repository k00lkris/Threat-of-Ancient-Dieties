**Background:** Cultist of the Annunaki

**Background and History:**

Tillie Trefusis grew up in the bustling city of Emon, where she lived an unremarkable early life. She was a plain-looking girl with dirty blonde hair and big hazel eyes, often overlooked in the vibrant and dynamic environment of the city. Tillie found solace in her friendship with Mirabel Divoux, a girl from a noble family who treated her with kindness and affection. The two were inseparable, and Tillie cherished their bond as sisters.

However, as they grew older, Tillie’s life took a dark turn. She was drawn into the shadows of Emon, where she encountered a group of cultists dedicated to resurrecting the Annunaki. The cult promised power, purpose, and a place in the new world order they sought to create. Tillie was easily seduced by their promises, and she quickly rose through their ranks due to her intelligence and manipulative skills.

Tillie’s recruitment of Mirabel into the cult was a calculated move. She saw potential in her friend’s restlessness and dissatisfaction with her noble life. Using their deep bond, Tillie manipulated Mirabel into participating in the cult’s rituals, subtly steering her towards more significant acts of loyalty. Her ultimate goal was to fully indoctrinate Mirabel and use her noble connections to further the cult’s ambitions.

Tillie played a crucial role in discovering the location of the Eldritch Crystal, an artifact essential to the resurrection ritual. Her knowledge of ancient texts and willingness to delve into forbidden lore made her invaluable to the cult’s leadership. She meticulously planned the expedition into the Shadowfell to retrieve the crystal, knowing it would greatly enhance their capabilities.

Tillie’s dedication to the cult and her manipulative nature make her a dangerous adversary. She is willing to do whatever it takes to achieve her goals, including sacrificing those she once called friends.

**Lair and Followers:**

Tillie often operates from hidden locations within Emon, such as abandoned buildings or secret rooms beneath the city. She is always surrounded by loyal cultists ready to do her bidding. These followers provide protection and assistance during her rituals and schemes.
