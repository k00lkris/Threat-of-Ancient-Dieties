**Seraphis, Master Assassin**

_Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil_

**Armor Class** 17 (studded leather, Dexterity) 
**Hit Points** 110 (20d8 + 20) *
*Speed** 40 ft.

**STR** 12 (+1)
**DEX** 20 (+5) 
**CON** 12 (+1) 
**INT** 16 (+3) 
**WIS** 14 (+2) 
**CHA** 14 (+2)

**Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +7 **Skills** Acrobatics +9, Deception +6, Insight +6, Investigation +7, Perception +6, Stealth +13 
**Damage Resistances** Poison 
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16 
**Languages** Common, Thieves' Cant, Elvish 
**Challenge** 9 (5,000 XP)

**Evasion**. If Seraphis is subjected to an effect that allows him to make a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, he instead takes no damage if he succeeds on the saving throw, and only half damage if he fails.

**Sneak Attack (1/Turn)**. Seraphis deals an extra 28 (8d6) damage when he hits a target with a weapon attack and has advantage on the attack roll, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of Seraphis that isn’t incapacitated and Seraphis doesn’t have disadvantage on the attack roll.

**Uncanny Dodge**. When an attacker that Seraphis can see hits him with an attack, he can use his reaction to halve the attack’s damage against him.

**Assassinate**. During his first turn, Seraphis has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn’t taken a turn. Any hit Seraphis scores against a surprised creature is a critical hit.

**Actions**

**Multiattack**. Seraphis makes three attacks with his daggers or short sword.

**Short Sword**. _Melee Weapon Attack_: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit_: 9 (1d6 + 5) piercing damage.

**Dagger**. _Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack_: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft., one target. _Hit_: 8 (1d4 + 5) piercing damage.

**Poisoned Blade** (Recharge 5–6). Seraphis coats his weapon with a deadly poison. The next time he hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take 22 (5d8) poison damage and become poisoned for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success.

**Bonus Actions**

**Cunning Action**. Seraphis can take the Dash, Disengage, or Hide action.

**Shadow Step**. When in dim light or darkness, Seraphis can teleport up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space he can see that is also in dim light or darkness. He then has advantage on the first melee attack he makes before the end of the turn.

**Reactions**

**Parry**. Seraphis adds 4 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, Seraphis must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

## Background

**Role in the Obsidian Veil**

Seraphis became one of the Veil's most feared enforcers, known for his ruthless efficiency and unwavering loyalty. His primary role was to track down and eliminate those who betrayed or threatened the organization. His reputation as a relentless hunter was well-earned, and few dared to cross the Veil knowing that Seraphis might come after them.

Despite his brutal methods, Seraphis was also known for his cunning and strategic mind. He often devised elaborate schemes to outwit the Veil’s enemies, using a combination of fear and manipulation to achieve his goals. His loyalty to the Veil was absolute, driven by a deep-seated belief in their mission to control and dominate the underworld.

**Appearance**

Seraphis is a striking figure, his presence commanding attention and fear. He is tall and lean, with a wiry strength that belies his true power. His skin is pale, almost ghostly, contrasting sharply with his jet-black hair, which he keeps tied back in a severe knot. His eyes are a piercing blue, cold and calculating, always observing and assessing those around him.

He dresses in dark, form-fitting clothing that allows for maximum mobility, often adorned with hidden pockets and compartments for his various tools and weapons. A long, black coat with silver accents flows behind him, adding to his intimidating appearance. The most distinctive feature of his attire is a mask that covers the lower half of his face, a symbol of his allegiance to the Veil and a reminder of his anonymity.

**Personality**

Seraphis is a complex character, his outward demeanor often masking the turmoil within. He is cold and calculating, rarely showing emotion and always maintaining control. His loyalty to the Veil is unshakeable, but it is driven by more than just duty. Deep down, Seraphis harbors a fear of returning to the life of poverty and helplessness he once knew. The Veil gave him purpose and power, and he clings to that with a fierce determination.

Despite his ruthless exterior, Seraphis has a keen intellect and a strategic mind. He is always thinking several steps ahead, planning his moves with precision. He respects those who demonstrate skill and cunning, even if they are his enemies. However, he has little patience for incompetence or betrayal, and his retribution is swift and merciless.

**Relationship with Vincinte**

Seraphis and Vincinte share a complicated history. As Vincinte’s mentor, Seraphis took great pride in his protégé’s skills and accomplishments. He saw a reflection of himself in Vincinte, and for a time, they worked together seamlessly. However, Vincinte’s eventual disillusionment with the Veil and his subsequent betrayal cut deep. To Seraphis, Vincinte’s defection was not just a personal affront but a dangerous threat to the Veil’s stability.

When Vincinte fled, Seraphis was tasked with bringing him back—or eliminating him. The chase became a personal vendetta, a mission to restore order and prove that no one could escape the Veil’s grasp. Despite his cold exterior, Seraphis harbored a mixture of disappointment and anger towards Vincinte, emotions he rarely allowed himself to feel.

**Abilities and Skills**

Seraphis is a master of stealth and assassination, his skills honed to perfection through years of training and experience. He is proficient with a variety of weapons, favoring daggers and short swords for close combat. His agility and speed make him a formidable opponent, able to strike quickly and disappear without a trace.

In addition to his physical prowess, Seraphis is also a skilled manipulator and strategist. He excels in gathering intelligence and using it to his advantage, often playing mind games with his targets to keep them off balance. His knowledge of poisons and traps adds another layer to his lethal arsenal.
