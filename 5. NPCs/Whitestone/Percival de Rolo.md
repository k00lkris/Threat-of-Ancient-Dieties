**Legendary Resistance (3/Day).** If Percy fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

**Magic Resistance.** Percy has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

**Magic Weapons.** Percy's attacks are magical.

**Reckless Attack.** Percy can throw aside all concern for defense to attack with fierce desperation. When he makes his first attack on his turn, he can decide to attack recklessly. Doing so gives him advantage on melee weapon attack rolls using Strength during this turn, but attack rolls against him have advantage until his next turn.

**Spellcasting.** Percy is a 20th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 22, +14 to hit with spell attacks). Percy has the following wizard spells prepared:

- Cantrips (at will): fire bolt, mage hand, prestidigitation
- 1st-level (4 slots): detect magic, shield, sleep
- 2nd-level (3 slots): blur, mirror image, misty step
- 3rd-level (3 slots): counterspell, fireball, lightning bolt
- 4th-level (3 slots): greater invisibility, polymorph
- 5th-level (3 slots): cone of cold, hold monster
- 6th-level (2 slots): chain lightning, disintegrate
- 7th-level (2 slots): finger of death, plane shift
- 8th-level (1 slot): antimagic field, maze
- 9th-level (1 slot): meteor swarm

---

**Actions**

**Multiattack.** Percy attacks twice with his firearm or makes one attack with his sword and one attack with his firearm.

**Bad News (Firearm).** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit:* 28 (4d10 + 6) piercing damage.

**Pepperbox (Firearm).** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +12 to hit, range 40/120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 21 (3d10 + 6) piercing damage.

**Retort (Longsword).** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 10 (1d8 + 6) slashing damage, plus 13 (3d8) radiant damage.

---

**Legendary Actions**

Percy can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Percy regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

- **Attack.** Percy makes one firearm or longsword attack.
- **Spellcast (Costs 2 Actions).** Percy casts a spell from his list of prepared spells, using a spell slot as normal.
- **Quick Reload (Costs 2 Actions).** Percy reloads his firearm.

---

**Description**

Percival de Rolo, also known as Percy, is a distinguished human gunslinger and wizard who played a pivotal role in the adventures of Vox Machina. After his heroic exploits alongside his companions, Percy has returned to Whitestone as a revered leader and advisor. Clad in adamantine half plate and wielding his signature firearms, Bad News and Pepperbox, Percy stands as a formidable guardian of his homeland.
