**Dorian, Guard Captain**

*Medium humanoid (human), lawful neutral*

---

**Armor Class** 18 (plate)

**Hit Points** 120 (16d8 + 48)

**Speed** 30 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) |

---

**Saving Throws** Str +7, Con +7

**Skills** Athletics +7, Perception +6, Intimidation +6

**Senses** passive Perception 16

**Languages** Common

**Challenge** 7 (2,900 XP)

---

**Actions**

**Multiattack.** Dorian makes two melee attacks.

**Longsword.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 3) slashing damage.

**Heavy Crossbow.** *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +5 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d10 + 2) piercing damage.

---

**Reactions**

**Parry.** Dorian adds 2 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, Dorian must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

Dorian cuts an imposing figure, standing tall with a stern countenance and a physique honed by years of disciplined training. His sharp gaze misses nothing, and his unwavering dedication to his duty as Guard Captain is evident in every aspect of his demeanor. Clad in the uniform of Whitestone's elite guard, Dorian carries himself with an air of authority that commands respect from his subordinates and instills apprehension in those who would dare to cross him.

*Dialog Examples:*

1. **Issuing Orders:** "Listen up, men. We've had reports of suspicious activity near the eastern perimeter. I want patrols doubled until further notice, and no one gets through without proper authorization. Understood?"

2. **Questioning a Stranger:** "State your business here in Whitestone, traveler. We don't take kindly to outsiders poking their noses where they don't belong."

3. **Expressing Concern:** "My lord, forgive my bluntness, but I have reservations about the recent influx of strangers into our city. It's my duty to ensure the safety of Whitestone and its citizens, and I won't hesitate to take whatever measures necessary to protect them."

4. **Assessing a Threat:** "I don't like the look of that one. Keep an eye on him, and if he so much as twitches the wrong way, I want him brought to me for questioning."

Dorian's unwavering loyalty to Lord Hargrave is matched only by his fierce dedication to the safety and security of Whitestone. With a keen eye for detail and a readiness to act decisively in the face of danger, he stands as a stalwart guardian against any who would threaten the peace of the city.
