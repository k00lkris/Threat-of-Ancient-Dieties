### Captain Soren

**Description:**
Captain Soren is a tall, imposing figure, standing at 6'2" with a broad-shouldered, muscular build that speaks of years of rigorous training. His neatly trimmed beard is a rich brown, matching his piercing blue eyes that seem to miss nothing. His face is stern, marked by a few small scars that hint at a history of battle. Soren's armor is polished to a shine, emblazoned with the crest of the de Rolo family, and he carries himself with the confidence and authority befitting his rank. Despite his stern appearance, there is an air of fairness and dedication to his duty that reassures those under his protection.

**Saving Throws** Strength +7, Constitution +6  
**Skills** Athletics +7, Insight +5, Intimidation +5, Perception +5  
**Senses** Passive Perception 15  
**Languages** Common, Elvish  
**Challenge** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Traits:**

**Brave.** Captain Soren has advantage on saving throws against being frightened.

**Leadership (Recharges after a Short or Long Rest).** For 1 minute, Captain Soren can utter a special command or warning whenever a non-hostile creature that he can see within 30 feet of him makes an attack roll or a saving throw. The creature can add a d4 to its roll provided it can hear and understand Soren. A creature can benefit from only one Leadership die at a time. This effect ends if Captain Soren is incapacitated.

**Actions:**

**Multiattack.** Captain Soren makes three melee attacks.

**Longsword.** Melee Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d8 + 4) slashing damage, or 10 (1d10 + 4) slashing damage if used with two hands.

**Javelin.** Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack: +7 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 30/120 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d6 + 4) piercing damage.

**Reactions:**

**Parry.** Captain Soren adds 3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. To do so, Soren must see the attacker and be wielding a melee weapon.

**Personality:**
Captain Soren is a no-nonsense leader who values duty and honor above all. He is deeply loyal to the de Rolo family and takes his responsibility to protect Whitestone and its people very seriously. He is fair and just, but can be unyielding when it comes to matters of security and justice. Despite his stern demeanor, he has a soft spot for the people he protects and will go to great lengths to ensure their safety and well-being.

**Background:**
Captain Soren has served in the Whitestone guard for over two decades. His rise through the ranks was swift due to his exceptional skills and unwavering dedication. He has fought in numerous battles and skirmishes, defending Whitestone from various threats. His experience and leadership have earned him the respect and loyalty of his men, and he is well-regarded by the citizens of Whitestone.
