**Seraphine, Head Maid**

Seraphine is a woman of graceful poise and unwavering dedication, with a demeanor that exudes authority and warmth in equal measure. Her years of service to Lord Hargrave have sculpted her into a figure of unwavering loyalty and astute discernment. With her hair neatly tied back in a bun, her attire immaculately pressed, and her demeanor always composed, Seraphine carries herself with an air of quiet confidence that commands respect from all who encounter her.

*Dialog Examples:*

1. **Greeting Lord Hargrave:** "My lord, welcome back to the manor. Your presence brings a sense of order and purpose to these halls."

2. **Reporting Household Matters:** "The preparations for the upcoming gala are proceeding smoothly, my lord. The invitations have been sent, and the staff is diligently attending to every detail."

3. **Conveying Concern:** "My lord, I must express my reservations regarding the recent business dealings with the Obsidian Veil. Their methods are... unorthodox, to say the least, and I fear they may bring unwanted attention to our endeavors."

4. **Issuing Instructions:** "Ensure that the guest chambers are prepared to the highest standard. We have esteemed visitors arriving from Emon, and I will accept nothing less than perfection."

Seraphine's steadfast presence and keen intuition make her an invaluable asset to Lord Hargrave's household, ensuring that the estate runs smoothly and that its inhabitants are well cared for, even as she navigates the murky waters of intrigue and ambition that swirl around her master's affairs.
