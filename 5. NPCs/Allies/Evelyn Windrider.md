**Medium humanoid (air genasi), neutral good**

**Armor Class:** 14 (natural armor)

**Hit Points:** 65 (10d8 + 20)

**Speed:** 30 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)

**STR:** 10 (+0)  
**DEX:** 16 (+3)  
**CON:** 14 (+2)  
**INT:** 12 (+1)  
**WIS:** 18 (+4)  
**CHA:** 16 (+3)  

**Saving Throws:** Dex +6, Wis +7

**Skills:** Medicine +7, Nature +4, Perception +7, Insight +7

**Damage Resistances:** Lightning, Thunder

**Senses:** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 17

**Languages:** Common, Primordial, Elvish

**Challenge:** 5 (1,800 XP)

**Innate Spellcasting:** Evelyn's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- At will: *levitate*, *gust*
- 3/day each: *feather fall*, *fog cloud*
- 1/day each: *control winds*

**Spellcasting:** Evelyn is a 10th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following druid spells prepared:

- **Cantrips (at will):** *druidcraft*, *guidance*, *mending*, *produce flame*
- **1st level (4 slots):** *cure wounds*, *entangle*, *faerie fire*, *goodberry*
- **2nd level (3 slots):** *lesser restoration*, *moonbeam*, *pass without trace*
- **3rd level (3 slots):** *call lightning*, *dispel magic*, *plant growth*
- **4th level (3 slots):** *freedom of movement*, *ice storm*, *stone shape*
- **5th level (2 slots):** *commune with nature*, *greater restoration*

**Elemental Affinity:** When Evelyn casts a spell that deals lightning or thunder damage, she can add her Charisma modifier (+3) to one lightning or thunder damage roll of that spell.

**Airy Step:** Evelyn can take the Disengage or Dash action as a bonus action on each of her turns.

**Actions:**

**Quarterstaff.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.

**Healing Touch (2/Day).** Evelyn touches another creature and restores 20 (4d8 + 2) hit points. Additionally, the creature is freed from any curse, disease, poison, blindness, or deafness.

**Wind Gust (Recharge 5–6).** Evelyn creates a line of strong wind 60 feet long and 10 feet wide blasting from her in a direction she chooses. Each creature in the line must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be pushed 15 feet away from her and knocked prone.

## Background
### Evelyn Windrider

**Description:**
Evelyn Windrider is a striking air genasi with an ethereal beauty that seems almost otherworldly. Her skin has a faint blue hue, like the sky at dawn, and her long, silvery hair flows like a gentle breeze even when there is no wind. Her eyes are a vibrant turquoise, shimmering with an inner light that reflects her connection to the elemental plane of air. Evelyn is tall and slender, moving with a grace that seems almost like floating. She often wears flowing, lightweight robes in shades of blue and white, adorned with intricate silver embroidery that resembles swirling clouds.

**Personality:**
Evelyn is calm and serene, exuding a sense of peace and tranquility wherever she goes. She speaks softly but with conviction, and her words often carry a deep wisdom. She is deeply connected to nature and the elements, particularly the air, and has a profound respect for all living things. Evelyn is a healer and a protector, always ready to help those in need. Despite her gentle demeanor, she possesses a steely resolve and will fiercely defend her friends and allies when necessary.

**Dialog Examples:**

1. **Greeting Adventurers:**
   - "Greetings, travelers. May the winds guide you to where you need to be. How can I assist you on this fine day?"
   - "Welcome, friends. You look weary from your travels. Please, rest and let me tend to your wounds."

2. **Talking About Nature:**
   - "The air is alive with whispers of the past and future. If you listen closely, you can hear the stories it tells."
   - "Nature is a delicate balance. We must tread lightly and respect the world around us if we wish to live in harmony."

3. **Offering Healing:**
   - "You have fought bravely, but your wounds need attention. Allow me to heal you, and you will soon be ready to face new challenges."
   - "The breath of life flows through all of us. Let me channel its power to mend your injuries."

4. **Discussing the Elemental Planes:**
   - "The elemental planes are wondrous places, each with its own unique beauty and dangers. The plane of air is my home, a realm of endless skies and floating islands."
   - "There is much we can learn from the elements. They are the building blocks of our world, and their power is both magnificent and terrifying."

5. **Advising on Matters of the Heart:**
   - "Follow your heart, but do not let it lead you astray. Balance your emotions with wisdom, and you will find the right path."
   - "Love is a powerful force, as unpredictable as the wind. Cherish it, but be prepared for the storms it may bring."

**Background:**
Evelyn Windrider was born in a remote village high in the Cliffkeep Mountains, where the air was thin and the winds were strong. From a young age, she exhibited a natural affinity for the air, often seen floating just above the ground or surrounded by a gentle breeze. Her parents, both skilled elementalists, recognized her potential and trained her in the ways of the air genasi.

As she grew older, Evelyn felt a calling to explore the world beyond her village. She traveled across Tal'Dorei, learning about different cultures and honing her abilities as a healer and protector. Along the way, she encountered numerous challenges and made many friends, always guided by her deep connection to the elemental plane of air.

Evelyn eventually settled in Whitestone, where she became a respected member of the community. She offers her services as a healer and advisor, using her knowledge of the elements to help those in need. Despite her serene and gentle nature, Evelyn is always ready to stand against those who threaten the balance of nature or the safety of her friends. Her wisdom and kindness have earned her the trust and admiration of many, making her a beloved figure in Whitestone and beyond.
