### Vincinte Laurent: The Broken Jester's Enigmatic Proprietor

**Backstory**

Vincinte Laurent, the owner of The Broken Jester in Kymal, is a man shrouded in mystery and contradiction. Much like the enigmatic Garak from *Star Trek: Deep Space Nine*, Vincinte's past is a tapestry woven with secrets, deception, and a complex web of allegiances. Born in the bustling city of Emon, Vincinte's early life was marked by privilege and education, though these privileges came at a cost.

#### Early Life

Vincinte Laurent was born into a prominent family in Emon, one with deep ties to the city's political and mercantile elite. His father, Dorian Laurent, was a respected diplomat, while his mother, Amara, was a powerful enchantress known for her skill in crafting magical items. From a young age, Vincinte was groomed for a life of influence and power, receiving extensive education in politics, commerce, and magic. However, this life of privilege also exposed him to the darker sides of society.

#### The Obsidian Veil

As he grew older, Vincinte was approached by a shadowy organization known as the Obsidian Veil, a secretive group operating within the shadows of Tal'Dorei's society. The Obsidian Veil specialized in intelligence gathering, espionage, and covert operations, often influencing the political landscape from behind the scenes. Seeing his potential, the Veil recruited Vincinte, offering him the chance to serve a greater purpose beyond the reach of conventional power.

For years, Vincinte operated as one of the Veil's most skilled agents, undertaking missions that required both his magical prowess and his keen intellect. He excelled in the art of subterfuge, gathering information, and eliminating threats to the Veil's interests. Despite the dangerous nature of his work, Vincinte found a sense of purpose and belonging within the Veil, something he had never experienced in his privileged upbringing.

#### The Turning Point

However, as time went on, Vincinte began to question the morality of his actions and the true intentions of the Obsidian Veil. The turning point came during a mission where he was ordered to assassinate a prominent political figure in Emon, a task that would lead to significant upheaval and innocent casualties. Struggling with his conscience, Vincinte ultimately chose to defy the Veil's orders, sparing the target's life and disappearing from the organization's radar.

#### A New Beginning in Kymal

Fleeing from his former life, Vincinte eventually found himself in the city of Kymal, a place known for its bustling trade and vibrant nightlife. Here, he established The Broken Jester, a shop that catered to adventurers and travelers seeking rare and valuable items. With his extensive knowledge of magic and enchantments, Vincinte quickly built a reputation as a skilled artisan capable of crafting and acquiring anything his clients might need.

#### The Broken Jester

The Broken Jester is a reflection of Vincinte's multifaceted personality. The shop is filled with an eclectic mix of items, from mundane supplies to rare magical artifacts. Those who frequent the shop often find Vincinte to be a charming and enigmatic figure, always ready with a witty remark or a cryptic piece of advice. Despite his outwardly affable demeanor, there are times when a glimpse of his darker, more calculating side can be seen, hinting at the complexities of his past.

#### Current Life

Vincinte maintains a network of contacts across Tal'Dorei, allowing him to procure rare items and information with remarkable efficiency. While he has distanced himself from the Obsidian Veil, he remains ever-vigilant, knowing that his past could catch up with him at any moment. He keeps his magical abilities and combat skills honed, ready to defend himself and his newfound life should the need arise.

#### Relationship with the Party

When the adventurers first meet Vincinte, they will find him to be an invaluable ally and resource. His ability to craft and enchant magical items will prove indispensable to their quest. However, as they grow closer, they may begin to unravel the layers of his past, discovering the complexities of the man behind the shop. Vincinte's loyalty to the party will be unwavering, though he may occasionally exhibit behavior that hints at the darker aspects of his history.

In Vincinte Laurent, the adventurers will find a character who embodies the duality of light and shadow, a man who has walked the fine line between hero and villain, and who now seeks redemption and purpose in a world filled with uncertainty.

## Description

Vincinte Laurent stands at an average height of about 5'10", with a lithe and agile build that speaks to both his nimbleness and his past as an operative of the Obsidian Veil. His skin is a warm olive tone, hinting at his Mediterranean heritage. His eyes are a striking shade of emerald green, sharp and observant, always seeming to take in more than they let on. They often glint with a mixture of intelligence and mischief, reflecting his cunning nature and quick wit.

His hair is jet black, kept neatly styled and swept back from his forehead, with just a hint of silver at the temples, giving him an air of distinguished maturity despite his relatively youthful appearance. A neatly trimmed goatee frames his mouth, adding to his roguish charm.

Vincinte's clothing is both practical and stylish, designed for both function and appearance. He typically wears a well-fitted dark leather vest over a fine silk shirt, the sleeves of which are often rolled up to his elbows. His trousers are similarly dark and practical, tucked into knee-high boots that are both sturdy and elegant. Over this, he wears a long, dark cloak that shimmers subtly with enchantments, giving him an almost ethereal presence.

Around his neck, Vincinte wears a silver amulet with an intricate design, a keepsake from his days with the Obsidian Veil. His fingers are adorned with a few carefully chosen rings, including his prized Ring of Mind Shielding, which he never removes.

Despite his often light-hearted and charming demeanor, there is an air of mystery and danger around Vincinte. His movements are fluid and graceful, betraying his extensive training and experience. When he speaks, his voice is smooth and melodic, capable of both soothing reassurances and razor-sharp wit.

In his shop, The Broken Jester, Vincinte's appearance is complemented by his surroundings. The shop is filled with an eclectic mix of goods, magical items, and curiosities, each reflecting his extensive travels and deep knowledge of the arcane. Whether he's negotiating a deal, crafting an enchantment, or simply enjoying a quiet moment with a cup of fine wine, Vincinte Laurent presents an image of a man who is always in control, always a step ahead, and always ready for whatever comes next.

**INT** 18 (+4)  
**WIS** 14 (+2)  
**CHA** 16 (+3)  

**Saving Throws** Dex +9, Int +9, Wis +7

**Skills** Arcana +9, Deception +8, Insight +7, Investigation +9, Perception +7, Sleight of Hand +9, Stealth +9

**Damage Resistances** Psychic, Necrotic

**Senses** Passive Perception 17

**Languages** Common, Elvish, Draconic, Infernal

**Challenge** 11 (7,200 XP)

**Background Feature: Obsidian Veil Operative**
Vincinte has advantage on Intelligence checks to recall information about covert organizations and their activities. He can also attempt to hide in plain sight when attempting to gather information or engage in espionage.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Vincinte makes two attacks with his Enchanted Dagger or casts two cantrips.

**Enchanted Dagger.** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (1d4 + 7) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) psychic damage.

### Spellcasting

Vincinte is a 15th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 17, +9 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following spells prepared:

**Cantrips (at will):** Mage Hand, Minor Illusion, Prestidigitation, Ray of Frost, Fire Bolt

**1st level (4 slots):** Detect Magic, Disguise Self, Mage Armor, Shield, Magic Missile

**2nd level (3 slots):** Invisibility, Misty Step, Mirror Image, Suggestion

**3rd level (3 slots):** Counterspell, Fly, Haste

**4th level (3 slots):** Greater Invisibility, Dimension Door, Phantasmal Killer

**5th level (2 slots):** Dominate Person, Hold Monster, Teleportation Circle

**6th level (1 slot):** Disintegrate, True Seeing

**7th level (1 slot):** Etherealness, Plane Shift

### Reactions

**Shield.** Vincinte can cast shield in response to being attacked, raising his AC by 5 until the start of his next turn.

**Counterspell.** Vincinte can cast counterspell when a creature he can see casts a spell, potentially negating the spell.

### Legendary Actions

Vincinte can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Vincinte regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

**Cantrip.** Vincinte casts a cantrip.

**Move.** Vincinte moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

**Cast a Spell (Costs 2 Actions).** Vincinte casts one of his spells of 2nd level or lower.

### Equipment

- **Enchanted Dagger:** A finely crafted dagger with an intricate hilt design resembling intertwining serpents with ruby eyes.
- **Ring of Mind Shielding:** While wearing this ring, Vincinte is immune to magic that allows other creatures to read his thoughts, determine whether he is lying, know his alignment, or know his creature type. Creatures can telepathically communicate with him only if he allows it.
- **Cloak of Displacement:** While wearing this cloak, it projects an illusion that makes him appear to be standing in a place near his actual location, causing attack rolls against him to have disadvantage. If he takes damage, the property ceases to function until the start of his next turn.
