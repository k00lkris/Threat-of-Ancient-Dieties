### **Uncorrupted Waterwatch Guard: Captain Renaud Varn**

**Race:** Human  
**Gender:** Male  
**Age:** 42  
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral  
**Class/Role:** Waterwatch Captain  
**Personality Traits:** Disciplined, Stoic, Frustrated, Idealistic but Pragmatic  
**Appearance:** Broad-shouldered, with dark brown hair just starting to grey at the temples. A short, well-trimmed beard and piercing blue eyes that always seem tired. He wears a reinforced Waterwatch captain’s uniform, though his is less adorned than those of his corrupt peers.

---

### **Backstory & Motivations**

Captain **Renaud Varn** has served the **Waterwatch** for nearly two decades. He **joined as an idealist**, believing he could help protect Stilben from the lawlessness that ran rampant in Outer Town. However, years of **watching his superiors take bribes, frame innocents, and let criminals walk free** have worn him down. He refuses to take bribes himself, which has left him increasingly isolated within the force. Many of his fellow officers **mock him behind his back**, calling him _"The Last Honest Guard."_

Despite this, Varn still **tries to do his job**, but he has little authority. His superiors regularly **overrule his decisions**, release arrested criminals for a pouch of gold, and refuse to support his investigations. He has **thought about quitting**, but he knows that if he leaves, there will be no one left to **stand up for the innocent** in Outer Town. Instead, he has **turned to outsiders**, seeking **mercenaries and adventurers** who can do what he cannot.

---

### **How He Can Help the Party**

- **Insider Information:** Varn knows where the worst corruption lies and can **point the party toward criminal activity** the Waterwatch ignores.
- **Access to Restricted Areas:** Though he lacks full authority, he can get the players into places **regular citizens cannot go**, such as the **Waterwatch Stockade** or certain sections of **Inner Town**.
- **Moral Dilemma:** He **wants to take down the corruption** but knows doing so could get him killed. He will only **fully commit** if he believes the party is capable of making a real difference.
- **Protection from the Law:** If the party runs afoul of the **Waterwatch**, Varn can provide **temporary cover**—but he cannot protect them forever.

---

### **Role in a Side Quest**

**Potential Side Quest:** _Cleaning Up the Watch_

- Varn has gathered evidence of a **major Waterwatch officer taking bribes from the Clasp** and the **Myriad**, but his authority isn’t enough to make it stick. He needs the party to **find hard proof** and possibly **take out a key figure** responsible for the corruption.
- He **fears for his life**, as he's been warned to back off multiple times. If the party takes the job, he will become a **recurring ally**, but if they fail or refuse, he may **disappear—possibly murdered or forced into exile**.

---

### **Personality & Dialogue Hooks**

- _"I joined the Watch to make a difference. I thought I could clean up this city, protect the people. Turns out, I was the only one who gave a damn."_
- _"I could look the other way. Everyone else does. But then... what am I, if I do?"_
- _"The moment you try to be better than the filth in this city, you paint a target on your back."_
- _"I won’t lie to you—helping me won’t be easy. But if we pull this off? We can make Stilben just a little less rotten."_

---

### **Possible Fates Depending on the Party’s Actions**

- **If the Party Succeeds:** Varn becomes **Captain of the Waterwatch** and starts making real reforms, remaining a key ally.
- **If the Party Fails:** He is either **killed by the corrupt Watch officers** or **forced into hiding**, leaving Stilben to fall further into crime and corruption.
- **If the Party Ignores Him:** He tries to fight alone but is **silenced permanently** within weeks. The corruption wins.

---

### **Why the Party Might Care**

- **A Rare Good Man in a Crooked City:** The players may be drawn to helping him simply because he is **one of the few truly honorable people** in Stilben.
- **Valuable Connections:** He can provide **access to Inner Town**, **reduce trouble with the law**, and **offer resources** if they’re on his good side.
- **Moral Dilemma:** Ignoring him means letting Stilben **descend even further into corruption**, and his death could haunt them.

---

### **Final Notes**

Captain Renaud Varn is designed to be a **tragic but inspiring figure**—a man who has **stood against corruption alone for years** and now needs **help before he’s swallowed by it**. His fate is entirely up to the players, making him an excellent **recurring NPC with major consequences** for Stilben’s future.
