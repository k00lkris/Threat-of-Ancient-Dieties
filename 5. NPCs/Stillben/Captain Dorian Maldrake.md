## Profile

Captain **Dorian Maldrake** commands the *Azure Gale*, the vessel the Reapers have booked to reach the **Shattered Isles** in search of the [[Heart of a Titan Summary|Heart of a Titan]]. His existence, captaincy, and the booking are established in Session 14; the characterization below is **new, proposed preparation**, not previously played history.

**Appearance (suggested):** Weathered face, salt-streaked coat, rolled-up sea charts and a brass speaking trumpet. His eyes rarely stay on one person for long: he is continually checking his ship.

**Personality:** Practical, alert, measured. He respects competent travelers, dislikes concealed risks to his crew, and refuses unnecessary heroics. He is neither a Myriad pawn nor automatically immune to criminal pressure.

**Voice:** Low and economical, with the patience of someone accustomed to explaining wind and tide to people who cannot control either.

**Motivations:** Deliver passengers and cargo, protect his crew, keep the *Azure Gale* seaworthy, and return from the Shattered Isles with his vessel intact.

## What He Knows

- The party has arranged passage aboard his ship.
- Stillben's waterfront is dangerous; the Clasp and Myriad exert influence.
- The Shattered Isles are hazardous waters, and his crew's survival takes precedence over an adventurer's curiosity.
- **He does not know** what actually happened to Hurk and Heijla unless the party tells him or discovers reliable information.

## Dialogue

> "You're welcome aboard, so long as your trouble stays on the quay."

> "I can wait for a tide. I cannot make the tide wait for you."

> "Those islands don't care what treasure you're after. My crew will care whether we come home."

## Session 15: Decisions and Consequences

| Party action | Maldrake's response |
|---|---|
| Arrives on time | Honors the agreed passage and begins departure preparations. |
| Reports missing companions | Listens; will not turn the ship into a rescue expedition without agreement. |
| Asks for extra time | Explains his obligations and offers a concrete cutoff, negotiated in play. |
| Brings Myriad pursuers to the ship | Demands an explanation and takes precautions to protect his crew. |
| Asks crew to investigate | May recommend dock contacts but doesn't conscript sailors into a gang conflict. |
| Threatens his crew | Can refuse passage or request assistance; he is not obliged to fight the PCs. |
| Misses departure | Ship may sail, provided the departure deadline was communicated and elapsed. |

> [!important] Timing
> No exact boarding deadline or fare is established in existing notes. Set these at the table when relevant, rather than claiming a previous agreement gave a specific clock time.

## Running the Captain

Maldrake is a **decision-making NPC**, not a quest marker. Let the players make their case. He may be persuaded to help, but his ship and crew remain his responsibility. He has no secret prewritten answer to the Myriad mystery.

> [!info] Combat use
> A dedicated combat statblock is intentionally deferred. This scene does not presume a battle with the captain. If combat becomes relevant, create and test one rather than silently substituting a powerful published monster.
