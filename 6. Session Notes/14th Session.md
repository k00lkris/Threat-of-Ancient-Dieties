### **Session Recap – The Streets of Shadows and Sails of Fate**

Darkness lingered in the crypts beneath Whitestone as the party stood over the lifeless body of **Vornelith Blackthorn**, his treacherous deeds silenced in a pool of crimson. Sifting through his robes, they uncovered his **spellbook**, a **cursed amulet**, and the lingering stench of necromantic ambition. But there was no time for reflection—the night’s horror had ended, and the dawn of their next journey awaited.

As they ascended from the depths of the Greyfields, **Captain Soren** awaited them, listening intently as they recounted their grim discovery. He thanked them, his relief palpable, and reminded them of his promise: magical passage to **Stillben**.

Through the streets of **Whitestone**, they marched—a stark contrast from the gloom of the crypts. **Chupacabra warning posters** littered the city, their fabricated menace now a full-blown citywide panic. **Letrexie**, ever the collector of oddities, swiped as many as she could, stuffing them away as trophies of her deception.

Back in **Castle Whitestone**, their words carried weight. **Percival** and **Vex’ahlia** listened closely as the puzzle pieces fell into place: **Vornelith had been working with Hargraves.** The urgency of their mission had never been clearer. Without delay, they were taken to **the Sun Tree**, where a figure awaited them—**Keyleth, the Voice of the Tempest**.

**Celestriel**, using her own magic, joined Keyleth in her conversation with the great tree. The words between them were sacred, known only to those attuned to the whispers of the natural world. Keyleth’s sorrow was evident—**she had sent Celestriel on her Aramente, and now, her chosen successor did not remember her.** But there was no time for grief. With a final blessing, Keyleth bid them farewell, and the party stepped through the Sun Tree’s embrace, vanishing into the unknown.

### **Welcome to Stillben**

The stench of **swamp rot, brine, and industry** filled their lungs as they **emerged from a tree** in **Lower Town, Stillben**—a city as foul as it was sprawling. Before they could even adjust to the grimy streets, chaos found them once more.

A **turf war** erupted around them, blood staining the docks as **the Clasp and the Myriad clashed in the open streets.** The party, **choosing discretion over valor**, stayed out of the fight, but the **Waterwatch guards** were quick to assume guilt by association. **Only their quick tongues and sharper wits** kept them out of the stockades.

But **Stillben was not done testing them.**

The **bridge into Inner Town** was guarded, and the price of entry was more than coin—**"Papers, please."** But no such documents were in their possession. The only way forward? **A bribe.** A well-placed **50 gold** later, and a fresh set of **counterfeit credentials** paved their way into the heart of the city.

On the **docks**, their next trial began: **finding a ship willing to take them to the Shattered Isles.** **Hurk, ever the maritime enthusiast,** regaled them with **all one could ever want to know about ships, sails, and piracy.** After careful deliberation, they placed their trust in the **Azure Gale, captained by Dorian Maldrake**—an expensive, but battle-tested choice.

With their voyage set for the next day, they sought **respite in one of Stillben’s finer inns.**

### **Deals in the Dark**

**Notos, Letrexie, and Celestriel** took to the **streets of Inner Town** in search of **alchemical ingredients.** But with Stillben’s rampant corruption, they feared **harassment from the guards** at every turn. To test the strength of their **forged papers**, Notos—**in disguise**—presented them at a checkpoint.

The guards bought the lie. But someone was watching.

**Captain Renaud Varn—the last uncorrupted Waterwatch officer.**

He confronted them, demanding to see the papers. **Notos hesitated. Then, reluctantly, he handed them over.**

**"Where did you get these?"**

Silence. Then, truth.

**The bribe. The bridge guards. The corruption.**

Varn **was furious**—not at them, but at the rot within his own ranks. He made them an offer: **real papers, in exchange for bringing down the corrupt guards**.

They agreed. But **on one condition:** **wait until tomorrow, after they were gone.**

Satisfied, **Varn handed them genuine travel papers,** and in that moment, Notos—**a master of deception**—saw it. **The forgeries they had bought were crude imitations.**

Their freedom had been built on a lie.

That night, **they left the city walls and gathered ingredients in the swamps**, taking one last breath of **Stillben’s fetid wilderness** before their voyage.

But **Stillben wasn’t done with them yet.**

As they settled into their suite, **a visitor arrived.**

A **Myriad "salesman"**, all honeyed words and **thinly veiled threats.**

They had been **observed**, the cartel **impressed** by how they handled themselves—**outsiders who had already maneuvered Stillben’s streets like veterans.** The Myriad wanted them to **prove their worth.**

**The job?** **Assassinate the Clasp’s captain.**

The party was given **one night** to decide. If they accepted, they were to visit the concierge and ask for **"tickets to the opera."**

When the Myriad enforcer left, **the debate began.**

**Hours of discussion.**

They wanted **nothing to do** with this war. But **what would refusal mean? Retaliation? A bounty on their heads?**

In the end, they chose deception.

They would leave at **staggered times**, **disguised**, masking their retreat. **No trace, no witnesses.**

And on the suite’s desk, **they left only a note.**

**"Not interested."**

### **The Night Before the Storm**

As the first light of dawn crept through Stillben’s smog-choked streets, the city’s underbelly stirred. Somewhere, in the shadows, **both the Myriad and the Clasp were watching.**

Tomorrow, **they would set sail.**

If they could make it to the docks.
