### Session Recap:

**Last session began with you all still inside Lord Hargraves' manor, trapped in his secret study. With a narrow staircase and a bottlenecked hallway leading out, Hargraves coaxed you down into the library, playing his hand as a manipulative charmer. He spoke of his grand plans to resurrect the Annunaki, offering grand visions of a world governed by divine order. As he laid out his reasoning, he wove a false sense of security around you, gaining just enough trust to set his plan into motion.**

**But when it became clear that you wouldn't fully commit to his cause, he revealed his deception. With cold, calculating words, he turned to his guards and said, ‘Kill my friends,’ giving the order to strike you down.**

**What followed was a fierce, high-stakes battle. Hargraves' guards were relentless, but together you pushed through, your tactics proving superior. In a surprising turn of events, Celestriel offered food to one of Hargraves' guard dogs mid-battle, successfully turning it against its master. The dog, now affectionately named Manuki, has joined your party as a loyal companion.**

**Once the dust had settled, you thoroughly searched Hargraves’ manor, uncovering valuable coins, magical items, and crucial evidence in the form of letters and his personal journal. Armed with this information, you immediately left the mansion and headed straight to Castle Whitestone, where you woke up Percival and Vex’ahlia de Rolo in the middle of the night.**

**Knowing that Hargraves had been using magical means to eavesdrop within the castle, you wisely chose to meet with them in the corrupted ziggurat beneath the castle, a place shielded from unwanted listening ears. There, you recounted everything: Hargraves' plan to resurrect the Annunaki, his treachery, and the dangerous artifacts he seeks. With the evidence laid bare, Percy and Vex’ahlia could no longer deny the threat Hargraves posed. Percy immediately dispatched guards to find and arrest him, but Hargraves was long gone.**

**Percy then shared valuable information with you: the Titan's Heart, one of the key artifacts needed for the resurrection ritual, could likely be found in the Shattered Isles, a distant and dangerous location. To get there, you’ll need to travel to a port city and charter a boat.**

**You spent the night in the safety of Whitestone Castle, but the next morning, more curious rumors reached your ears. You overheard Paleguard discussing fresh blood mysteriously splashed across gravestones in the Greyfield for three nights in a row, with no apparent cause. In a moment of inspiration—or mischief—you convinced the guards that the culprit was a fearsome creature from the Plane of Mex: the Chupacabra. So convincing was your lie that the guards have begun spreading warnings throughout Whitestone, putting up signs about the 'Chupacabra' to alert the town.**

**Now, with your things gathered, you are ready to head out. Your journey will take you back through Turst Fields and onwards to the Shattered Isles. But what new dangers will cross your path, and what fresh mysteries will you uncover?**
