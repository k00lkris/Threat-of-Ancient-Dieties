This session turned out to be mostly a shopping session.

## Turst Fields
In Turst Fields, the players encountered the mother who's daughter wandered off into the woods. They agreed to go look for the kid, but their previous obligations to escort the caravan hindered the amount of time they were allowed to search.

After about 3 hours of searching, they found that the girl had misled the wolves that were perusing her and ran off into the opposite direction. They marked the last position they were able to search and asked the city guard to pick up where they were unable to continue.

## Travel to Whitestone
Resulting from a Nat 1 on the encounter roll, the players did not encounter the bandits waiting on the road.

## Whitestone
When the players arrived in Whitestone, they hit the market.

Hurk hit [[The Seafarer's Rest]] right out of the gate. There, he talked with Miranda about his loss of memory, what time he could remember with The Salty Dogs and asked for guidance. Miranda told him that the last place The Salty Dogs were seen were near an island cluster south of Marquet and never heard from again.

While Hurk was talking to Miranda, the rest of the players went to [[The Emerald Apothecary]] to pick up healing potions. While the healing potions were too expensive, they picked up Poultice of Restoration for the entire party and paid for training in crafting their own potions.

The party rejoined and went to [[The Gemstone Grotto]] and tried to haggle their 500 gp of gems. Hurk told an elaborate tale to the owner, who did not believe him. They haggled him to offering 400 gp for the gems, which they refused.

They went to [[The Lucky Coin]] to try and sell Tasha's Mirror. After failed haggling attempts, Felix decided to look into the mirror, where he was stricken with temporary insanity where he could only speak in babbel until [[Letrixe Embel]] hit him with a Lesser Restoration spell. Felix told them to get the mirror out of his shop.

Finally, the party went to [[The Velvet Rose]]. They first tried to sell the amulet they picked up off the bugbear they encountered in the mines from the first session. Cassandra Velvet recognized the amulet, being a secret cultist herself, and told her that she wouldn't deal with the artifact, but referred her to [[Lord Bartholomew Hargraves]] to sell it.

Celestiel then tried to haggle the gems they failed to sell at [[The Gemstone Grotto]]. Though negotiations were successful, with Cassandra offering 750 gp for the gems, Hurk threw in an amethyst shard he had picked up while searching for the Celestial Amethyst Dragon. Cassandra saw the significance of the crystal and agreed to pay them 825 gp for the lot of them.

The players then took rest at [[The Silver Chalice]] where they rented the luxury suite. They did not partake in the mini-bar offered. There were no encounters that evening.

The next day, after breakfast, Celestriel went to speak with [[The Sun Tree]]. The tree told her that the de Rolos were good people and that it was suspicious of Hargraves, having seen him engage in secret meetings with hooded figures.

The party then went to the Hargraves Manor to either try and sell the amulet or confront Hargraves directly. As Hargraves was not present at the manor, they were turned away by the guards at the gate. Prior to being turned away, however, Celestriel turned into a raven and scouted the property seeing that it was heavily guarded by guards and dogs.

The party then went to Whitestone Castle where they met [[Evelyn Windrider]]. She recognized Heijla and told her a little bit about the past she had no memory of. She agreed to tell Heijla more about her past later in the evening, as she had duties to attend to. Her recognition of Heijla did provide them an audience with Percy and Vex'halia.

After recanting their journey so far to Percy and Vex'halia, they understood the gravity of their quest. Though skeptical about the involvement of [[Lord Bartholomew Hargraves]], they requested additional evidence of his involvement in return for their aid in the adventure.

The players mentioned requiring the hart of a titan to Percy which sparked his attention. Hargraves, through his covert listening devices, also learned about the heart of a titan.

## Behind the Scenes.
Cassandra Velvet is sympathetic to [[Lord Bartholomew Hargraves]] mission to resurrect the Annunaki. She is a secret cultist and is in possession of a similar amulet herself. After encountering the players, she rushed off to Hargraves to inform him about the players and their possession of the amulet.

This information startled Hargraves and expedited his plans. Fearing that the players would expose him to the de Rolos, he planted magical listening devices in [[Whitestone Castle]] that he could use to eavesdrop on any conversations the party might have. He then met with some of his cultists present in Whitestone to begin packing up their operations and finding a more secret location to continue their efforts.

### Consequences
- Cassandra Velvet will begin packing up her shop and evacuating Whitestone.
- [[Lord Bartholomew Hargraves]] is aware of the players presence and will begin to plan his own escape.
- If the players wait too long to search for clues at Hargraves manor, they will be attacked by cultist assassins.
- Guards at Hargraves estate will be more hostile to the players and, if discovered trespassing, will attack on sight.
- Percival will begin researching titans and possible locations to locate one.
