### **The Final Omen – A Raven Queen Event**

If the party lingers too long in the crypt or interacts with the **Raven Queen statue**, a supernatural event occurs:

**Read-Aloud Text:** 
> _The candles flicker as an unnatural chill sweeps through the chamber. Shadows coil in the corners, stretching unnaturally toward the center of the room. A distant, feminine whisper—both melodic and chilling—fills the air:_
>
_"The path you walk is one of consequence. The threads of fate entwine with the chains of deception. Choose your steps carefully, lest the hand of the Unseen One guides you blindly into the abyss."_
>
_A single raven, impossibly black, appears on the Raven Queen’s statue, watching. It caws once before dissolving into a swirl of feathers, fading from sight._

🛠 **Possible Mechanical Effects:**

- If the players **swear an oath** to uphold balance in the name of the Raven Queen, **they gain inspiration**.
- If they **desecrate the tomb** or ignore the warning, **they gain a level of exhaustion**.
- If they **investigate further**, they may uncover **more about Elara Windsail** and her cryptic connection to fate.
